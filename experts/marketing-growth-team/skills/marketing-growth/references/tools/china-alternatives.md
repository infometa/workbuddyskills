This reference maps international marketing tools to their China-market equivalents, covering analytics, advertising, email, CRM, payments, and more.

## SEO

| International | China Alternative | Notes |
|--------------|-------------------|-------|
| Google Search Console | 百度搜索资源平台, 360站长平台 | Webmaster tools |
| Ahrefs / Semrush | 5118, 爱站网, 站长工具 | SEO analysis tools |
| Screaming Frog | Xenu, 自建爬虫 | Site crawling |

**Baidu SEO differences from Google:**
- Baidu prefers Chinese-hosted content (.cn domains, ICP备案)
- JavaScript rendering is limited — server-side rendering critical
- Baidu Spider has different crawl behavior (slower, less frequent)
- Meta keywords still have some weight in Baidu
- Baidu Webmaster verification required for indexing
- Mobile-first approach (百度移动搜索)

## WeChat Ecosystem (Critical for China)

WeChat is not just a messaging app — it's an entire marketing ecosystem:

| Function | WeChat Feature | Use Case |
|----------|---------------|----------|
| Content Marketing | 公众号 (Official Account) | Long-form content, newsletters |
| E-commerce | 小程序 (Mini Program) | In-app stores, services |
| Short Video | 视频号 (Channels) | Short-form video content |
| Social Ads | 朋友圈广告 | Native social advertising |
| CRM | 企业微信 (WeCom) | Customer relationship management |
| Payments | 微信支付 | Transactions and subscriptions |
| Community | 微信群 | Group marketing, community |

## Key Differences in China Marketing

1. **Mobile-first** — Desktop is secondary; design for mobile WeChat browser
2. **WeChat-centric** — WeChat replaces email, social media, payments, and even apps
3. **KOL/KOC economy** — Influencer marketing is mainstream, not alternative
4. **Mini Programs** — Replace many standalone apps and landing pages
5. **QR codes everywhere** — Primary bridge between offline and online
6. **ICP requirement** — Websites must have ICP备案 to be accessible in China
7. **Content regulation** — Advertising and content subject to stricter review
8. **Private traffic (私域流量)** — Building owned audiences is the primary strategy
