# Malaysia Statutory Filing Calendar for HR & Admin
## Source: LHDN (Inland Revenue Board), SSM (Companies Commission), KWSP (EPF), PERKESO (SOCSO), Royal Malaysian Customs (SST), Companies Act 2016
## Validity: 2026

---

## MONTHLY DEADLINES (雇主月度合规)

| Deadline | What | To Whom | How | Late Penalty |
|----------|------|---------|-----|-------------|
| **15th** | EPF (KWSP) contributions | KWSP | e-Caruman / i-Akaun employer portal | Late payment interest: dividend rate + 1%, min RM10 fine |
| **15th** | SOCSO (PERKESO) contributions | PERKESO | ASSIST portal | Late payment interest: 6% per annum |
| **15th** | EIS (SIP) contributions | PERKESO | ASSIST portal (combined with SOCSO) | Same as SOCSO |
| **15th** | PCB (MTD) income tax remittance | LHDN | e-PCB / e-Data PCB / MYTax | 10% penalty on late remittance |
| **15th** | HRDF levy (if applicable) | HRDF | e-TRANS | 10% late penalty on levy amount + interest |
| **Last day of following month** | SST-02 return + payment | Customs (RMCD) | MySST portal | 10-40% penalty on unpaid SST |

### EPF Contribution Rates (2026)
| Employee Wages | Employer (%) | Employee (%) | Total (%) |
|---------------|-------------|-------------|----------|
| ≤ RM5,000/month | 13% | 11% | 24% |
| > RM5,000/month | 12% | 11% | 23% |
| Foreign workers | 2% | 2% | 4% |
| Employees ≥ 60 years | 4% | 0% | 4% |

### SOCSO Contribution (2026)
- Employer: 1.75% of wages (capped at insured wage ceiling RM5,000)
- Employee: 0.5% of wages (capped)
- EIS: Employer 0.2%, Employee 0.2% (capped at RM5,000 ceiling)

### PCB (MTD) Calculation
Based on LHDN PCB Schedule:
- Determined by monthly taxable income (after EPF deduction, max RM4,000 relief)
- PCB Tables published by LHDN annually
- Computerised calculation method (Rule 3, Income Tax (Deduction from Remuneration) Rules 1994)

### HRDF Levy (PSMB Act 2001)
- Manufacturing: 1.5% of employee wages
- Services: 1.0% of employee wages
- Exemption: Employers with <10 Malaysian employees
- Registration mandatory for employers with ≥10 Malaysian employees
- Coverage recently expanded to include more sectors under Act 612 amendment

## ANNUAL DEADLINES

### LHDN (Inland Revenue Board) — Employer Filing

| Deadline | What | Details |
|----------|------|---------|
| **31 March** | Form E (Employer's Return) | Summary of all employee earnings + PCB remitted in previous year; filed via e-E / MYTax; penalty: RM200-20,000 for late/non-filing |
| **Within 7 months of FYE** | Form C (Company Tax Return) | Companies with FYE 31 Dec → due 31 July; e-Filing mandatory; estimated tax (CP204) must be paid in 12 monthly instalments from 2nd month of basis period |
| **30 days before new FY** | CP204 (Estimate of Tax Payable) | Must submit estimate ≥85% of actual; <85% triggers 10% penalty on shortfall |

### SSM (Companies Commission)

| Deadline | What | Details |
|----------|------|---------|
| **Within 6 months of FYE** | Annual General Meeting (AGM) | Private companies: AGM required within 6 months of FYE; can pass written resolutions in lieu (S297) |
| **Within 30 days of AGM** | Annual Return (AR) | Filed via MBRS (Malaysian Business Reporting System); must include audited financial statements (for non-exempt private companies); late filing: RM200/day up to max RM10,000 |
| **Within 30 days of AGM** | Audited Financial Statements | Filed together with AR via MBRS; exemption: dormant companies, exempt private companies meeting thresholds |
| **14 days of change** | Change of Registered Office / Particulars | S47 notification for change of address, S58 for change of directors/shareholders |

### SST (Sales & Service Tax) — for registered businesses

| Deadline | What |
|----------|------|
| **Bimonthly** | SST-02 return (sales threshold ≥RM500,000 annual for manufacturers; specific service thresholds apply) |
| **31 March (annual)** | Reconciliation return if annual declaration scheme chosen |

### Exempt Private Company Thresholds
A private company is "exempt" if:
- ≤20 members AND no corporate shareholder, OR
- Minister declares it exempt
- Exempt companies may prepare financial statements without full compliance with approved accounting standards (but still need proper accounts)

## QUARTERLY DEADLINES

| Deadline | What | To Whom |
|----------|------|---------|
| **Last day of month following quarter** | SST-02 return (if quarterly filer) | Customs |
| **14 days after quarter end** | Quarterly Board Meeting (best practice, not statutory) | Internal |

## COMPLIANCE CALENDAR SUMMARY (for FYE 31 December company)

| Month | Action |
|-------|--------|
| **Jan** | Renew business premise license (if not done in Dec) |
| **Monthly by 15th** | EPF + SOCSO + EIS + PCB + HRDF |
| **By 31 Jan** | Issue EA Forms to all employees (previous year earnings summary) |
| **By 28/29 Feb** | Issue CP58 to agents/commission recipients |
| **By 31 Mar** | File Form E (employer return) |
| **By 30 June** | AGM (within 6 months of FYE 31 Dec) |
| **By 31 Jul** | Annual Return + Audited FS via MBRS (within 30 days of AGM) |
| **By 31 Jul** | Form C (company tax return) — 7 months from FYE 31 Dec |
| **Monthly** | CP204 tax instalment payment (from month 2 of basis year) |
| **Nov-Dec** | Renew premise + signboard licenses for next year |
| **Any change** | Update SSM within 14 days |

## EMPLOYEE DOCUMENTATION DEADLINES

| Deadline | Document | Recipient |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| **Within 14 days of hiring** | Written employment contract (EA 1955 S10 for specified periods >1 month) | Employee |
| **By 28 Feb** | EA Form (Statement of Remuneration for previous year) | Employee |
| **7 days before termination** | Notice of termination (or pay in lieu) | Employee |
| **On termination** | Termination letter + final pay (including pro-rated leave, notice pay) | Employee |
| **Within 30 days of change** | Update employee records (salary, position) | Internal + payroll |
