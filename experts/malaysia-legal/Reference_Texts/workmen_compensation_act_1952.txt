================================================================================
MALAYSIA WORKMEN'S COMPENSATION ACT 1952 (Act 273)
Comprehensive Summary for HR & Employer Compliance
================================================================================
Source: JTKSM, AGC, Tokio Marine, Seekers
Note: Primarily applicable to NON-SOCSO-covered workers
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Workmen's Compensation Act 1952 (WCA) provides compensation for workers who
suffer workplace injuries or occupational diseases. It is an ALTERNATIVE or
SUPPLEMENTARY system to SOCSO (Employees' Social Security Act 1969).

2. WHO IS COVERED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WCA applies to workers NOT covered by SOCSO, including:
- Foreign domestic workers (maids) — NOT covered by SOCSO
- Workers in non-SOCSO-covered sectors
- Casual/temporary workers not registered with SOCSO
- Self-employed workers in certain circumstances

For SOCSO-covered workers: SOCSO is primary; WCA may supplement for additional
remedies or for workers whose SOCSO claim was rejected on technical grounds.

3. COMPENSATION BENEFITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Death (100% disablement = death)
- Lump sum payment based on age and monthly wages
- Maximum: 48 months' wages or RM 36,000, whichever is LESS
- Funeral expenses: RM 2,000

3.2 Permanent Total Disablement
- Worker unable to perform ANY work
- Lump sum: 48 months' wages or RM 36,000

3.3 Permanent Partial Disablement
- Percentage of total disablement compensation based on scheduled injuries
- Examples:
  - Loss of arm above elbow: 70% of total benefit
  - Loss of leg: 70%
  - Loss of eye: 30%
  - Loss of finger: 14%

3.4 Temporary Disablement (up to 5 years)
- Half-monthly payments
- Rate: 1/3 of monthly wages or RM 115/month, whichever is LESS

3.5 Medical Expenses
- Employer liable for reasonable medical expenses
- Up to RM 1,500 or as determined by Labour Department

4. EMPLOYER LIABILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Employer is liable to pay compensation if:
a) Injury caused by accident arising OUT OF and IN THE COURSE of employment
b) Occupational disease contracted during employment
c) Death resulting from such injury or disease

Defences available to employer:
- Worker's WILLFUL MISCONDUCT (e.g., intoxication)
- Injury caused by worker's DELIBERATE DISOBEDIENCE of safety rules
- Injury NOT resulting in disablement exceeding 3 DAYS

5. COMPARISON: WCA vs SOCSO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Aspect              WCA 1952                    SOCSO (Act 4)
-----------         -----------                 --------------
Coverage            Non-SOCSO workers           All employees ≤ RM 6,000
Payer               Employer DIRECTLY           PERKESO (social insurance)
Max compensation    RM 36,000 (lump sum)        Lifetime pension (IPS)
Medical             Up to RM 1,500              Unlimited (panel clinics)
Rehabilitation      Limited                     Comprehensive (SOCSO rehab)

6. WORKMEN'S COMPENSATION INSURANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All employers MUST purchase Workmen's Compensation Insurance for employees
NOT covered by SOCSO (most commonly: foreign domestic workers).

Insurance covers:
- Statutory liability under WCA 1952
- Legal costs of defending claims
- Lump sum payments as scheduled

7. CLAIMS PROCEDURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Employer MUST report accident within 10 DAYS to Labour Department
Step 2: Worker undergoes medical examination
Step 3: Labour Department assesses disablement percentage
Step 4: Labour Department determines compensation amount
Step 5: Employer/Insurance pays compensation

8. PENALTIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Failure to report accident: fine
- Failure to insure foreign workers: fine
- Failure to pay compensation: Labour Department may order payment;
  non-compliance leads to prosecution
