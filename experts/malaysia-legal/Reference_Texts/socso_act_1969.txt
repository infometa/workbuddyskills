1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SOCSO (Pertubuhan Keselamatan Sosial / PERKESO) administers social security
protection for employees under the Employees' Social Security Act 1969.
It provides financial and medical coverage for work-related injuries,
occupational diseases, and permanent invalidity.

2. TWO MAIN SCHEMES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Employment Injury Scheme (EISocso)
- Covers work-related accidents and occupational diseases
- Includes commuting accidents
- Covers accidents during official work travel and company events

2.2 Invalidity Pension Scheme (IPS)
- Covers permanent disability (NOT work-related)
- Provides lifetime monthly pension if unable to work

3. WHO MUST CONTRIBUTE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mandatory for:
- All Malaysian employees earning RM 4,000 or less per month (when first employed)
- Employees who were already contributing before salary exceeded RM 4,000
- All private sector employees
- Foreign workers: Employment Injury Scheme ONLY (since June 2019)

ONCE REGISTERED, ALWAYS REGISTERED: If you start contributing when salary is
RM 4,000 or below, you MUST continue for life regardless of salary increases.

4. CONTRIBUTION RATES 2026

5. BENEFITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Employment Injury Scheme
- Medical Benefits: Free treatment at SOCSO panel clinics/hospitals
- Temporary Disablement Benefit: 80% of salary during recovery (max 24 months)
- Permanent Disablement Benefit: Monthly pension for life
- Dependants' Benefit: Monthly pension for dependants if employee dies
- Funeral Benefit: RM 2,000
- Rehabilitation: Physical and vocational rehabilitation
- Education Benefit: Financial assistance for children's education

5.2 Invalidity Pension Scheme
- Invalidity Pension: Monthly pension based on average wages
- Constant Attendance Allowance: Additional RM 200/month if constant care needed
- Survivors' Pension: Monthly pension for dependants
- Funeral Benefit: RM 2,000

6. CLAIM PROCEDURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Employer MUST report injury/accident within 7 DAYS using Form 21
- Employee seeks treatment at SOCSO panel facilities
- For invalidity claims: Submit Form 10 with medical reports
- SOCSO assesses and notifies of decision
- Benefits typically paid within 30-60 days of approval

7. EMPLOYER OBLIGATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Register company and all eligible employees with PERKESO
- Deduct and remit contributions monthly by 15th of following month
- Report workplace accidents within 7 days
- Keep employment records
- Cooperate with PERKESO inspections

8. PENALTIES FOR NON-COMPLIANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Late payment: interest charges
- Failure to register/contribute: fines and potential prosecution
- Failure to report accidents: fines

9. IMPORTANT NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- SOCSO is INSURANCE, not savings — contributions CANNOT be withdrawn
- SOCSO is separate from EPF (savings) and EIS (unemployment)
- Foreign workers covered for Employment Injury only since June 2019
- Wage ceiling increased to RM 6,000/month October 2024
