================================================================================
MALAYSIA MINIMUM WAGES ORDER 2026
Comprehensive Summary for HR & Employer Compliance
================================================================================
Source: National Wages Consultative Council Act 2011, QuickHR
Effective: February 2025 (Minimum Wages Order 2024)
================================================================================

1. CURRENT RATE (2026)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RM 1,700 PER MONTH (nationwide)
Hourly rate: RM 8.18
Daily rate (26-day basis): RM 65.38

Applied uniformly across ALL states and territories:
Peninsular Malaysia, Sabah, Sarawak, and Labuan.

2. LEGAL FRAMEWORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Governed by the Minimum Wages Order under the National Wages Consultative
Council Act 2011. Non-compliance is a LEGAL VIOLATION, not just HR oversight.

3. HISTORY OF MINIMUM WAGE INCREASES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Year    Rate (Monthly)         Coverage
2013    RM 900 / RM 800        Peninsular / Sabah & Sarawak (first introduced)
2016    RM 1,000 / RM 920      Peninsular / Sabah & Sarawak
2018    RM 1,050 / RM 920      Two-tier (cities / rural)
2020    RM 1,200               All states (uniform)
2022    RM 1,500               All states
Feb'25  RM 1,700               All states (current)

4. WHO IS COVERED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ALL private-sector employees, including:
- Probationary staff (from day one — NO grace period)
- Part-time workers (proportional based on hours worked)
- Foreign workers

5. CRITICAL EMPLOYER OBLIGATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Gross basic salary must be AT LEAST RM 1,700/month
- Allowances, incentives, and benefits CANNOT substitute for base pay
  (the BASIC WAGE itself must meet RM 1,700)
- Must issue itemised payslips showing basic wages, allowances,
  deductions, and net pay
- Must maintain wage records for at least 6 YEARS
- Records subject to JTKSM inspection

6. PENALTIES FOR NON-COMPLIANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Under National Wages Consultative Council Act 2011:

First offence: Fine up to RM 10,000 PER EMPLOYEE
Repeat offence: Fine up to RM 20,000 per employee OR imprisonment
Back-payment orders: Employer must pay ALL outstanding wages owed
Labour complaints: Employees can file directly with JTKSM office

7. FOREIGN WORKERS — SPECIAL PROVISION (June 2026)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Effective 1 June 2026:
- Higher minimum salary thresholds for Employment Pass (EP) holders
- All new and renewal EP applications must meet revised thresholds
- Applies to foreign professionals, not general foreign workers

8. IMPACT ON STATUTORY CONTRIBUTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
At RM 1,700/month minimum wage:
- EPF Employee (11%):  RM 187.00/month
- EPF Employer (13%):  RM 221.00/month
- SOCSO Employee:       RM 9.90/month
- SOCSO Employer:       RM 34.50/month
- EIS Employee (0.2%):  RM 3.40/month
- EIS Employer (0.2%):  RM 3.40/month

TOTAL minimum monthly employer cost per worker: RM 1,958.90
(not including overtime, allowances, HRDF levy, etc.)

9. KEY COMPLIANCE TIPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Audit all employee salaries before minimum wage increases
- Review part-timer hourly rates ensure meeting RM 8.18/hour
- Foreign worker EP holders: check June 2026 new thresholds
- Update payroll systems before effective date
- Document all salary structures for JTKSM inspection readiness
