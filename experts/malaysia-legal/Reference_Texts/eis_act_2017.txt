================================================================================
MALAYSIA EMPLOYMENT INSURANCE SYSTEM ACT 2017 (Act 800)
Comprehensive Summary for HR & Employer Compliance
================================================================================
Source: PERKESO, QuickHR
Last Updated: 2026-03
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Employment Insurance System (EIS) provides financial assistance and
re-employment services for employees who lose their jobs. Administered by
PERKESO/SOCSO. Funded by monthly contributions from employers and employees.

2. WHO IS COVERED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Must contribute:
- Malaysian citizens/permanent residents aged 18-60
- Employed under a Contract of Service
- Working in private sector (full-time or part-time)

EXEMPTED categories:
- Domestic employees (domestic helpers)
- Civil servants
- Local authority and statutory body employees
- Self-employed individuals
- Employees aged 57+ who have never contributed to EIS
- Employees who have reached compulsory retirement age
- Fixed-term contract workers whose contract expired without renewal

3. CONTRIBUTION RATES 2026
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Wage ceiling: RM 6,000/month
Total rate: 0.4% of assumed monthly wage
  Employer: 0.2%
  Employee: 0.2%

Capped at 0.4% of RM 6,000 = RM 24/month maximum total contribution
  (Employer: RM 12, Employee: RM 12)

4. INCOME SUBJECT TO EIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INCLUDED:
- Basic salary
- Overtime pay
- Commission and service charges
- Allowances (housing, meal, performance-based)
- Incentives
- Payment for leave (annual, sick, maternity, rest days, public holidays)

EXCLUDED:
- Bonus and Director fees
- Expenses claims and reimbursements
- Mileage claims and travelling allowance
- EPF contribution
- Gratuity or dismissal/retrenchment payments
- Benefits-in-Kind

5. EIS BENEFITS (7 Types)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Job Search Allowance (JSA)
- Financial support for 3-6 months
- Starts at 80% of assumed monthly wage, gradually decreasing
- First month: 80%, decreasing to 30% by month 6

5.2 Early Re-employment Allowance (ERA)
- One-time 25% incentive of remaining JSA if accepting job during JSA period

5.3 Reduced Income Allowance (RIA)
- For workers with multiple income sources who lose some sources

5.4 Training Allowance
- Daily allowance of RM 10-RM 20 during approved training
- Training fee up to RM 4,000 paid directly to provider

5.5 Re-employment Placement Programme
- Job matching through MYFutureJobs
- Career counselling by Employment Services Officers

6. CONTRIBUTING QUALIFYING CONDITIONS (CQC)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For first EIS claim:
- Minimum 12 months' contributions within 24-month period
- Willing to participate in Re-employment Placement Programme
- Must register with MYFutureJobs
- Must report to PERKESO upon receiving job offer or resuming work

7. EMPLOYER OBLIGATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Register with PERKESO for EIS
- Deduct employee contributions from salary
- Remit BOTH employer + employee portions by 15th of following month
- Inform employees about EIS coverage

8. PAYMENT METHODS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Via PERKESO Assist Portal (eis.perkeso.gov.my):
- Internet banking
- Financial Process Exchange (FPX)
- Direct Debit Authorisation (DDA)
- Money order/cheque to PERKESO
- Bank counter (Maybank, RHB Bank, Public Bank)

9. PENALTIES FOR NON-COMPLIANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Late submission: prosecution, fine up to RM 10,000
- Non-submission: imprisonment up to 2 years
- 10% annual interest on late payments

10. CLAIM PROCEDURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Employee applies via EIS Portal or nearest PERKESO office
2. Provide termination letter and relevant documents
3. PERKESO verifies contribution record (CQC)
4. Register with MYFutureJobs for job search
5. Benefits processed upon approval
