## 2. TYPICAL LEASE TERMS

### Standard Commercial Lease Structure
- Lease Period: 3 years (3+3 option is standard; first fixed 3 years, tenant option to renew another 3)
- Deposit: Typically 3.5 months' gross rent equivalent
  - 2 months' security deposit (refundable)
  - 1 month's utility deposit (refundable)
  - 0.5 month's stamp duty fee
- Payment: Monthly in advance (1st of each month)
- Rent Review: Every 3 years (upon renewal), typically 10-15% increase
- Notice Period: 2-3 months before expiry

### Key Negotiation Points
- **Fit-out Period (免租期)**: 1-3 months rent-free, depending on fit-out scope; negotiate BEFORE signing
- **Fit-out Contribution**: Landlord may contribute RM5-15/sq ft towards fit-out; more in soft markets
- **Restoration Clause (复原条款)**: Critical — must clarify whether tenant must restore to original condition upon exit; negotiate for "as-is" handover
- **Subletting**: Usually requires landlord consent; some buildings prohibit entirely
- **Operating Hours**: Most office buildings 7am-7pm; after-hours AC charges MYR 150-400/hour (per floor)

### Service Charge & Sinking Fund
- Service Charge: MYR 1.50-3.50/sq ft/month (covers security, cleaning, common area maintenance)
- Sinking Fund: 10% of service charge (for major repairs)
- Total Occupancy Cost = Rent + Service Charge + Sinking Fund

## 3. MSC MALAYSIA STATUS BUILDINGS

MSC Malaysia Cybercentre/Cybercity status offers tax incentives for qualifying ICT companies:
- Pioneer Status: 100% tax exemption for 5+5 years (renewable)
- Investment Tax Allowance: 100% on qualifying capex for 5 years
- Key MSC locations: Cyberjaya, KL Sentral, TRX, Penang Bayan Lepas, Johor Medini
- Updated under MDEC's Malaysia Digital (MD) framework since 2022

## 5. REGISTERED OFFICE REQUIREMENT (Companies Act 2016)

Section 237(1): Every company MUST maintain a registered office in Malaysia:
- Must be a physical address (not PO Box)
- All statutory records kept here or at notified place (S47)
- Company name must be displayed outside (S30)
- Change of registered office must be notified to SSM within 14 days

## 6. PRACTICAL TIPS

- Always engage a licensed real estate negotiator (LPPEH-registered) — landlord covers agent fee
- Verify land title and strata title ownership before signing
- Check for outstanding quit rent and assessment tax
- Include "quiet enjoyment" clause and "force majeure" (COVID lesson)
- For serviced offices/virtual offices: confirm SSM acceptance as registered office
- Fit-out approval: must submit plans to building management + local council (if structural changes)
- Signboard: separate license from local council (see business_premise_license_malaysia.txt)
