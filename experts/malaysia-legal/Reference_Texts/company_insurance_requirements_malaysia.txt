# Malaysia Company Insurance Requirements
## Source: Workmen's Compensation Act 1952; SOCSO Act 1969; Financial Services Act 2013; industry insurance benchmarks
## Validity: 2026

---

## 1. MANDATORY INSURANCE (强制保险)

### Workmen's Compensation Insurance (Foreign Workers)
- All foreign workers (non-Malaysian) MUST be covered under Workmen's Compensation Insurance
- Required under Workmen's Compensation Act 1952 + amendments under Foreign Workers Compensation Scheme (FWCS)
- Coverage obtained through approved insurers (appointed by Bank Negara + Ministry of Finance)
- Premium: approximately MYR 60-130/year per foreign worker (varies by insurer)
- Coverage: death, permanent disablement, medical expenses up to statutory limits
- Must be maintained for the ENTIRE duration of employment pass

### SOCSO (PERKESO) — for Malaysian & Permanent Resident Employees
- Mandatory for ALL Malaysian employees and permanent residents
- Not required for foreign workers (replaced by Workmen's Compensation Insurance)
- Coverage: Employment Injury Scheme (work accident) + Invalidity Pension Scheme (long-term disability)
- Premium: shared employer + employee (see statutory_filing_calendar_malaysia.txt for rates)

## 2. RECOMMENDED CORPORATE INSURANCE (建议商业保险)

### Group Hospitalisation & Surgical (GHS) — Employee Medical Insurance
- Not legally mandatory, but market norm for professional/office employees
- Typical coverage per employee: MYR 20,000-100,000 annual limit (per hospitalisation)
- Room & board: MYR 150-300/day
- Premium: MYR 600-2,500/employee/year (varies by age, plan, deductible)
- Option to cover dependents (spouse + children) at additional cost
- Probation: coverage typically starts upon confirmation (3-6 months)

### Group Term Life (GTL)
- Sum assured: typically 24-36 months of salary
- Premium: MYR 0.50-1.50 per MYR 1,000 sum assured
- Often bundled with Group Personal Accident (GPA)

### Group Personal Accident (GPA)
- 24/7 worldwide coverage for accidental death, permanent disablement
- Sum assured: typically 24-60 months of salary
- Premium: MYR 0.30-0.80 per MYR 1,000 sum assured

### Public Liability Insurance
- Essential for: businesses with customer-facing premises (retail, F&B, offices with visitors)
- Coverage: bodily injury or property damage to third parties on premises
- Typical sum insured: MYR 1-5 million
- Premium: MYR 500-3,000/year

### Professional Indemnity Insurance
- Essential for: consultants, accountants, lawyers, architects, IT service providers
- Coverage: errors & omissions, professional negligence claims
- Sum insured: MYR 500,000-5,000,000 (varies by contract requirements)
- Premium: 0.5-3% of sum insured

## 3. PROPERTY & ASSET INSURANCE (财产保险)

### Fire Insurance (Building & Contents)
- Required: for leased premises (landlord typically insures building; tenant insures contents)
- Coverage: fire, lightning, explosion, riot, strike, malicious damage
- Sum insured: reinstatement value of contents + fit-out
- For strata properties: Management Corporation carries master fire policy (check before double-insuring)

### Burglary / Theft Insurance
- Coverage: theft following forcible entry
- Premium: based on security features (alarm, CCTV, safe)
- Typical sum insured: MYR 50,000-500,000

### Machinery Breakdown / Electronic Equipment
- Essential for: manufacturers, data centres, IT-heavy operations
- Coverage: sudden and unforeseen physical damage requiring repair/replacement

---

## 4. DIRECTORS & OFFICERS (D&O) LIABILITY INSURANCE

- Covers directors and officers against personal liability for wrongful acts in managing the company
- Increasingly expected under Malaysian Code on Corporate Governance (MCCG 2021)
- Relevant after S17A MACC Act (corporate liability for corruption — 2020 amendment)
- Typical sum insured: MYR 1-10 million
- Premium: MYR 3,000-20,000/year depending on company size/sector

---

## 5. CYBER INSURANCE (网络安全保险)

- Increasingly relevant under PDPA 2010 (data breach notification requirement)
- Coverage: data breach response costs, forensic investigation, legal liability, business interruption
- Sum insured: MYR 500,000-5,000,000
- Premium: MYR 3,000-15,000/year

---

## 6. FOREIGN WORKER INSURANCE — ADDITIONAL COVERS

### Foreign Worker Hospitalisation & Surgical (FWHS / SKHPPA)
- Mandatory since 2011 for all foreign workers under the Employment Pass
- Administered through approved insurers under FWCS
- Annual premium: approx MYR 120 per worker
- Coverage: MYR 10,000-20,000 annual hospitalisation limit

### Foreign Worker Guarantee Bond (Bank Guarantee)
- Required by Immigration Department for certain categories
- Amount: MYR 250-2,500 per worker (varies by nationality and sector)
- Purpose: repatriation cost guarantee
