MALAYSIA EPF — EMPLOYEES PROVIDENT FUND ACT 1991 (Act 452)
Comprehensive Summary for HR & Employer Compliance

1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Employees Provident Fund (EPF/KWSP) is Malaysia's mandatory retirement
savings scheme. Both employer and employee contribute a percentage of wages.
EPF is governed by the EPF Act 1991 and administered by KWSP.

2. CONTRIBUTION RATES 2025-2026
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Standard Rates (Malaysian Citizens & PRs, Age < 60)

Salary ≤ RM 5,000:
  Employee: 11%   Employer: 13%   Total: 24%

Salary > RM 5,000:
  Employee: 11%   Employer: 12%   Total: 23%

Optional reduced employee rate:
  Employee: 9% (voluntary election)   Employer: 12%   Total: 21%

2.2 Age 60 and Above

Mandatory:
  Employee: 0%   Employer: 4%   Total: 4%

Voluntary maximum:
  Employee: 5.5%   Employer: 4%   Total: 9.5%

2.3 Foreign Workers
Since October 2025: BOTH employer AND employee MUST contribute 2% each (total 4%)
Employer contribution: 2% (MANDATORY)
Employee contribution: 2% (MANDATORY — confirmed by KWSP, https://www.kwsp.gov.my/en/w/news/epf-begins-mandatory-contributions-for-non-malaysian-citizen-employees-effective-october-2025)

3. NO WAGE CEILING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Unlike SOCSO (capped at RM 6,000), EPF has NO wage ceiling.
Contributions are calculated on ACTUAL wages paid.

4. CONTRIBUTION SCHEDULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EPF uses the THIRD SCHEDULE of the EPF Act 1991 which provides EXACT
contribution amounts for every wage level. Employers must refer to this
schedule for accurate deductions.

5. PAYMENT DEADLINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Contributions MUST be remitted by the 15th of the following month.
Late payment attracts DIVIDEND-BASED interest penalty.

6. EMPLOYER OBLIGATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Register company and all eligible employees with KWSP
- Deduct and remit contributions monthly by 15th
- Maintain accurate wage records
- Issue itemised payslips
- Keep payroll records for minimum 6 years
- Report new hires within 7 days

7. PENALTIES FOR NON-COMPLIANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Late payment: interest at dividend rate on outstanding amount
- Failure to contribute: fine up to RM 10,000 or imprisonment up to 3 years
- False statements: fine up to RM 20,000 or imprisonment up to 6 years
- Employers CANNOT deduct employer portion from employee wages

8. EPF DIVIDEND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Declared annually by KWSP
- Historical rates: 5-6% per annum range
- Tax-free returns for members
- Dividend credited to member accounts

9. WITHDRAWALS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Age 55: Full withdrawal (Account 1 + Account 2)
- Account 2 withdrawals (before 55):
  - Housing (purchase/build/renovate)
  - Education (self/children)
  - Medical expenses (serious illness)
  - Hajj pilgrimage
  - Age 50: partial withdrawal
- Account 1: primarily for retirement; limited pre-retirement access

10. CONTRIBUTION EXAMPLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
At RM 1,700/month (minimum wage):
  Employee (11%):  RM 187.00
  Employer (13%):  RM 221.00
  TOTAL monthly:   RM 408.00 per employee
