================================================================================
PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION (AMENDMENT) ACT 2024 (Act A1727)
Summary of Key Changes
Effective: Phased — 1 Jan 2025 / 1 Apr 2025 / 1 Jun 2025
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
The Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act 2024 received royal assent on
9 October 2024, gazetted 24 December 2024, and came into effect in 3 phases.

PHASE 1 — 1 January 2025
- Administrative amendments (electronic service of notices, etc.)

PHASE 2 — 1 April 2025
1. Terminology change: "data user" → "data controller"
2. Definition updates:
   - "Sensitive personal data" expanded to include BIOMETRIC data
   - "Personal data breach" defined (breach/loss/misuse/unauthorised access)
   - Deceased individuals' data EXCLUDED from PDPA scope
3. Data processors directly regulated under s.9 Security Principle
4. INCREASED PENALTIES:
   - s.5(2): Max fine raised from RM300,000 → RM1,000,000
   - Imprisonment raised from 2 years → 3 years
5. Cross-border data transfer (s.129) amendments:
   - Whitelisting regime REMOVED
   - Transfer permitted to countries with SUBSTANTIALLY SIMILAR data
     protection laws or equivalent level of protection
   - Transfer Impact Assessment (TIA) required to evaluate receiving
     jurisdiction's legal framework

PHASE 3 — 1 June 2025
6. Mandatory Data Protection Officer (DPO) — NEW s.12A
   - Data controllers AND data processors must appoint DPO
   - Thresholds (per DPO Guidelines 2025):
     a) Processes personal data of >20,000 individuals, OR
     b) Processes sensitive personal data of >10,000 individuals, OR
     c) Engages in regular/systematic monitoring of individuals
   - DPO must reside in Malaysia ≥180 days/year (or be easily contactable)
   - DPO must be proficient in BM and English
   - DPO must have good understanding of PDPA
   - DPO appointment must be reported to Commissioner within 21 days
   - Dedicated business email for DPO required
   - DPO can be in-house or outsourced

7. Mandatory Data Breach Notification (DBN) — NEW s.12B
   - Data controller must notify Commissioner of personal data breach
   - Timeline: as soon as practicable, within 72 hours of awareness
   - If breach likely to cause SIGNIFICANT HARM:
     Notify affected individuals within 7 DAYS
   - Significant harm includes: physical injury, financial loss,
     identity fraud risk, sensitive data exposure
   - Maintain breach records for MINIMUM 2 YEARS

8. Right to Data Portability — NEW
   - Individuals can request transfer of personal data between controllers
   - Subject to technical feasibility
   - Data must be in commonly used, machine-readable format

PENALTY SUMMARY (post-2024 Amendment)
- General data protection principles (s.5): RM1,000,000 / 3 years
- Cross-border transfer violation (s.129): RM300,000 / 2 years
- Sensitive personal data violation (s.40): RM200,000 / 2 years
- Data breach notification violation (s.12B): RM250,000 / 2 years
- DPO non-compliance (s.12A): subject to administrative direction

KEY GUIDELINES ISSUED (2025)
1. DPO Guidelines (Circular No. 1/2025) — effective 1 June 2025
2. DBN Guidelines (Circular No. 2/2025) — effective 1 June 2025
3. Cross-Border Personal Data Transfer (CBPDT) Guidelines — 29 April 2025
4. Data Portability Guidelines
