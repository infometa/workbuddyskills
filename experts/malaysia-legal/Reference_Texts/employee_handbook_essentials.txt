1. WHY AN EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK MATTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A comprehensive employee handbook provides:
- Clear communication of COMPANY POLICIES
- LEGAL COMPLIANCE documentation
- REFERENCE for dispute resolution
- DEFENCE in Industrial Court: shows employer had clear policies
- Onboarding efficiency for new hires

2. ESSENTIAL SECTIONS — MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 Company Introduction
- Company name, registration number, address
- Core values, mission statement
- Organisation structure overview

2.2 Employment Terms
- Probation period (typically 3-6 months, max 6 months by convention)
- Confirmation criteria
- Working hours (8 hours/day, 45 hours/week maximum)
- Rest days and rest breaks
- Overtime policy (rate: 1.5x normal, 2x rest day, 3x PH)
- Flexible working arrangements policy (NEW — must address FWA)

2.3 Remuneration & Benefits
- Salary structure and payment date
- Allowances (fixed, travel, shift, etc.)
- Overtime calculation method
- EPF, SOCSO, EIS contributions (statutory)
- Medical benefits (outpatient, hospitalisation, dental)
- Insurance coverage (group term life, personal accident)
- Leave entitlements (see Section 3)

2.4 Leave Entitlements
ANNUAL LEAVE (Employment Act minimum):
  < 2 years: 8 days
  2-5 years: 12 days
  > 5 years: 16 days
(Many employers provide 14-21 days regardless)

SICK LEAVE:
  < 2 years: 14 days
  2-5 years: 18 days
  > 5 years: 22 days
Hospitalisation: up to 60 days

MATERNITY LEAVE: 98 days (Employment Act)
PATERNITY LEAVE: 7 consecutive working days (Employment Act)
PUBLIC HOLIDAYS: 11 gazetted holidays per year
MARRIAGE LEAVE: 3 days (if company policy)
COMPASSIONATE/BEREAVEMENT: 3 days (if company policy)

2.5 Code of Conduct
- Attendance and punctuality
- Dress code
- Workplace behaviour and respect
- Anti-harassment policy (MANDATORY under Anti-Sexual Harassment Act 2022)
- Social media usage
- Conflicts of interest
- Whistleblower protection
- Gift and entertainment policy

2.6 Disciplinary Procedure
- Minor misconduct → verbal warning → written warning → final warning
- Major misconduct → Show-cause letter → Domestic Inquiry → decision
- Right to representation
- Appeal procedure
- Examples of minor vs. major misconduct

2.7 Safety and Health
- Employer's OSH obligations
- Employee's OSH duties
- Emergency procedures
- First aid
- Reporting accidents

2.8 Data Protection and Confidentiality
- PDPA 2010 compliance
- Confidentiality of company information
- Data security (especially for remote workers)
- Return of company property on termination

2.9 IT and Communication
- Acceptable use of company IT systems
- Email and internet usage
- Software licensing
- Data backup
- Social media policy

2.10 Grievance Procedure
- How to raise complaints
- Escalation path
- Investigation timeline
- Non-retaliation guarantee
- External avenues (Labour Office, Industrial Court)

3. OPTIONAL BUT RECOMMENDED SECTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Performance management / appraisal system
- Training and development
- Career progression
- Employee recognition
- Corporate social responsibility
- Environmental policy
- Remote working policy (detailed)
- BYOD (Bring Your Own Device) policy
- Travel and expense policy
- Personal data consent (PDPA)

4. LEGAL REQUIREMENTS — MUST-DISPLAY ITEMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Employers MUST display workplace notices:
- Anti-sexual harassment policy (Anti-Sexual Harassment Act 2022)
- Working hours and break times
- EPF/SOCSO registration numbers
- OSHA policy (if company has 5+ employees)

5. HANDBOOK MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Issuance
- Provide to EVERY employee (during onboarding)
- Have employee SIGN acknowledgment of receipt
- Keep signed copy in personnel file

5.2 Updates
- Review annually; update for new laws
- Notify ALL employees of changes in writing
- Update acknowledgment if changes are material

5.3 Disclaimer
- "This handbook does not constitute an employment contract"
- "The Company reserves the right to amend policies"
- "In case of conflict with Malaysian law, law prevails"
