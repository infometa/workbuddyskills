================================================================================
MALAYSIA ACT 446 — EMPLOYEES' MINIMUM STANDARDS OF HOUSING,
ACCOMMODATIONS AND AMENITIES ACT 1990
Comprehensive Summary for HR & Employer Compliance
================================================================================
Source: JTKSM, Patuh446.my, AGC
Last Amended: Act A1604 (2019 Amendments)
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Act 446 prescribes minimum standards for worker housing and nurseries for
workers and their dependants. It is enforced by JTKSM (Department of Labour
Peninsular Malaysia) through mandatory certification. Originally the "Workers'
Minimum Standards of Housing and Amenities Act 1990", renamed "Employees'..."

2. WHO MUST COMPLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ALL employers who provide accommodation to workers, particularly:
- Manufacturing sectors with foreign worker hostels
- Construction sites with worker quarters
- Plantation and agricultural sectors
- Any employer providing centralized or non-centralized worker housing

3. KEY SPACE STANDARDS (JTK Requirements)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Minimum Floor Area per Worker
- 3.6 SQUARE METRES (3.6 sqm / 38.75 sq ft) per worker
  (NOTE: Some official guidelines reference 3.0 sqm, but 3.6 sqm is
   the JTK audit standard for new certifications)

3.2 Minimum Air Volume
- 20 CUBIC METRES per worker for dormitory-style rooms

3.3 Bed Requirements
- Individual bed/mattress for each worker (NO shared beds)
- Bunk beds: must meet structural safety standards
- Minimum spacing between beds

4. SANITATION STANDARDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Toilet Ratio
- 1 toilet : 15 workers
- Separate male/female facilities
- Must have proper flushing system

4.2 Bathroom Ratio
- 1 shower/bathroom : 15 workers
- Adequate water supply (hot and cold)

4.3 Kitchen Facilities
- Adequate cooking facilities
- Proper ventilation
- Clean water supply
- Food storage areas

5. TYPES OF ACCOMMODATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Centralized Accommodation
- Purpose-built hostel blocks
- Requires Certificate of Accommodation from JTKSM
- Must meet all Act 446 standards

5.2 Non-Centralized Accommodation
- Rented houses/apartments
- Also subject to Act 446 if provided by employer
- Must meet minimum standards

6. JTKSM CERTIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Certificate of Accommodation
- MANDATORY for centralized accommodation
- Application via JTKSM IDP (Integrated Digital Portal)
- Requires:
  - Floor plans with measurements
  - Worker list and capacity calculations
  - Photos of facilities
  - Supporting documents (tenancy agreement, SSM, etc.)

6.2 Audit Process
JTK auditors check:
- Room capacity and floor area calculations
- Bed/bunk bed arrangements
- Toilet and bathroom facilities count
- Kitchen and dining areas
- Fire safety and emergency exits
- First aid facilities
- Waste management
- Cleanliness and pest control
- Water and electricity supply
- Worker records and occupancy register

7. PENALTIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Fine up to RM 50,000 per offence
- Possible imprisonment for serious/repeat violations
- PLKS (foreign worker pass) renewal may be BLOCKED
- Hostel closure orders
- Compound fines for minor violations

8. EMPLOYER OBLIGATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Provide accommodation meeting minimum standards
- Apply for Certificate of Accommodation before occupancy
- Maintain worker records and occupancy register
- Allow JTKSM inspections
- Renew certification periodically
- Report changes in occupancy or facilities

9. 2019 AMENDMENTS (Act A1604)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Key changes:
- Extended coverage to ALL sectors (not just plantations/mining)
- Strengthened enforcement powers
- Increased penalties
- Added requirement for Certificate of Accommodation
- Specified detailed facility standards

10. PRACTICAL COMPLIANCE TIPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Calculate room capacity using 3.6 sqm formula BEFORE applying
- Document ALL measurements with photos
- Keep occupancy register updated daily
- Schedule regular self-audits
- Use digital tools (Patuh446) for compliance tracking
- Budget RM 50,000+ fines avoidance = invest in proper facilities
