================================================================================
MALAYSIA BENEFITS-IN-KIND (BIK) — TAX TREATMENT GUIDE
Comprehensive Summary for HR & Payroll Compliance
================================================================================
Source: LHDN Public Ruling No. 11/2019, ANC Group, AutoCount, AJOBTHING
Applicable: YA 2025-2026
================================================================================

1. WHAT IS BIK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Benefits-in-Kind (BIK) are NON-CASH benefits provided by employer to employee
that are TAXABLE as part of employment income. Defined under Section 32 of the
Income Tax Act 1967. BIK must be reported in Form EA (Section B) and included
in employee's PCB/MTD calculation.

2. TAXABLE BIK — KEY CATEGORIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Company Car (Public Ruling 11/2019)
TAXABLE VALUE per annum (based on car cost):
Car Cost (RM)            Annual BIK Value
Up to 50,000             RM 1,200
50,001 - 75,000          RM 2,400
75,001 - 100,000         RM 3,600
100,001 - 150,000        RM 5,000
150,001 - 200,000        RM 7,000
200,001 - 250,000        RM 9,000
250,001 - 350,000        RM 15,000
350,001 - 500,000        RM 21,250
Above 500,000            RM 25,000

FUEL (if company pays): Additional value range RM 600 - RM 2,600/year
based on car cost.
DRIVER: Additional RM 600/month if driver provided.

2.2 Accommodation
BIK value: LOWER of:
- 30% of employment income AND
- Defined value of premises (or actual rent paid by employer)
Unfurnished: as above
Furnished: + RM 840/year (lounge/dining) or RM 3,360/year (semi/full)

2.3 Utilities (employer-paid)
Water/electricity/telephone: ACTUAL amount paid by employer
Must be itemised if employer pays directly

2.4 Loan Subsidy
If employer provides INTEREST-FREE or LOW-INTEREST loan:
BIK = (Market interest rate - Actual interest rate) x Loan amount
Market interest rate set by LHDN (typically ~4%)

2.5 Medical & Dental
Outpatient medical: TAX EXEMPT (per Income Tax (Exemption) Order)
Dental: TAX EXEMPT
Maternity: TAX EXEMPT
Traditional medicine (ayurveda, acupuncture): TAX EXEMPT
Medical treatment for SERIOUS DISEASES: up to RM 3,000 exemption

2.6 Insurance Premiums
Life insurance paid by employer: TAXABLE (but employee may claim personal
relief on own contribution)
Medical insurance paid by employer: TAX EXEMPT (up to RM 3,000)
Group insurance: employer contribution taxable if > RM 3,000/year

3. TAX EXEMPT BIK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOT taxable:
- Meals provided during working hours
- Transport between pick-up points and workplace
- Parking at workplace
- Uniforms and protective clothing (required for job)
- Childcare provided at workplace (up to RM 2,400/year)
- Mobile phones (one per employee)
- Broadband subscription (one per employee)
- Sports/recreational club membership
- Long service awards (not exceeding RM 1,000)
- Staff discounts (on company products, cost value or less)
- Training and conference expenses

4. PCB/MTD APPLICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The BIK value (annual) must be DIVIDED by 12 and ADDED to monthly salary
for PCB calculation. This ensures tax is deducted progressively rather than
withheld all at year-end.

Formula: Monthly PCB calculation base = Gross Salary + (Annual BIK/12)
BIK value MUST appear in employee's Form EA (Borang EA) end-of-year.

5. PERQUISITES (Cash Allowances Taxable)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Unlike BIK (non-cash), perquisites are CASH or CONVERTIBLE benefits:
- Petrol allowance
- Travel allowance (personal use portion)
- Handphone allowance (if > actual business use)
- Club membership fees
- Housing allowance
- Meal allowance

ALL perquisites are TAXABLE in full and included in gross employment income.

6. EMPLOYER OBLIGATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Calculate BIK values annually for all employees receiving benefits
- Include in monthly PCB/MTD withholding
- Report in Form EA Section B (by 28 February each year)
- Maintain records of all benefits provided
- Keep supporting documents for 7 years
- Apply for LHDN advance ruling if uncertain about BIK classification

7. COMMON AUDIT ISSUES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LHDN commonly flags:
- Company car BIK under-reported (using wrong car cost bracket)
- Accommodation value understated (using below-market rates)
- Foreign expatriates' benefits NOT reported at all
- Directors' benefits treated as business expenses
- Commuting allowance misclassified as travel allowance
