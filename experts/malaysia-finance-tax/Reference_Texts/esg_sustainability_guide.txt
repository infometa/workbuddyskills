# Part 1: National Sustainability Reporting Framework (NSRF)

## 1.1 Overview

The Securities Commission Malaysia launched the NSRF on 24 September 2024, developed jointly with Bank Negara Malaysia, Bursa Malaysia, and the Audit Oversight Board. Malaysia is the first ASEAN economy to mandate ISSB-aligned sustainability disclosures at national level.

**Baseline standards:**
- IFRS S1 (General Requirements for Disclosure of Sustainability-related Financial Information)
- IFRS S2 (Climate-related Disclosures)
- Climate-first approach during transition: issuers may begin with climate disclosures before expanding to broader sustainability topics

## 1.2 Phased Compliance Timeline

### Phase 1: 2025
- Main Market listed issuers with market capitalisation ≥ RM2 billion (~130 companies, >80% of Bursa total market cap)
- Climate-first reporting for annual periods starting 1 Jan 2025
- Full IFRS S1 and S2 compliance required from 1 Jan 2027

### Phase 2: 2026
- All other Main Market listed issuers
- Climate-first reporting for annual periods starting 1 Jan 2026
- Full IFRS S1 and S2 compliance required from 1 Jan 2028

### Phase 3: 2027
- ACE Market listed corporations
- Large non-listed companies with revenue ≥ RM2 billion
- Climate-first reporting starting 1 Jan 2027
- Full compliance required from 1 Jan 2030

### Transition Reliefs
- Main Market issuers: 2 full financial years of transition relief
- ACE Market and large non-listed: 3 years of transition relief
- During transition, issuers may focus on climate disclosures before addressing full IFRS S1

## 1.3 Scope 3 Emissions Timeline

| Cohort | Limited Scope 3 | Full Scope 3 (all 15 GHG Protocol categories) |
|-------|----------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Main Market ≥ RM2bn | From Jan 2025 | From Jan 2028 |
| Main Market < RM2bn | From Jan 2026 | From Jan 2029 |
| ACE Market + large non-listed | From Jan 2027 | From Jan 2030 |

## 1.4 Assurance Roadmap

Reasonable assurance on Scope 1 and Scope 2 GHG emissions:

| Cohort | Assurance Start |
|-------|----------------|
| Main Market ≥ RM2bn | Jan 2027 |
| Main Market < RM2bn | Jan 2028 |
| ACE Market + large non-listed | Jan 2029 |

Scope 3 assurance will follow in later phases.

# Part 2: Bursa Malaysia Sustainability Reporting Requirements

## 2.1 4th Edition Sustainability Reporting Guide (Dec 2024)

The Bursa Malaysia sustainability reporting guide (4th Edition) integrates IFRS S1 and S2 into listing requirements. Every listed issuer must include a Sustainability Statement in its annual report covering:

- **Governance** — structures for sustainability oversight
- **Strategy** — management of sustainability-related risks and opportunities
- **Risk Management** — processes applied to sustainability matters
- **Metrics and Targets** — performance measurement indicators

## 2.2 Prescribed Performance Data Table

Minimum 3 years of ESG performance data required. Prescribed indicators include:
- Scope 1 and Scope 2 GHG emissions
- Energy consumption
- Water usage
- Waste management data
- Anti-corruption measures
- Workforce diversity metrics

Bursa specifies measurement methodologies for each indicator.

## 2.3 Materiality Assessment

Every issuer must conduct and document a materiality assessment aligned with IFRS S1 principles — identifying sustainability-related risks and opportunities that could reasonably affect cash flows, access to finance, or cost of capital.

## 2.4 ESG Reporting Platform (Bursa LINK)

All ESG disclosures must be submitted through the ESG Reporting Platform on Bursa LINK. The platform enables structured, comparable, machine-readable disclosures.

# Part 3: SEDG — Simplified ESG Disclosure Guide for SMEs

## 3.1 Overview

Capital Markets Malaysia launched SEDG, making Malaysia the first country globally with a standardised, structured ESG disclosure framework for SMEs.

### Structure
- **Version 1**: 35 priority disclosures across 3 tiers (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced)
- **Version 2**: Adds 3 disclosures, aligns with ASEAN Simplified ESG Disclosure Guide (launched Apr 2025)
- Aligned with Bursa Malaysia sustainability indicators, GRI Standards, ISSB Standards, and GHG Protocol

### Tiers
| Tier | Focus | SME Readiness |
|-----|-------|--------------|
| Basic | Foundational ESG data (energy, waste, workforce) | Any SME |
| Intermediate | Supply chain, product responsibility | SME with basic ESG data |
| Advanced | Full value chain, climate risk assessment | ESG-mature SME |

SMEs can start at Basic tier and progress as data maturity improves.

## 3.2 Why SEDG Matters for Listed Companies

PLCs reporting Scope 3 emissions need emissions data from suppliers. SEDG readiness in the supply chain directly affects a listed company's data quality, assurance readiness, and Bursa LINK submission. Engaging suppliers on SEDG adoption is a data governance requirement, not a CSR exercise.

# Part 4: Green & Sustainable Finance

## 4.1 SC SRI Sukuk Framework

The Securities Commission's Sustainable and Responsible Investment (SRI) Sukuk framework:

- Established in 2014, first of its kind globally
- Proceeds used for green/social/sustainability projects
- Tax incentives: Stamp duty exemption on SRI Sukuk instruments, tax deduction for issuance costs
- Malaysia remains the world's largest SRI Sukuk market

### SRI Taxonomy
| Category | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Green | Renewable energy, energy efficiency, pollution prevention, green buildings |
| Social | Affordable housing, healthcare access, education, microfinance |
| Sustainability | Mix of green and social objectives |
| Sustainability-linked | KPIs-based, with coupon step-up/step-down |

## 4.2 BNM Value-based Intermediation (VBI)

BNM's VBI framework guides Islamic financial institutions in delivering outcomes aligned with Shariah principles of justice and sustainability:

- **VBI Strategy Paper** (2018): Foundation framework
- **VBI Sectoral Guides**: Palm oil, renewable energy, energy efficiency
- **Value-based Intermediation Financing and Investment Assessment** (VBIAF): Assessment framework for Shariah-compliant sustainable finance
- **i-ESG**: Integrated ESG assessment for Islamic finance

## 4.3 Green Technology Financing Scheme (GTFS)

Managed by MGTC (Malaysian Green Technology and Climate Change Corporation):

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Maximum loan | Up to RM20 million |
| Interest subsidy | 2% reduction on market rate |
| Guarantee coverage | 60% (via CGC/SJPP) |
| Eligible sectors | Renewable energy, energy efficiency, green buildings, green data centres |
| Tenure | Up to 15 years |

## 4.4 ESG-Linked Loans

Banks offering preferential rates for SMEs meeting ESG criteria:

| ESG Achievement | Rate Discount |
|----------------|--------------|
| Environmental certification (ISO 14001) | 0.50% reduction |
| Social responsibility benchmarks | 0.25% reduction |
| Governance standards (internal audit) | 0.25% reduction |
| Combined ESG score above threshold | Up to 1.00% reduction |

# Part 5: Carbon Tax & Climate Policy

## 5.1 Carbon Pricing in Malaysia

### Current Status (2026)
- Malaysia has not yet formally legislated a carbon tax
- **Proposed implementation**: 2026-2027, starting with iron, steel, and energy sectors
- **Estimated rate**: RM15–RM50 per tonne CO2e (final rate under consultation)
- PETRONAS voluntary carbon credit purchase: RM50/tonne CO2 (from 2023)

### National Energy Transition Roadmap (NETR)
| Phase | Timeline | Key Targets |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| Phase 1 | 2021-2025 | RE capacity 40% of total mix |
| Phase 2 | 2026-2030 | RE capacity 50%, carbon tax implementation |
| Phase 3 | 2031-2040 | RE capacity 70%, net-zero grid |
| Phase 4 | 2041-2050 | RE capacity >85%, net-zero emissions |

### CBAM Alignment
The sectors under Malaysia's proposed carbon tax overlap with EU CBAM:
- Iron, steel, aluminium, cement, fertilisers, energy
- Malaysian exporters in these sectors face dual compliance: domestic carbon tax + CBAM reporting for EU-bound goods

## 5.2 Carbon Exchange & Trading

- **Malaysia Carbon Exchange (MCX)** launched by Bursa Malaysia
- Voluntary carbon market (VCM) trading platform
- Types: Nature-based (forestry, mangrove), technology-based (renewable energy offsets)
- Pricing: Typically MYR 30-80/tonne for nature-based credits

# Part 6: National Energy Transition & Green Investment Incentives

## 6.1 Green Investment Tax Allowance (GITA)

| Category | Incentive | Qualifying Activities |
|---------|-----------|---------------------|
| GITA (Assets) | 100% ITA on qualifying capital expenditure for 5 years | Solar/PV, biomass, mini-hydro, energy efficiency equipment |
| GITA (Projects) | 100% ITA on qualifying capital expenditure for 5 years | Integrated waste management, green building, green data centre |

## 6.2 Green Income Tax Exemption (GITE)

- **GITE (Services)**: 70% statutory income exemption for 5 years
- **Qualifying activities**: Green technology services (consultancy, design, testing, commissioning)
- Available until YA 2030

## 6.3 Budget 2026 Green Allocations

| Scheme | Allocation | Purpose |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| GTFS continuation | RM2 billion | Green technology financing |
| Low Carbon City grants | RM200 million | Urban sustainability |
| EV infrastructure | RM500 million | Charging stations, EV incentives |
| Reforestation credits | RM100 million | Carbon credit generation |

# Part 7: Key Compliance Deadlines & Reminders

| Obligation | Deadline | Applicable To |
|-----------|----------|---------------|
| Bursa Sustainability Statement (Phase 2) | Annual periods starting ≥ 1 Jan 2026 | Main Market < RM2bn market cap |
| Full IFRS S1/S2 (Phase 1) | Annual periods starting ≥ 1 Jan 2027 | Main Market ≥ RM2bn market cap |
| Full IFRS S1/S2 (Phase 2) | Annual periods starting ≥ 1 Jan 2028 | All Main Market issuers |
| Reasonable assurance S1/S2 (Phase 1) | Jan 2027 | Main Market ≥ RM2bn |
| Carbon tax legislation | Expected 2026-2027 | Iron, steel, energy sectors |
| SEDG alignment for SME suppliers | Increasingly expected from 2026 | SME supply chain |

## Key Reminders

- **ESG data infrastructure** is the rate-limiting factor — start building data pipelines 12+ months ahead
- **Scope 3 readiness** requires supplier engagement — SEDG adoption in supply chain is critical
- **Carbon tax + CBAM + NSRF** converge in the same regulatory cycle — integrated emissions data infrastructure reduces cumulative compliance cost
- **Green finance** (GTFS, ESG-linked loans) offers direct cost savings via lower interest rates
- **GITA/GITE** incentives for green technology remain available but expire YA 2030 — apply early
