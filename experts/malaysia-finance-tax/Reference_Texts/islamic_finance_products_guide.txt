# Malaysia Islamic Finance & Takaful Products Guide
# 马来西亚伊斯兰金融与保险产品指南
# Source: Bank Negara Malaysia, Securities Commission, ISRA
# Last updated: 2025

## Part 1: Core Principles
- Shariah principles: Prohibition of riba (interest), gharar (uncertainty), maysir (gambling)
- Asset-backed and risk-sharing transactions
- Shariah Committee oversight for all Islamic financial institutions

## Part 2: Islamic Banking Products

### 2.1 Murabahah (Cost-Plus Sale)
- Bank purchases asset, sells to customer at marked-up price with deferred payment
- Most common Islamic financing structure in Malaysia
- Used for: Home financing, vehicle financing, trade finance, working capital

### 2.2 Musharakah (Joint Venture / Partnership)
- Bank and customer contribute capital jointly; profits shared per agreed ratio
- Diminishing Musharakah: Bank's share reduces over time as customer buys it out
- Used for: Home financing, project financing, business expansion

### 2.3 Mudharabah (Profit-Sharing)
- Capital provider (Rab al-Mal) provides funds, entrepreneur (Mudharib) provides management
- Profits shared per agreed ratio; losses borne by capital provider
- Used for: Investment accounts, savings accounts, fund management

### 2.4 Ijarah (Leasing)
- Bank leases asset to customer for periodic rental payments
- Ijarah Muntahiyah bi al-Tamlik: Lease-to-own
- Used for: Vehicle financing, equipment leasing, aircraft financing

### 2.5 Istisna' (Manufacturing Contract)
- Bank contracts to manufacture/construct an asset; progress payments tied to milestones
- Used for: Property development, infrastructure projects

## Part 3: Sukuk (Islamic Bonds)
- Malaysia is the world's largest Sukuk market (~40% of global outstanding)
- Bursa Malaysia is the largest Sukuk listing exchange globally
- Types: Sukuk Ijarah (lease-based), Sukuk Murabahah (sale-based), Sukuk Musharakah (partnership-based)
- SRI Sukuk framework pioneered by Malaysia

## Part 4: Takaful (Islamic Insurance)
- Participants contribute to common fund (Tabarru'), mutual guarantee and risk-sharing
- Surplus distributed among participants (vs profits to shareholders)
- Family Takaful (life equivalent): Individual, investment-linked, medical, education
- General Takaful: Motor, fire, marine, personal accident, travel
- Key operators: Etiqa, AIA PUBLIC, Great Eastern, Prudential BSN, Syarikat Takaful Malaysia

## Part 5: Islamic Capital Markets
- Bursa Malaysia Shariah Index: Business screening (non-compliant <5-20%), Financial screening (debt <33%, cash <33%)
- Shariah Advisory Council (SAC) of SC: Highest Shariah authority for capital markets
- Islamic REITs (i-REITs): Rental income from Shariah-compliant properties

## Part 6: Regulatory Bodies
- BNM: Islamic banking and Takaful regulation
- SC: Islamic capital markets regulation
- SAC BNM: National Shariah authority for Islamic finance
- ISRA: Research and thought leadership
- INCEIF: Islamic finance education and training
