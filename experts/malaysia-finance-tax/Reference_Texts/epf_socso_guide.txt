# Part 1: EPF (Employees Provident Fund / KWSP)

## 1.1 Employer Contribution Rates (by employee wage tier)

| Monthly Wage | Employer Rate | Note |
|-------------|--------------|------|
| < RM 5,000 | 13% | Or 12% if classified under specific categories (e.g. wage < RM 5,000 but company elects lower rate) |
| ≥ RM 5,000 | 12% | Standard rate |
| Foreign workers | RM 5 per month | Fixed (opt-in for employee portion) |
| Domestic servants | RM 5 per month (employer only) | Optional registration |

## 1.2 Employee Contribution Rates

| Category | Rate |
|----------|------|
| Malaysian citizens & PRs | 11% of monthly wages |
| Reduced option | 9% (subject to EPF approval, periodic exercise) |
| Foreign workers | Not required (can opt in voluntarily) |
| Self-employed voluntary | 11% (variable option: 5%-30% of declared income) |

## 1.3 EPF Account Structure (from 2024)

| Account | Allocation | Purpose | Withdrawal Age |
|---------|-----------|---------|----------------|
| Account 1 (Retirement) | 70% of monthly contribution | Core retirement savings | Age 55 (partial) / 60 (full) |
| Account 2 (General) | 20% | Housing, education, medical | Age 50 (conditional) / 55 (full) |
| Account 3 (Flexible) | 10% (from 2024) | Short-term financial needs | Anytime (min. RM 50 withdrawal) |

### Withdrawal Conditions for Account 2
- **Housing**: Purchase, build, reduce loan; up to full Account 2 balance
- **Education**: Self, spouse, children; up to full Account 2 (approved institutions)
- **Medical**: Critical illness for self/family; up to full Account 2
- **Age 50 withdrawal**: Up to 30% of total savings (partial)

### Age 55 vs Age 60 Withdrawal
| Withdrawal | Full Amount | Account Structures |
|------------|-------------|-------------------|
| Age 55 | Up to full savings | Withdrawable entirely; no restriction |
| Age 60 | Any remaining savings | Cleared final account |

## 1.5 EPF Late Payment Penalties

- **Penalty rate**: 10% per annum on arrears (compounded monthly)
- **Penalty waiver**: Available for specific government relaxation programs (e.g., COVID-19 era)
- **Recovery action**: EPF can issue summons, garnish bank accounts, initiate winding-up proceedings

# Part 2: SOCSO (Social Security Organization / PERKESO)

## 2.1 Employment Injury Scheme (EIS)

- **Employer-only contribution**: 0.5% – 3.75% of monthly wage
- **Coverage**: All employees earning ≤ RM 5,000/month (mandatory); > RM 5,000 (voluntary)
- **Risk categories**:

| Category | Risk Level | Contribution Rate |
|----------|-----------|-----------------|
| Category 1 | Low risk (office work, admin, IT) | 0.50% |
| Category 2 | Medium risk (retail, light manufacturing) | 1.25% |
| Category 3 | Higher risk (construction, heavy industry) | 2.00% |
| Category 4 | High risk (mining, logging, demolition) | 3.75% |

### Benefits Covered
- Medical treatment (unlimited)
- Temporary disablement benefit (up to 24 months)
- Permanent disablement benefit (pension or lump sum)
- Death benefit (lump sum + pension to dependents)
- Dependents' benefit

## 2.2 Invalidity Pension Scheme (IPS)

| Contributor | Rate |
|-------------|------|
| Employer | 1.75% of monthly wage (capped at RM 5,000) |
| Employee | 0.50% of monthly wage (capped at RM 5,000) |
| Total | 2.25% of monthly wage |

### Conditions
- Must have at least 24 months' contributions (for invalidity pension)
- Covers: Invalidity pension, survivors benefits, funeral benefits (RM 2,000)
- Return of contributions if no claim made by age 55

## 2.3 SOCSO Late Payment Penalty

- **Penalty**: 6% per annum on arrears
- Late payment fee: Additional RM 50 per offence

# Part 3: Employment Insurance System (EIS / SIP)

## 3.1 Contribution Rates

| Contributor | Rate |
|-------------|------|
| Employer | 0.20% of monthly wage (capped at RM 5,000) |
| Employee | 0.20% of monthly wage (capped at RM 5,000) |
| Total | 0.40% of monthly wage |

## 3.2 Benefits

| Benefit Type | Description | Duration |
|-------------|-------------|----------|
| Job Search Allowance (JSA) | Up to 80% of last drawn wages (reducing scale) | Up to 6 months |
| Reduced Income Allowance | For those accepting lower-paying jobs | Up to 6 months |
| Training Fee | Approved training (up to RM 10,000) | One-off |
| Training Allowance | RM 30–100 per day during training | During training period |
| Early Re-employment Allowance | One-off payment if re-employed early | Before JSA exhausts |

# Part 4: HRDF (Human Resource Development Fund / HRD Corp)

## 4.1 Contribution Rate by Industry

| Industry Category | Rate | Threshold |
|------------------|------|-----------|
| Category A (Manufacturing, Mining, Quarrying) | 1.0% of monthly wage | ≥ 10 employees with paid-up ≥ RM 2.5M |
| Category B (Services, Trading, Agriculture) | 0.5% of monthly wage | ≥ 10 employees with paid-up ≥ RM 2.5M |

### Levy Cap
- Maximum monthly wage ceiling: RM 10,000 (capped contribution)
- Companies with < 10 employees or paid-up < RM 2.5M: Not mandatory (can opt in voluntarily)

### Training Grant Rates
| Company Type | Grant Rate (from levy) |
|-------------|----------------------|
| Standard levy-paying employer | Up to 100% of training costs |
| SME levy-paying employer | Up to 120% of training costs |
| Double deduction training | 200% (for approved programmes, via LHDN) |
| Digital upskilling levy | Additional 50% for digital/tech courses (2026) |

# Part 5: Employer Tax Obligations (PCB / Monthly Deductions)

## 5.1 Monthly Tax Deduction (PCB / MTD)

- **Rate**: Progressive rates (0-30%) based on chargeable income
- **Due date**: 15th of following month
- **Submission**: e-PCB via MyTax portal

### PCB Categories
| Category | Applies To |
|----------|-----------|
| Normal PCB | All employees with monthly income > RM 2,500 (after EPF deduction) |
| Additional PCB (PCB2) | Bonus, commission, director fees paid in lump sum |
| Monthly PCB Schedule | LHDN schedules based on MARITAL STATUS AND NUMBER OF DEPENDENTS |

## 5.2 Annual Employer Filings

| Form | Description | Due Date |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **Form EA** | Employee salary statement (individual) | Issued by end of February |
| **Form E** | Employer return (summary of all employees) | 31 March annually |
| **Form CP22A** | New employee notification | Within 30 days of hire |
| **Form CP21** | Employee leaving Malaysia | Before departure |

### Penalties for Non-Compliance
- Late Form E filing: Up to RM 400–RM 2,000
- Non-remittance of PCB: Penalty up to 45% of unpaid tax
- Directors personally liable for employer tax defaults

# Part 6: Foreign Worker Levy (FWL) & Special Contributions

## 6.1 Foreign Worker Levy (FWL) — by State/Sector

The FWL (levi pekerja asing) is paid by employers and varies by sector and state:

| Sector | Levy Rate (per worker/year) |
|--------|---------------------------|
| Manufacturing | RM 1,250 – RM 2,500 |
| Construction | RM 1,250 – RM 2,500 |
| Plantation | RM 750 – RM 1,500 |
| Agriculture | RM 750 – RM 1,500 |
| Services (restaurant, cleaning) | RM 1,850 – RM 3,500 |
| Domestic helpers (maids) | RM 868 – RM 1,200 |

> Note: Levy rates are revised periodically. Sabah/Sarawak may have different rates.

## 6.2 Foreign Worker EPF/SOCSO

| Contribution | Requirement |
|-------------|-------------|
| EPF (employer) | RM 5 per month (fixed) |
| EPF (employee) | Not required (opt-in) |
| SOCSO | Mandatory (same rates as local workers) |
| EIS | Not applicable for foreign workers |

| Obligation | Due Date | Authority | Penalty |

| EPF contribution | 15th of following month | EPF | 10% p.a. on arrears |

| Form CP22A (new hire) | Within 30 days | LHDN | Guidelines |
| Foreign Worker Levy | Varies (quarterly/annually) | Immigration/KDN | Revocation of permit |

# Part 8: Key Reminders

- **Total minimum employer cost per local employee**: EPF (12-13%) + SOCSO (2.25-4.50%) + EIS (0.20%) + HRDF (0.5-1.0%) ≈ **15-19% on top of salary**
- **Foreign worker costs differ**: EPF fixed RM 5/month, SOCSO same as local, EIS exempt, but includes FWL (RM 750–3,500/year)
- **Flexible Account (Account 3)** from 2024 — 10% of monthly contribution available for anytime withdrawal (min. RM 50)
- **2025 EPF dividend expected ~5.50-6.00%**
- **Late contributions** attract significant compounding penalties
- **Directors** can be held personally liable for employer tax defaults
- **Monthly PCB** must be deducted and remitted for employees exceeding RM 2,500 monthly income (after EPF deduction)
- **SST on payroll services**: Some payroll processing services subject to 8% service tax

> Source attribution: EPF Act 1991, SOCSO Act 1969, EIS Act 2017, HRDF/PSMB Act 2001, Income Tax Act 1967, Immigration Dept (FWL schedule), Budget 2026 updates
