# Malaysia Insurance & Takaful Guide
# 马来西亚保险与伊斯兰保险指南
# Source: ICLG Insurance & Reinsurance 2026, BNM (FSA 2013/IFSA 2013), PIDM, PIAM
# Last updated: February 2026

## Part 1: Regulatory Framework

### 1.1 Key Legislation
- Financial Services Act 2013 (FSA) — Primary law for conventional insurance
- Islamic Financial Services Act 2013 (IFSA) — Primary law for Takaful/Islamic insurance
- Companies Act 2016 — Corporate form requirements
- Malaysia Deposit Insurance Corporation Act 2011 — PIDM framework
- Road Transport Act 1987 — Motor insurance requirements
- Workmen's Compensation Act 1952 — Employer liability requirements

### 1.2 Regulator
Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM) — Authorised as the primary regulator for insurance and Takaful
Minister of Finance: Approves insurance licences on BNM recommendation

### 1.3 Industry Associations
- PIAM (General Insurance Association of Malaysia) — General insurance agents registration
- LIAM (Life Insurance Association of Malaysia) — Life insurance agents registration
- ISM (Insurance Services Malaysia) — Industry statistics and services
- MTA (Malaysian Takaful Association) — Takaful operators association

## Part 2: Types of Insurance

### 2.1 Life Insurance (Life Business)
- Individual life policies
- Group life insurance
- Investment-linked policies (ILP)
- Medical and health insurance
- Critical illness insurance

### 2.2 General Insurance (General Business)
Motor Insurance: 
- Third-party only, Third-party fire & theft, Comprehensive
- Largest general insurance class in Malaysia

Fire/Household Insurance:
- Fire, lightning, explosion, aircraft damage
- Residential and commercial property

Medical Insurance:
- Hospitalisation and surgical
- Outpatient care
- Medical card plans

Marine, Aviation & Transit (MAT):
- Cargo insurance, hull insurance, aviation insurance

Liability Insurance:
- Public liability, product liability, professional indemnity
- Employer liability (compulsory for foreign workers)

### 2.3 Reinsurance
- Professional reinsurers (e.g., Malaysian Re)
- Treaty and facultative reinsurance
- Cession requirements under FSA

## Part 3: Compulsory Insurance Requirements

### 3.1 Motor Third-Party Liability
- Legal basis: Road Transport Act 1987, Section 96
- ALL motor vehicles on Malaysian public roads must have at minimum third-party coverage
- Largest insurance class by premium volume
- Motor Insurance Pool (MIP): High-risk drivers and vehicles

### 3.2 Foreign Workers' Compensation
- Legal basis: Workmen's Compensation Act 1952
- ALL employers of foreign workers must purchase insurance
- Covers workplace injuries, death, and occupational diseases
- Separate from SOCSO (which covers Malaysian citizens/PRs)

### 3.3 Marine Liability
- Malaysia-registered ships must be insured by licensed Malaysian insurer
- Ships entering Malaysian waters must hold insurance certificate

### 3.4 Professional Indemnity
- Mandatory for specific professions:
  - Legal practitioners (all law firms and lawyers)
  - Medical practitioners (doctors and hospitals)
  - Architects, engineers, accountants (by respective professional bodies)

## Part 4: Takaful (Islamic Insurance)

### 4.1 Regulatory Framework
- Islamic Financial Services Act 2013 (IFSA) — separate regulatory framework
- Operates on Shariah principles: mutual cooperation, risk-sharing, no riba (interest) or gharar (uncertainty)
- Shariah Committee oversight for each Takaful operator

### 4.2 Types of Takaful

Family Takaful (Life equivalent):
- Individual family Takaful
- Investment-linked Takaful
- Medical and health Takaful
- Education Takaful

General Takaful (Non-life equivalent):
- Motor Takaful
- Fire Takaful
- Marine Takaful
- Travel Takaful

### 4.3 Takaful Operators (Selected)
- AIA PUBLIC Takaful Bhd
- Etiqa Takaful Bhd (Maybank group)
- Great Eastern Takaful Bhd
- Prudential BSN Takaful Bhd
- Sun Life Malaysia Takaful Bhd
- Syarikat Takaful Malaysia Keluarga Bhd
- Takaful Ikhlas Family Bhd

### 4.4 Market Position
- Malaysia ranks #1 globally on Islamic Finance Development Index (IFDI) 2024
- Islamic financing ~45.6% of total banking system loans/financing
- Takaful penetration rate growing but still below conventional insurance

## Part 5: Licensing & Market Entry

### 5.1 Licence Categories
- Life insurance business
- General insurance business
- Professional reinsurer
- Digital Insurance / Takaful Operator (DITO) — New category from Jan 2025

### 5.2 Corporate Form Requirements
- Insurance companies (excluding professional reinsurers): Must be public companies
- Professional reinsurers: May be established as subsidiaries of foreign institutions

### 5.3 Key Application Requirements (BNM Licensing Procedure)
- Financial strength and solvency
- Clear and comprehensive business plan
- Fit and proper key persons
- Risk management and internal control framework

### 5.4 Digital Insurance (DITO) Framework
- BNM opened DITO application: January 2, 2025
- Deadline: December 31, 2026
- Goal: Increase insurance penetration using digital capabilities
- Targeted at: Insurtech, foreign insurers, large conglomerates with digital infrastructure

## Part 6: Policyholder Protection (PIDM)

### 6.1 Coverage
- Perbadanan Insurans Deposit Malaysia (PIDM) — established under PIDM Act 2011
- Protects policyholders if an insurer member fails
- Coverage limits depend on type of policy
- All licensed insurers are PIDM members

### 6.2 Deposit Insurance
- PIDM also administers deposit insurance for banks
- Bank deposit coverage: Up to RM250,000 per depositor per bank
- Separation of deposit insurance and benefit protection systems

## Part 7: Key Industry Trends

### 7.1 Medical Cost Inflation
- Rising medical costs pressuring health insurance profitability
- Premium increases raising affordability concerns
- BNM addressing: Price transparency, Diagnosis-Related Groups (DRG) payment mechanism

### 7.2 Open Finance
- Consent-based data sharing reshaping insurance landscape
- Opportunities: Innovation, improved underwriting, personalised products
- Challenges: Data protection, cybersecurity, governance

### 7.3 Digital Transformation
- DITO licences driving industry digitisation
- Insurtech and digital distribution channels
- AI and big data in underwriting and claims
