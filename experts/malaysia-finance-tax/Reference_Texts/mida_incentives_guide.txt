# Malaysia Investment Incentives & MIDA Guide
# 马来西亚投资激励 & MIDA 指南
# Source: MIDA (Malaysian Investment Development Authority), LHDN
# Last updated: 2026

## Part 1: Overview

MIDA (Malaysian Investment Development Authority) is the principal agency under MITI for promoting and coordinating industrial development. MIDA approves manufacturing licenses under Act 139/1975 and processes tax incentive applications.

## Part 2: Key Tax Incentives

### 2.1 Pioneer Status
- Tax exemption on 70% of statutory income for 5 years
- High-tech/strategic projects: 100% exemption for 5-10 years
- Unabsorbed capital allowances and losses can be carried forward post-pioneer period
- Exempt income credited to Exempt Account
- Application is project-based, requiring alignment on factory location, capacity, and capex plan
- Available for manufacturing and selected high-value services

Eligibility:
- Promoted Activities or Products (PA/PP list)
- Minimum investment threshold varies by sector
- No Bumiputera shareholding requirement for manufacturing (100% foreign ownership permitted)

### 2.2 Investment Tax Allowance (ITA)
- Alternative to Pioneer Status (mutually exclusive for same activity/product)
- General: 60% allowance on qualifying capital expenditure (QCE) - buildings, plant, machinery
- High-tech/strategic: 100% ITA
- Offset against 70% of statutory income
- Unused balance carried forward to subsequent years
- Claim period: 5 years

### 2.3 Reinvestment Allowance (RA)
- For existing manufacturers expanding, modernizing, or diversifying
- 60% of QCE offset against 70% of statutory income
- Claimable for 15 consecutive years from first claim
- Company must have been in operation for at least 36 months

### 2.4 Additional Incentives

Green Technology (GITA/GITE):
- Green ITA: 100% on QCE (offset 70% of statutory income)
- Green Income Tax Exemption: 70% exemption for 5-10 years
- Eligible activities: Renewable energy, energy efficiency, green building, electric vehicles

Digital Economy:
- MSC Malaysia status: 100% tax exemption for 5-10 years
- PDE (Principal Digital Enterprise): Various incentives

Halal Industry:
- Pioneer Status or ITA for halal food production and halal logistics
- Additional incentives for halal parks and industrial zones

R&D Incentives:
- Double deduction on R&D expenditure
- Investment allowance for R&D activities (up to 100%)
- Accelerated Capital Allowance for R&D assets

Approved Service Projects (ASPs):
- Tax incentives for logistics, education, healthcare, tourism
- Varies by sector and investment value

## Part 3: Manufacturing License

### 3.1 Application Requirements
- Companies with shareholders' funds >= RM2.5M OR >= 75 full-time employees:
  Must obtain Manufacturing License under the Industrial Coordination Act 1975
- Below thresholds: Operating under exemption (but cannot apply for ITA/Pioneer)

### 3.2 Processing Time
- Manufacturing License: 4-8 weeks
- Pioneer Status / ITA: 3-6 months after submission

## Part 4: Sector-Specific Incentives

Electrical & Electronics (E&E):
- Pioneer Status for wafer fabrication, IC design, LED manufacturing
- ITA for supporting industries and component manufacturing

Biotechnology:
- BioNexus status: 100% tax exemption for 10 years
- ITA for biotech-related capital expenditure

Renewable Energy:
- Green Investment Tax Allowance (GITA)
- Feed-in Tariff (FiT) for renewable energy generation
- Net Energy Metering (NEM) scheme

## Part 5: Principal Hub Incentives
- Companies establishing regional/global principal hubs in Malaysia
- 0%-10% concessionary tax rate for 5 years (renewable)
- Must meet operational and expenditure thresholds

## Part 6: Application Process

1. Pre-application consultation with MIDA
2. Submit application with business plan, financial projections, and project details
3. MIDA evaluation (including property fundamentals: CF status, zoning, infrastructure)
4. Cabinet Committee on Investment approval (for large projects)
5. Issuance of incentive approval letter
6. Implementation and compliance reporting

Premium industrial zones (Selangor, Penang, Johor, Kulim) typically clear faster.
Heavy/specialized manufacturing requires zone alignment.
