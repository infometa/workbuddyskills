# Malaysia Companies Act 2016 - Financial & Audit Compliance Guide
# 马来西亚公司法 2016 - 财务与审计合规指南
# Source: SSM (Companies Commission of Malaysia), PwC, Amaze Advisory
# Last updated: 2024-12

## Part 1: Overview of Financial Compliance

All companies registered in Malaysia (Sdn Bhd and foreign companies) must meet specific regulatory obligations under the Companies Act 2016 (Act 777), administered by the Companies Commission of Malaysia (SSM).

Key regulatory bodies:
- SSM: Company registration, annual returns, corporate governance
- LHDN: Corporate tax, employer tax, withholding tax
- RMCD: SST, import/export duties
- EPF/SOCSO/EIS/HRDF: Employee statutory contributions

## Part 2: Annual Return (SSM)

### 2.1 Filing Deadline
- Within 30 days from the anniversary of incorporation each year
- Must include: registered office address, directors, shareholders, company secretary, paid-up capital, shareholding structure, beneficial owners

### 2.2 Penalties for Late Filing
- Late submission: RM50-RM500 late filing penalty
- Persistent non-compliance: Company may be struck off the register

## Part 3: Audited Financial Statements

### 3.1 Requirement
- All private limited companies (Sdn Bhd) must prepare and file financial statements
- Must comply with MFRS (Malaysian Financial Reporting Standards) or MPERS (Malaysian Private Entities Reporting Standard)
- Must be approved by board of directors before submission

### 3.2 Audit Exemption Criteria
Certain categories may qualify for audit exemption:
- Dormant companies
- Zero-revenue companies
- Small private companies meeting specific thresholds:
  - Annual revenue <= RM300,000
  - Total assets <= RM300,000
  - No more than 5 employees (or as specified by SSM)

### 3.3 Filing Deadline
- Within 6 months after the financial year-end
- Submitted to SSM together with the annual return

### 3.4 Penalties
- Failure to submit: RM5,000-RM50,000 fine
- Directors may face personal liability, fines, and disqualification

## Part 4: Corporate Tax Filing

### 4.1 Form C (Annual Tax Return)
- Deadline: 7 months from the financial year-end
- Must include tax computation and supporting schedules

### 4.2 Estimated Tax Payment (CP204)
- Required for companies with chargeable income exceeding RM20,000
- First CP204 must be submitted within 3 months from the start of the financial year
- Monthly installments based on estimated tax

### 4.3 Penalties for Late/Non-submission
- Penalty up to 45% of unpaid tax
- Late payment: 15% compounded penalty

## Part 5: Employer Obligations

Monthly payroll (PCB/EPF/SOCSO/EIS):
- PCB (monthly tax deduction): Due by 15th of following month
- EPF: Employee 11% + Employer 12-13%, due by 15th of following month
- SOCSO: Due by 15th of following month
- EIS: Due by 15th of following month

Annual employer filings:
- Form E (Employer Tax Return): Due by 31 March annually
- Form EA (Employee Salary Statement): Must be issued to employees by end of February

## Part 6: SST Filing
- Frequency: Bimonthly (every 2 months)
- SST-02 return must be submitted to RMCD
- Digital Service Providers: 8% DST if annual revenue exceeds RM500,000

## Part 7: Transfer Pricing Documentation
- Required for multinational companies and related party transactions
- Must maintain contemporaneous documentation
- Arm's length range: 37.5th to 62.5th percentile

## Part 8: Beneficial Ownership (BO) Reporting
- Companies must maintain an updated BO register
- Must submit BO information to SSM
- Ongoing updates required when changes occur

## Part 9: Directors' Responsibilities
- Prepare financial statements giving a true and fair view
- Ensure compliance with Companies Act 2016
- File annual returns and financial statements on time
- Maintain proper accounting records
- Personal liability for non-compliance
