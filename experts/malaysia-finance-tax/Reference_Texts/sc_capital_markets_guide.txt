# Malaysia Securities Commission & Capital Markets Guide
# 马来西亚证券委员会与资本市场指南
# Source: Securities Commission Malaysia (SC), Capital Markets and Services Act 2007 (CMSA)
# Last updated: 2026

## Part 1: Legal Framework

### 1.1 Key Legislation
- Capital Markets and Services Act 2007 (CMSA, Act 671) — Primary capital markets legislation
- Securities Industry Act 1983 (repealed, consolidated into CMSA)
- Futures Industry Act 1993 (repealed, consolidated into CMSA)
- Securities Commission Malaysia Act 1993 (SCA)
- Capital Markets and Services (Fees) Regulations 2025 — Effective 1 January 2026

### 1.2 Administering Authority
Securities Commission Malaysia (SC / Suruhanjaya Sekuriti)
- Established: 1 March 1993
- Website: www.sc.com.my
- Electronic Application System (EASy): Online licensing portal
- Capital Market Masterplan 3 (CMP3): Five-year plan for capital market development (2021-2026)

### 1.3 Market Size
- Capital market: RM4.2 trillion (2024)
- Fund management AUM: Exceeded RM1.1 trillion (2024)
- Fund management AUM grew from RM975.5 billion (2023)

## Part 2: SC Regulatory Functions

### 2.1 Core Functions
1. Regulate capital market activities and intermediaries
2. Supervise exchanges (Bursa Malaysia), clearing houses, and depositories
3. Approve prospectuses and corporate proposals
4. License capital market participants
5. Enforce securities laws and regulations
6. Promote investor education and protection

### 2.2 Regulated Activities Under CMSA 2007
- Dealing in securities
- Dealing in derivatives
- Fund management
- Investment advice
- Corporate finance advisory
- Custodian services
- Clearing and settlement services
- Securities lending and borrowing

## Part 3: Capital Markets Services Licence (CMSL)

### 3.1 Licence Types

Full-Fledged Fund Management Company:
- Minimum paid-up capital: RM2,000,000 (maintained at all times)
- Scope: Unlimited AUM for retail and sophisticated investors
- Requirements: Licensed director (10+ years experience), dedicated compliance officer
- Application fee: RM2,000

Boutique Portfolio Management Company:
- Minimum paid-up capital: RM500,000
- Scope: Max RM750 million AUM, sophisticated investors only, max 50 clients
- Requirements: Compliance function can be outsourced (SC approval needed)
- No licensed director mandatory

### 3.2 Licensing Process
1. Submit application via SC's EASy portal
2. Provide: Business plan, corporate profile, organizational structure, 5-year financial projections
3. Demonstrate directors/CEO/key management meet fit and proper criteria (CMSA S.64)
4. SC client charter: 6 weeks processing for complete applications
5. SC may conduct readiness audits or interviews

### 3.3 Fit and Proper Criteria
Assessment of: Probity, financial integrity, reputation, character, competence, capability to perform functions efficiently, honestly, and fairly.

### 3.4 Annual Licence Fees (From 1 Jan 2026, AUM-based)
- Equities: 0.0125% of AUM
- Bonds/mixed assets: 0.01% of AUM
- Money market/private mandates: 0.0025% of AUM
- Minimum annual fee: RM20,000

## Part 4: Digital Assets & Digital Investment Managers

### 4.1 Digital Assets as Securities
- Capital Markets & Services (Prescription of Securities) (Digital Currency and Digital Token) Order 2019
- Digital currencies and tokens classified as securities
- FMCs managing digital assets must use SC-registered Digital Asset Exchanges (DAX)

### 4.2 Registered DAX Platforms
- Luno
- HATA Digital
- MX Global
- SINEGY
- Tokenize

### 4.3 Digital Investment Manager (DIM / Robo-Advisor)
- Automated discretionary portfolio management using technology
- Not a separate licence category — variation to existing CMSL
- Examples: StashAway, Wahed, MYTHEO, Akru Now, Raiz Malaysia
- Must demonstrate digital value proposition, technology capabilities, expertise

## Part 5: Single-Family Office Scheme

### 5.1 Incentive Framework
- Introduced to attract private wealth and skilled talent
- Target: RM2 billion AUM by end of 2026
- As of late 2025: 6 family offices approved, nearly RM400 million AUM
- Single-Family Office Incentive Scheme gazetted in 2025

## Part 6: Capital Market Masterplan 3 (CMP3)

### 6.1 Strategic Goals (2021-2026)
1. Enhance market competitiveness and efficiency
2. Strengthen investor confidence and protection
3. Promote innovation and digital transformation
4. Support sustainable and responsible investment (SRI)
5. Deepen regional and international linkages

## Part 7: Compliance & Reporting

### 7.1 Ongoing Obligations for CMSL Holders
- Maintain minimum capital requirements
- File annual audited financial statements with SC
- Submit periodic regulatory returns
- Maintain proper risk management framework
- Comply with Guidelines on Conduct for Capital Market Intermediaries
- REPORTING OF SUSPICIOUS TRANSACTIONS (AML/CFT)

### 7.2 SC Enforcement Powers
- Suspend or revoke licence for non-compliance
- Impose fines and penalties
- Issue directions and remedial orders
- Investigate market misconduct (insider trading, market manipulation)
- Prosecute criminal offences under CMSA 2007

## Part 8: Sustainable and Responsible Investment (SRI)

### 8.1 SRI Framework
- SRI Sukuk — Malaysia pioneered SRI Sukuk framework
- Tax incentives for SRI issuers
- Mandatory sustainability reporting for listed issuers (Bursa)
- Sustainability taxonomy for financial sector (BNM/SC joint)

### 8.2 Key Initiatives
- ASEAN Sustainable and Responsible Fund Standards
- Mandatory climate-related disclosures (roadmap)
- Green, Social and Sustainable Bond/Sukuk framework
