# Part 1: New Incentive Framework (NIF)

Malaysia introduced the **New Incentive Framework (NIF)** replacing the previous sector-based incentive system. NIF is outcome-based, focusing on tiered incentives tied to quality contributions to the national economy.

## 1.1 Core Principle

NIF evaluates investment quality on **5 pillars** via the **NIA Scorecard**:

| Pillar | Description | Weighting |
|--------|-------------|-----------|
| 1. Economic Complexity | High value-add products, technology innovation | Highest weight |
| 2. High-Value Jobs | Skilled, high-income employment creation | High weight |
| 3. Domestic Linkages | Local supply chain, local procurement | Medium weight |
| 4. Inclusive Development | Regional and social equity | Medium weight |
| 5. Sustainable Practices | Environmental, ESG alignment | Medium weight |

### NIA Scorecard Outcomes

| Quality Level | Tier | Incentive Level |
|--------------|------|-----------------|
| High | Tier 1 | Best terms (lower rate, longer period) |
| Medium | Tier 1 | Standard terms |
| Medium | Tier 2 | Moderate terms |
| Baseline | Tier 2 | Minimum terms |

## 1.2 NIF Tax Incentive Types

### Special Tax Rate (STR)
| Quality Level | Rate | Period |
|--------------|------|--------|
| High | Reduced to 0-5% | Up to 15 years |
| Medium | Reduced to 5-10% | Up to 10 years |
| Baseline | Reduced to 10-15% | Up to 5 years |

### Investment Tax Allowance (ITA)
| Quality Level | Allowance | Cap |
|--------------|-----------|-----|
| High | 80-100% of QCE | Offset up to 100% of SI |
| Medium | 60-80% of QCE | Offset up to 70% of SI |
| Baseline | 40-60% of QCE | Offset up to 70% of SI |

# Part 2: Pioneer Status (PS)

## 2.1 Standard PS (Pre-NIF)

Available for promoted products/activities under the Promotion of Investments Act 1986:

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Tax exemption | 70% of statutory income for 5 years |
| Extension | Up to additional 5 years (for high-tech, strategic industries) |
| 100% exemption | Available for promoted areas (e.g., E&E, medical devices, aerospace) |
| Unabsorbed losses/capex | Can be carried forward post-exemption period |

## 2.2 NIF-Aligned PS

Under NIF, Pioneer Status converts to the Special Tax Rate mechanism, with rates determined by NIA Scorecard quality level (see Part 1.2).

# Part 3: Investment Tax Allowance (ITA)

| Aspect | Standard ITA | Enhanced ITA (Strategic) |
|--------|-------------|------------------------|
| Allowance | 60% of qualifying capital expenditure (QCE) | 80-100% of QCE |
| Offset | Up to 70% of statutory income | Up to 100% of SI |
| Period | 5 years | 10 years (strategic industries) |
| Unutilised allowance | Carry forward 7 years | Carry forward 10 years |

### Qualifying Capital Expenditure (QCE)
- Factory building (including renovation/extension)
- Plant and machinery
- Equipment for R&D
- Environmental protection equipment
- Automation/Industry 4.0 equipment
- ICT infrastructure (for digital manufacturing)

---

# Part 4: Reinvestment Allowance (RA)

Available to manufacturing companies that have been in operation for at least 3 years and are undertaking reinvestment.

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Allowance | 60% of qualifying capital expenditure |
| Offset | Up to 70% of statutory income |
| Period | Available for 15 consecutive years of assessment |
| Expansion | Additional 15 years for expansion projects |
| Qualifying | Additional production capacity, automation, modernisation, diversification |

# Part 5: Industry-Specific Guides

## 5.1 Electrical & Electronics (E&E)

Malaysia is a global E&E manufacturing hub (largest exporter sector).

### Key Incentives
| Segment | Incentive | Benefit |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| Wafer fabrication | PS: 100% exemption 10 years + 5 year extension | Among most generous incentives |
| IC design | MD Status / PS | 0-10% tax rate |
| Semiconductor assembly & test | PS: 70-100% exemption 5-10 years | Standard high-tech PS |
| LED/solar manufacturing | PS / ITA | 70-100% exemption |
| PCB manufacturing | PS / ITA | Standard manufacturing incentives |
| Data centre / cloud | MD Status + PS | 0-10% rate |

### E&E-Specific NIA Considerations
- High economic complexity (wafer/IC design): Likely High tier
- EMS (sub assembly): Likely Medium-Baseline tier
- R&D expenditure for new processes: Boosts NIA score

## 5.2 Automotive & EV

| Segment | Incentive | Duration |
|---------|-----------|----------|

| Charging equipment manufacturing | PS / ITA | 5 years |
| Next-generation vehicles (HEV/PHEV/FCEV) | PS: 70-100% | 5-10 years |
| R&D (EV technology) | Double deduction | Ongoing |

# Part 6: Manufacturing License Requirements

## 6.1 Manufacturing License (ML)

Under the Industrial Coordination Act 1975 (ICA):

| Threshold | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Shareholders' funds ≥ RM 2.5 million | Must apply for ML |
| Full-time employees ≥ 75 | Must apply for ML |
| Below both thresholds | ICA10 (Manufacturing License Exemption) sufficient |

### ML Application Process
1. Submit via MIDA online system (MIDA HQ)
2. Required documents: Company profile, project feasibility study, product list, equipment list, factory layout
3. Processing time: 4-8 weeks
4. Annual reporting: Quarterly production statistics to MIDA

## 6.2 ICA10 (ML Exemption)
- For companies with shareholders' funds ≤ RM 2.5 million AND full-time employees ≤ 75
- No quarterly reporting requirement
- Not eligible for certain MIDA tax incentives (unless NIF applies)

# Part 8: Manufacturing Subsector Tax Rates Summary

| Subsector | Typical Effective Tax Rate | Key Incentive |
|-----------|---------------------------|---------------|
| SME Manufacturer (< RM 50M revenue) | 15–17% | SME rate on first RM 150K + PS/ITA |
| Large Manufacturer (standard) | 24% | Reduced via PS/ITA if eligible |
| High-tech / Strategic manufacturer | 0-10% | NIF High tier or PS 100% |
| E&E (wafer/IC) | 0-5% | PS 100% + MD Status |
| EV manufacturing | 0-5% | PS 100% + EV-specific incentives |
| Aerospace | 0-10% | Strategic PS |
| Food manufacturing | 10-17% (SME) / 24% (large) | Standard PS/ITA |
| Halal certified manufacturer | 0-10% | Halal PS + additional deduction |

# Part 9: Key Reminders

- **NIF is the new norm** — outcome-based, tiered by NIA Scorecard quality assessment
- **High-tier NIF** can yield 0% rate for up to 15 years (better than old PS)
- **Manufacturing License (ML)** required if shareholders' funds ≥ RM 2.5M or employees ≥ 75
- **E&E, EV, aerospace, pharmaceuticals** are priority sectors with most generous incentives
- **Automation** incentives (accelerated CA, Smart Automation Grant) available for Industry 4.0
- **R&D double deduction** is evergreen (not restricted by PS/ITA period)
- **RA** available for 15 consecutive years for expansion/modernisation
- **Pillar Two (GMT)** affects large MNEs — effective tax rate must be ≥ 15% or QDMTT applies
- **Apply via MIDA** for all tax incentives — separate application from company incorporation
