# Malaysia Bursa Listing Rules & IPO Guide
# 马来西亚交易所上市规则与IPO指南
# Source: Bursa Malaysia Securities Berhad, Listing Requirements
# Last updated: 2025

## Part 1: Overview

### 1.1 Bursa Malaysia
- Malaysia's stock exchange: 1,070+ listed companies (May 2025)
- Three listing platforms: Main Market, ACE Market, LEAP Market
- 2024: 55 IPOs (highest in nearly two decades)
- 2025: ~60 IPOs (11 Main, 44 ACE, 5 LEAP)

### 1.2 Regulatory Framework
- Capital Markets and Services Act 2007 (CMSA)
- Bursa Malaysia Securities Berhad Listing Requirements (Main Market / ACE Market / LEAP Market)
- Administered by Bursa Malaysia and Securities Commission (SC)

## Part 2: Main Market (Premium Board)

### 2.1 Admission Tests

**Profit Test:**
- Aggregate after-tax profit: At least RM20 million over 3-5 financial years
- Most recent financial year: At least RM6 million after-tax profit
- Uninterrupted profit for 3-5 full financial years

**Market Capitalization Test:**
- Minimum RM500 million market capitalization upon listing
- Revenue in the most recent financial year

**Infrastructure Project Test:**
- For companies undertaking long-term infrastructure projects
- Concession lifespan: At least 15 years
- Project costs: Not less than RM500 million

### 2.2 Public Shareholding Spread
- Minimum 25% of total shares held by public shareholders
- At least 1,000 public shareholders
- Each holding not less than 100 shares

### 2.3 Management Requirements
- Continuity of same management for at least 3 full financial years before submission
- No ongoing sponsor required after listing

### 2.4 Investor Access
- Open to all investors (retail and institutional)
- Most prestigious board attracting local and international investors

## Part 3: ACE Market (Growth Board)

### 3.1 Admission Criteria
- No minimum profit or track record requirement
- Companies evaluated based on future growth potential
- Sponsor-driven model

### 3.2 Sponsor Requirement
- Must appoint an Approved Sponsor for listing process
- Sponsorship required for at least 3 financial years post-listing
- Sponsor who submitted application must act as sponsor for at least 1 full FY post-admission

### 3.3 Public Shareholding Spread
- Minimum 25% public shareholding
- At least 200 public shareholders
- Each holding not less than 100 shares

### 3.4 Management Requirements
- Continuity of same management for at least 3 full FY prior to submission (or since incorporation)
- Open to all investors

## Part 4: LEAP Market (SME Board)

### 4.1 Admission Criteria
- No profit record or track record required
- Designed specifically for Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs)
- Focus: Access to capital without heavy regulatory burden

### 4.2 Adviser Requirement
- Must appoint an Approved Adviser for listing
- Secure and maintain a Continuing Adviser for at least 3 years post-listing
- Adviser who submitted application must act for at least 1 FY post-admission

### 4.3 Public Shareholding Spread
- Minimum 10% of total ordinary shares at admission
- No strict minimum number of public shareholders (flexible)
- Higher flexibility as investors are sophisticated

### 4.4 Investor Restrictions
- Open only to Sophisticated Investors (as defined under CMSA 2007)
- High-net-worth individuals and institutions only
- General public cannot invest in LEAP Market

## Part 5: IPO Process

### 5.1 Key Steps
1. Pre-IPO Preparation (6-12 months):
   - Engage advisers, sponsors (ACE), or advisers (LEAP)
   - Financial audit and reporting
   - Corporate restructuring if needed
   - Prepare prospectus

2. Submission to SC / Bursa:
   - Submit listing application with prospectus
   - SC reviews for CMSA compliance
   - Bursa reviews for LR compliance

3. SC Approval:
   - Prospectus registration
   - Approval of listing

4. IPO Launch:
   - Book building (institutional)
   - Retail offering
   - Price determination

5. Listing:
   - Admission to official list
   - Commence trading

### 5.2 Typical Timeline
- Main Market: 6-12 months from start
- ACE Market: 4-8 months
- LEAP Market: 3-6 months

## Part 6: Continuing Obligations

### 6.1 Financial Reporting
- Annual audited financial statements within 4 months (Main) / 5 months (ACE/LEAP)
- Quarterly unaudited reports within 2 months of each quarter end
- Immediate disclosure of material information

### 6.2 Corporate Governance
- At least 2 independent directors (or 1/3 of board)
- Audit Committee, Nomination Committee, Remuneration Committee
- Internal audit function
- Related party transaction approvals

### 6.3 Disclosure Requirements
- Immediate announcement of material transactions
- Share buybacks and treasury shares
- Changes in directors, substantial shareholders
- Takeover and merger compliance (Malaysian Code on Take-Overs & Mergers)

### 6.4 Continuing Sponsorship (ACE)
- Sponsor for min 3 full FY post-listing
- Sponsor monitors compliance with LR
- Semi-annual sponsor report to Bursa

## Part 7: Market Statistics

### 7.1 IPO Activity (2024-2025)
- 2024: 55 IPOs across all markets
- 2025: ~60 IPOs
- Composition: Main Market (~11), ACE Market (~44), LEAP Market (~5)

### 7.2 Market Capitalization
- Capital market (incl. bonds): RM4.2 trillion (2024)
- Fund management AUM: >RM1.1 trillion (2024)

## Part 8: Special Considerations

### 8.1 Digital Assets Listed Companies
- Digital currency and token classified as securities under CMSA
- Must trade through SC-registered Digital Asset Exchanges

### 8.2 Sustainable Reporting
- Mandatory sustainability reporting for Main Market listed issuers
- Climate-related disclosures roadmap (phased implementation)

### 8.3 Foreign Companies
- Foreign-owned companies can list on Bursa Malaysia
- Same admission criteria apply
- Additional disclosure requirements for foreign jurisdiction risks
