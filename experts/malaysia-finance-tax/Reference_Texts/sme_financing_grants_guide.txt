# Part 2: Government Guarantee Schemes

## 2.1 GGSM2 (Government Guarantee Scheme for SMEs) — RM20 Billion

Enhanced flagship financing program:

### Key Features (2026 Update)
| Feature | Previous | New (2026) |
|---------|----------|------------|
| Guarantee coverage | Up to 70% | Up to 80% |
| Maximum loan | RM10 million | RM20 million per SME |
| Guarantee fee | 1.0% p.a. | 0.5% p.a. |
| Minimum operations | 2 years | 1 year |
| Application | Multi-step | Single submission to participating banks |

### Eligibility
- SSM registered business
- At least 1 year in operation
- Malaysian-owned (51% or more)
- Annual sales not exceeding RM50 million
- Not classified as impaired account by any bank
- Not in prohibited industries (gambling, weapons, etc.)

## 2.2 SJPP (Syarikat Jaminan Pembiayaan Perniagaan)

Umbrella body for government guarantees. Administers multiple schemes:
- **SJPP-GGSM2**: RM20 billion (above)
- **SJPP-Green**: For green technology projects
- **SJPP-Women**: For women-owned enterprises
- **SJPP-SME Digitalisation**: For digital transformation

Maximum guarantee fee: 0.5% p.a. (2026 rate)

# Part 3: Microfinancing Programs

## 3.1 TEKUN Nasional (RM2 Billion)

| Loan Amount | Interest Rate | Tenure | Processing Time |
|------------|--------------|--------|----------------|
| RM1,000 – RM100,000 | 4%–6% p.a. reducing | 6–60 months | 7 days (2026) |

### New in 2026
- Faster processing: 7 days (was 14 days)
- New digital application platform
- Mentorship programme included
- TEKUN Wanita: Special allocation for women entrepreneurs

### Eligibility
- Bumiputera entrepreneurs (majority programs)
- Non-Bumiputera programs also available via TEKUN Nasional (separate window)
- Business operational ≥ 6 months

## 3.2 Amanah Ikhtiar Malaysia (AIM) — RM1 Billion

| Loan Amount | Target Group | Feature |
|------------|-------------|---------|
| RM2,000 – RM100,000 | B40 and low-income entrepreneurs | Higher limits for proven borrowers |

- Focus: Women from low-income households
- Group-based lending model
- Business development support included

| BSN MyMicro Financing | RM50,000 | From 4% p.a. | Up to 5 years |

# Part 4: CGC (Credit Guarantee Corporation) Schemes

## 4.1 BNM-CGC Financing Plan (RM10 Billion)

Announced June 2026 — CGC to roll out RM10 billion in BNM-CGC financing plan:

| Coverage | Details |
|----------|---------|
| Guarantee | Up to 80-90% for micro enterprises |
| Loan amount | Up to RM5 million per SME |
| Target | MSMEs (especially micro and small) |
| Fee | Reduced guarantee fee (as low as 0.35% p.a.) |

## 4.2 CGC BizMula

- First-time entrepreneur financing
- Up to RM500,000
- Guarantee coverage: up to 90%
- No collateral required for loans ≤ RM50,000

## 4.3 CGC BizWanita

- For women-owned SMEs
- Up to RM1 million
- Guarantee coverage: up to 80%
- Lower documentation requirements

# Part 13: Key Reminders

- **Government funds operate first-come-first-served** — apply early when portals open
- **Documents to prepare**: SSM profile, IC of directors, 6-12 months bank statements, business plan, financial statements, invoices/contracts
- **Combined Applications**: SMEs can apply for multiple schemes (e.g., BNM fund + CGC guarantee + digital grant)
- **Digital grants typically exhaust fastest** — apply in Q1 when portals open
- **Track implementation**: Monitor SME Corp website, MOF announcements, MDEC portals
- **Register with agencies**: TEKUN, CGC, MDEC portals for early notification
- **Join SME associations** for early scheme information and application support

### Application Flow Summary
1. Determine business category (micro/small/medium/exporter/tech)
2. Identify matching schemes from this guide
3. Prepare standard documentation set
4. Apply via agency portal or participating bank
5. Follow up within 7-14 days
6. If rejected, consider CGC-guaranteed alternatives or microfinancing
