# Malaysia Regional Investment Incentives Guide
# 马来西亚区域投资激励指南
# Source: MIDA, Iskandar Regional Development Authority (IRDA), ECERDC, RECODA, SEDIA
# Last updated: 2025

## Part 1: Iskandar Malaysia (Johor)
- Special economic zone in Johor (2,217 sq km)
- Promoted Sectors: Creative industries, education (EduCity), healthcare (Medini), logistics, tourism, financial advisory
- Pioneer Status: 100% tax exemption for 5-10 years for qualifying promoted activities
- ITA: 100% on QCE for qualifying services and manufacturing
- Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone (JS-SEZ): Agreement signed Jan 2025
- Key incentive: 15% corporate tax rate (vs standard 24%) for companies in qualifying zones

## Part 2: ECER (East Coast Economic Region)
- Covers: Kelantan, Terengganu, Pahang, Mersing (Johor)
- Administered by: ECER Development Council (ECERDC)
- Priority Sectors: Tourism, oil & gas, petrochemicals, manufacturing, agriculture, education
- Pioneer Status eligibility for promoted activities
- ITA of 80% on QCE (vs standard 60%)
- Additional customs duty exemptions on imported machinery

## Part 3: SCORE (Sarawak Corridor of Renewable Energy)
- Covers: Central Sarawak (Mukah, Tanjung Manis)
- Administered by: RECODA
- Focus: Energy-intensive industries, renewable energy, timber, tourism
- 100% Pioneer Status exemption for promoted sectors
- Halal hub incentives in Tanjung Manis

## Part 4: SDC (Sabah Development Corridor)
- Covers: All of Sabah state
- Administered by: SEDIA
- Priority: Agriculture, tourism, manufacturing, logistics, oil & gas, biotechnology
- Enhanced Pioneer Status and ITA rates

## Part 5: NCER (Northern Corridor Economic Region)
- Covers: Perlis, Kedah, Penang, Northern Perak
- Focus: Electronics (Penang), agriculture, tourism, logistics
- Integrated agro-tourism packages, E&E supply chain incentives

## Part 6: Key Benefits Across Regions
- Enhanced tax exemption periods (5-10 years vs standard 5)
- Higher ITA rates (80-100% vs standard 60%)
- Customs duty exemptions on imported machinery and raw materials
- Streamlined approvals through single-window agencies
- State-level incentives: Land premium rebates, infrastructure support

## Part 7: Recent Developments (2025-2026)
- JS-SEZ with Singapore (Jan 2025): 15% corporate tax rate for qualifying companies
- Forest City Special Financial Zone: Concessionary tax rates for fintech and financial services
- Expansion of promoted activities list under MIDA
