# Part 1: Regulatory Architecture

Malaysia's fintech activities are regulated by two main authorities:

| Authority | Jurisdiction | Core Legislation |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM) | Banking, payments, insurance/takaful, money services | Financial Services Act 2013 (FSA), Islamic Financial Services Act 2013 (IFSA) |
| Securities Commission (SC) | Capital markets, P2P lending, equity crowdfunding, digital assets | Capital Markets and Services Act 2007 (CMSA) |

### BNM Strategic Direction
**Financial Sector Blueprint 2022-2026** — four key strategies:
1. Build critical digital infrastructure
2. Support vibrant digital financial services
3. Strengthen cybersecurity
4. Leverage technology for regulation (SupTech/RegTech)

### SC Digital Market Strategy
- Enhance financing access (digital fundraising)
- Increase investor participation
- Develop institutional market
- Build collaborative ecosystem
- SC's `aFINity` platform: Engagement window for fintech stakeholders

# Part 2: Digital Banking

## 2.1 Regulatory Framework

BNM introduced a digital banking licensing framework (2022). Five successful applicants awarded:

| Digital Bank | Consortium | Timeline | Focus Segment |
|-------------|-----------|----------|--------------|
| GX Bank (GXS) | Grab, Singtel, Kuok Group | Operational 2024+ | Mass market, micro-SMEs |
| Boost Bank | Boost (Axiata), RHB Bank | Operational 2024+ | Lifestyle, youth, gig economy |
| AEON Bank | AEON Credit Service, AEON Financial Service | Operational 2024+ | Retail, consumer finance |
| KAF Digital Bank | KAF Investment Bank, others | Under development | SME, wholesale |
| (Fifth license) | Consortium | Under development | Mixed segment |

### Key Regulatory Requirements
- **Transition period**: 3-5 years (phased-in compliance)
- **Phase 1** (first 3-5 years): Simplified regulatory requirements
- **Phase 2** (post-transition): Full FSA/IFSA compliance
- **Capital requirement**: RM100 million minimum (Phase 1), RM300 million (steady-state)
- **Must demonstrate**: Sustainable business model, risk management framework, operational readiness

## 2.2 Digital Insurers and Takaful Operators (DITO)

BNM's DITO framework (2024) — opening insurance/takaful to digital operators:

### DITO Framework Key Elements
| Aspect | Requirement |
|--------|------------|
| Capital | RM100 million minimum |
| Distribution | Fully digital (no agents/branches) |
| Products | Simplified, standardised products |
| Timeline | 3-5 year transition to full compliance |
| Application | Open window (no cap on licenses) |

### DITO Approved/Applied Operators
- Digital insurance platforms (multiple applications under review)
- Focus: Microinsurance, health, travel, motor (direct online)

# Part 3: Digital Payments & E-Money

## 3.1 Regulatory Framework (BNM)

- **Payment Systems Act (PSA) / FSA 2013**: Regulates payment systems and instruments
- **E-Money Guidelines**: BNM policy on electronic money issuance
- **Merchant Acquiring**: Regulated under FSA

### License Categories

| Category | Description | Capital | Examples |
|----------|------------|---------|----------|

| E-Money Issuer (Non-bank) | Stored value facility | RM3-5 million | Boost, Touch 'n Go |
| Payment Facilitator | Merchant aggregation | RM500,000 | Fave, MyDebit |
| Remittance Business | Cross-border money transfer | RM500,000 | InstaReM, MoneyMatch |

# Part 4: E-Money & Payment Innovations

## 4.1 Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) Regulation

- BNM oversight for bank-linked BNPL products
- **BNPL providers**: Atome, Grab PayLater, Shopee SPayLater, GeoPay (Boost)

# Part 5: P2P Lending & Equity Crowdfunding

## 5.1 P2P Lending (SC-Regulated)

| Framework | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Regulation** | CMSA 2007, SC Guidelines on Recognized Markets |
| **Operator license** | Recognized Market Operator (RMO) |
| **Investor limits** | Sophisticated investors: No limit; Retail investors: Max RM50,000 per platform |
| **Borrower limits** | Up to RM3 million per issuer, per platform |
| **Platforms (2026)** | Funding Societies, Capsphere, B2B FinPAL, PitchIN, Fundaztic, Ethis, etc. |

### Key Rules
- Operators must conduct credit assessment on borrowers
- Mandatory risk disclosure to investors
- Maximum 25% of funds from a single lender per note
- Fund custody via licensed trustee/nominee

### MyCIF (Malaysia Co-Investment Fund)
- Government co-investment fund under MOF
- Co-invests alongside retail investors on P2P/ECF platforms
- **Silver Economy Programme** (2026): Focus on MSMEs serving ageing population
- **Profit-sharing incentives** for VC/PE-led deals

## 5.2 Equity Crowdfunding (ECF)

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Regulator** | SC |
| **Max raise per issuer** | RM10 million (RM20 million for accredited investors) |
| **Investor limit** | Retail: RM5,000 per project per platform |
| **Platforms** | pitchIN, CrowdPlus, Ata Plus, etc. |
| **Secondary trading** | SC allows ECF shares to be traded on approved platforms |

### CoSIF (Strategic Co-Investment Fund)
- Joint MITI + SC initiative (Feb 2025)
- Initial allocation: RM131.5 million
- Supports SMEs and mid-tier companies in strategic sectors
- Via ECF and P2P platforms

## 5.3 Other Digital Investment Services

| Service | Regulation | Description |
|---------|-----------|-------------|
| Digital Investment Management (DIM) | SC (CMSA) | Digital robo-advisory, portfolio management |
| DIM licensees | Wahed Invest, StashAway, Akru | Automated wealth management |
| Digital Asset Management | SC | Tokenised fund management |
| Crowdfunding (donation/reward) | Unregulated (non-securities) | Project-based crowdfunding |

# Part 6: Digital Assets & Cryptocurrency

## 6.1 Digital Asset Exchange (DAX) Regulation

SC regulates digital asset exchanges under CMSA:

### Approved DAX Operators (2026)
- HATA Digital
- Luno Malaysia
- SINEGY
- Tokenize Technology
- MX Global
- (Sixth operator pending)

### Regulatory Requirements
| Requirement | Detail |
|------------|--------|
| Registration | Must register as Recognized Market Operator |
| Asset custody | Segregated client assets; cold wallet majority |
| KYC/AML | Full CDD for all customers |
| Reporting | Daily transaction reporting to SC |
| Safeguards | Insurance coverage, audit requirements |
| White-listing | Only SC-approved digital assets tradable |

### Recent Regulatory Updates (2025-2026)
- **Jun 2025**: SC consultation paper on DAX reform:
  - Relaxed listing for certain digital assets (no prior regulatory approval)
  - Enhanced customer asset protection and governance
  - Higher financial and operational standards
- **Feb 2026**: BNM's Digital Asset Innovation Hub (DAIH) launched three programmes testing ringgit stablecoin and tokenised deposits for wholesale payments

## 6.2 Tokenisation Framework

### SC — Tokenised Capital Market Products (May 2025)
- Consultation paper on tokenised equities, bonds, and funds
- Tokenised products treated as traditional CMS products (not digital assets)
- Clear distinction from digital tokens and cryptocurrencies

### BNM — Asset Tokenisation Discussion Paper (Oct 2025)
- Collaborative approach to asset tokenisation in Malaysian financial sector
- Focus: Wholesale, infrastructure, trade finance
- Distributed ledger technology (DLT) for settlement

## 6.3 Stablecoins

| Development | Date | Details |
|------------|------|---------|
| BNM Digital Asset Innovation Hub | Jun 2025 | Controlled environment for testing |
| Ringgit stablecoin pilots | Feb 2026 | Three programmes announced |
| Focus | Wholesale payments | Not retail-focused |
| Regulatory framework | Under development | Expected guidelines 2026-2027 |

# Part 8: Data Privacy & Cybersecurity

## 8.1 Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) 2010

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Regulator | Department of Personal Data Protection (JPDP) |
| Scope | Fintech companies collecting personal data in commercial transactions |
| Extra-territorial | Applies to data controllers outside Malaysia using equipment in Malaysia |
| Cross-border transfer | From 1 Apr 2025: Only to jurisdictions with substantially similar PDPA-equivalent laws |
| Representative | Non-Malaysian controllers must appoint a Malaysia representative |
| Penalty | RM10,000 – RM1,000,000 fine and/or up to 3 years imprisonment; directors personally liable |

### 7 Core Principles
1. **General principle**: Consent for collection/processing
2. **Notice and choice**: Inform purpose and obtain consent
3. **Disclosure**: Limits on disclosure to third parties
4. **Security**: Safeguard personal data
5. **Retention**: Not longer than necessary
6. **Data integrity**: Accurate, complete, not misleading
7. **Access**: Right to access and correct

## 8.2 Cybersecurity Act 2024 (CSA)

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Scope | National Critical Information Infrastructure (NCII) |
| NCII sectors | Banking & Finance, Information, Communication & Digital |
| Key obligations | Cyber risk assessment, incident reporting, audit, licensed service providers |
| Enforcement | Chief Executive appointed |

### BNM — Technology Risk Management Policy (Nov 2025)
- Minimum technology risk management requirements for financial institutions
- Covers: Cyber resilience, cloud outsourcing, third-party risk, AI governance

# Part 9: AI in Finance

## 9.1 BNM AI Discussion Paper (Aug 2025)

BNM's proposed approach to AI adoption in Malaysian financial sector:

- **Responsible AI**: Fairness, accountability, transparency, ethics
- **Governance**: Board-level oversight for AI models
- **Risk management**: Model validation, bias testing, explainability
- **Consumer outcomes**: AI must improve, not degrade, consumer outcomes

# Part 10: Key Reminders

- **Digital banking** is in Phase 1 transition — simplified rules until steady-state (expected 2028-2030)
- **DITO framework** opens insurance to fully digital operators — microinsurance focus
- **P2P and ECF** are growing rapidly with MyCIF and CoSIF co-investment
- **Digital assets** under significant regulatory evolution — stablecoin pilots, tokenisation framework
- **Cross-border payments** leadership continues via DuitNow QR and Project Nexus
- **PDPA cross-border restrictions** (Apr 2025) — data localisation requirements tightening
- **Cybersecurity Act 2024** — mandatory for NCII entities including financial institutions
- **Open Banking** developing with industry-led standards (no mandated open API requirement yet)
