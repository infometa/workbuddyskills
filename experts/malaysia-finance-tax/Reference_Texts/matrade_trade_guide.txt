# Malaysia MATRADE & Export Promotion Guide

# Source: MATRADE (Malaysia External Trade Development Corporation)
# Last updated: 2025

## Part 1: MATRADE Overview
MATRADE is Malaysia's external trade development and promotion agency, established in 1993. Its mission is to promote Malaysia's exports globally.

## Part 2: Market Development Grant (MDG)
Purpose: Assist Malaysian SMEs, professional service providers, trade associations in increasing global sales.
Grant amount: Up to RM200,000 per company (reimbursable grant)
Eligibility for SMEs:
  Manufacturing: Annual sales <=RM50M OR <=200 employees
  Trading: Annual sales <=RM20M OR <=75 employees
  Services: Annual sales <=RM20M OR <=75 employees (excl. real estate, tourism, finance, insurance)
  At least 60% Malaysian equity ownership

Claimable Activities:
- International trade fairs and exhibitions
- Trade missions and business matching
- Product development and adaptation for export
- Advertising and promotion abroad
- Export market research
- Quality certification and accreditation
- Intellectual property registration abroad
- E-commerce platform participation

Claim rates: 50%-70% of eligible costs

## Part 3: Other MATRADE Services
- MyExport: Digital platform for trade facilitation
- MADANI Digital Trade (MDT): Centralised service platform
- Trade Information Centre (TIC): Market intelligence and research
- Export Advisory Services: One-on-one export coaching
- International Trade Fairs: MATRADE organises participation in major trade fairs
- Trade Missions: Outbound and inbound trade missions
- Buyer Matchmaking: Connecting Malaysian exporters with international buyers

## Part 4: Related Agencies
- MIDA: Investment promotion and manufacturing licences
- EXIM Bank: Trade financing and export credit
- SME Corp: SME development and coordination
- Malaysia Productivity Corporation (MPC): Productivity enhancement
- Halal Development Corporation (HDC): Halal industry development
