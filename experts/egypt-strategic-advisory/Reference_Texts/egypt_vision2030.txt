# Egypt Vision 2030
# Source: State Information Service (sis.gov.eg)
# Retrieved: 2026-07-09

## Executive Summary

Egypt Vision 2030 is a national long-term strategic agenda launched in February 2016. It aims to achieve the principles and goals of sustainable development across all fields and localize them within Egyptian national institutions. The Vision is based on two guiding principles:
- Inclusive Sustainable Development
- Balanced Regional Development

It integrates the three dimensions of sustainable development: economic, social, and environmental. The Vision was updated in early 2018 to adapt to changing local, regional, and global contexts. The ultimate goal is to improve the quality of life and living standards for Egyptian citizens.

## Core Principles

1. Inclusive Sustainable Development
2. Balanced Regional Development
3. Economic, social, and environmental dimensions integration
4. Quality of life improvement
5. Social inclusion and participation
6. High, inclusive, and sustainable economic growth
7. Investment in people through knowledge, innovation, and scientific research
8. Climate change resilience and sustainable ecosystems
9. Governance modernization, transparency, and anti-corruption
10. Peace, security, and regional/global leadership

## Eight Strategic Goals and Priority Areas

### Goal 1: Improve Quality of Life and Living Standards
- Reduce all forms of poverty and eliminate hunger
- Provide comprehensive social protection system
- Improve accessibility, quality, and competitiveness of education
- Improve accessibility and quality of healthcare
- Improve basic services (water, electricity, transport, housing)
- Enrich cultural life
- Develop digital infrastructure

Key initiatives:
- "Decent Life" (Haya Karima) initiative for poverty reduction and job creation
- "Takaful and Karama" conditional cash transfer program
- Digital transformation in education (e-exams, e-libraries, Egypt Knowledge Bank, remote learning)
- "Egypt Health" mobile application and "Egypt Safe" online medical consultation
- Virtual museum tours and online cultural activities
- Internet speed and capacity upgrades

### Goal 2: Justice, Social Inclusion, and Participation
- Achieve equal rights and opportunities
- Achieve spatial justice and reduce regional development gaps
- Empower women, youth, and most vulnerable groups
- Support community development participation
- Strengthen belonging and Egyptian identity
- Promote digital inclusion

Key initiatives:
- "Wasel" application for hearing-impaired COVID-19 consultations
- "We Hear You" initiative for citizen complaints
- "Egyptian People Foundation" supporting health sector
- "Your Country is Behind You" campaign supporting Egyptians abroad

### Goal 3: Competitive and Diversified Economy
- Achieve high, inclusive, and sustainable economic growth
- Improve economic flexibility and competitiveness
- Increase employment and decent work opportunities
- Improve business environment and entrepreneurship culture
- Achieve financial inclusion
- Integrate environmental and social dimensions into economic development
- Achieve fiscal sustainability
- Transition to digital, sustainable, and knowledge-based economy

Key initiatives:
- Egypt Economic Reform Program
- 300 basis points interest rate reduction
- Preferential lending for private industry, tourism, and real estate
- "Egypt's Sons" and "Annual Platinum Certificates" for social inclusion
- Free e-wallets and prepaid cards
- Supporting entrepreneurs and startups

### Goal 4: Knowledge, Innovation, and Scientific Research
- Invest in people and build creative capacities
- Stimulate innovation and spread innovation culture
- Strengthen links between education, scientific research, and development

Key initiatives:
- "Apply Your Idea" initiative for technology solutions
- First Virtual Egypt Hackathon
- COVID-19 research teams and emergency research calls

### Goal 5: Integrated Sustainable Ecosystem
- Address climate change impacts
- Enhance ecosystem adaptability
- Increase resilience to natural risks and disasters
- Increase reliance on renewable energy
- Protect nature, resources, and biodiversity
- Adopt sustainable consumption and production patterns
- Utilize natural resources efficiently

Key initiatives:
- Large seawater desalination projects
- Energy subsidy rationalization
- Renewable energy projects with domestic and foreign investment
- "Get Ready Green, Get Ready for the Future" environmental awareness campaign

### Goal 6: Governance of State Institutions and Society
- Administrative reform to improve efficiency and effectiveness
- Establish transparency and combat corruption
- Support monitoring, evaluation, follow-up, and data availability systems
- Strengthen partnerships among all development stakeholders
- Strengthen accountability and rule of law
- Empower local administration

Key initiatives:
- Government services digitalization and automation
- Public sector wage increases
- Partnerships with private sector to protect workers from layoffs

### Goal 7: Peace and Security
- Ensure sustainable food, water, and energy security
- Ensure political, economic, social, and environmental stability
- Ensure cybersecurity
- Secure Egyptian borders and combat terrorism and organized crime

Key initiatives:
- Government financing for local wheat purchases
- Daily cash withdrawal/deposit limits
- Fast financing for affected micro-projects
- Loan repayment deferrals
- Capital market support (stamp tax reductions, capital gains tax adjustments)
- Tourism support (property tax exemptions, credit facilities, utility bill deferrals)

### Goal 8: Strengthen Egypt's Leadership
- Strengthen Egypt's regional and international position
- Strengthen regional and international partnerships

Key initiatives:
- Medical aid and supplies to Italy, China, the United States, and others
- Partnership with Facebook for COVID-19 awareness dissemination

## Implementation Mechanisms

1. Dynamic Updates: The Vision is not static. It was revised in early 2018 to respond to changing local, regional, and global environments and to align with the UN 2030 Agenda.
2. Participatory Planning: The update process involved ministries, private sector, civil society organizations, and consultations with top experts across fields.
3. Strategic Implementation: The Vision consists of sector-specific strategies that form a comprehensive and coherent framework.
4. National Agenda Transformation: Egypt is transitioning from strategies with executive nature to a National Sustainable Development Agenda that is inclusive, flexible, and can adjust implementation priorities while maintaining long-term goals.
5. Monitoring and Evaluation: Emphasis on monitoring, evaluation, follow-up, and data availability systems to ensure governance and accountability.

## Key Takeaways for Investors

- Egypt Vision 2030 provides a multi-level, dynamic national transformation blueprint.
- It sets ambitious long-term goals supported by eight strategic pillars with specific sub-goals and actionable initiatives.
- Priority sectors include renewable energy, digital transformation, manufacturing, tourism, agriculture, and infrastructure.
- The Vision emphasizes improving the business environment, financial inclusion, and private sector partnership.
- Investors should monitor sector-specific strategies and policy windows aligned with Vision 2030 priorities.
