## Overview

The Suez Canal Economic Zone (SCZone) is the General Authority for Suez Canal Economic Zone, established as a world-class free zone and trade hub along the banks of the expanded Suez Canal. It is strategically located on the main trade route between Europe and Asia, with 8% of world trade passing through the Suez Canal.

## Investment Advantages

### One-Stop-Shop Services
- Company incorporation: SCZone官方宣传称48小时，实际全流程约2-6周
  - 基础公司注册（名称预留+提交章程）可在数日内完成
  - 全部流程（含文件认证、资本注入、商业注册、税务登记）通常需要2-4周（宣传口径）至3-6周（实务口径）
  - 耗时差异主要取决于：股东文件公证/认证链长度、活动代码审批、地址核实
  - ⚠️ 来源：Ahmed Moussa律所实务指南 + DLA Piper SCZone投资指南2026
