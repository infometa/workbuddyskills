# Egypt Industry Analysis and Sector Outlook
# Source: CNBC Africa / SIM.films Egypt Documentary 2025
# Retrieved: 2026-07-09

## Macroeconomic Context

- Population: 115 million
- GDP: $380 billion
- Africa's second-largest economy
- Predicted GDP growth: 4.7% in 2025-2026 (above IMF's previous 4.1% forecast)
- FDI target: 15% growth for 2024-2025
- 60% of population under 30 years old
- SMEs employ 72% of population

## Key Sectors

### 1. Construction and Infrastructure
- Annual growth rate: 7.4%
- Contracting business projected to reach $75 billion by 2029
- Major projects:
  - New Alamein City
  - High-speed rail and urban railway networks
  - Critical seaport and road infrastructure
  - New Administrative Capital ($45 billion, 700 km²)
    - Hosts government ministries
    - Business district
    - Iconic Tower (nearly 400 meters tall)
- Government has invested close to $550 billion over past ten years in infrastructure
- 7,000 km of roads in new cities

### 2. Real Estate
- Expected to grow by $30 billion by 2028
- Growth drivers: growing population, urbanization, demand for quality housing
- Key areas: Greater Cairo, North Coast, Red Sea
- Egypt Vision 2030 goal: increase housing footprint from 7% to 14% of Egyptian land
- Growth across 27 governorates with new cities
- Smart, sustainable cities and mixed-use spaces

### 3. Energy
- Sixth-largest oil reserves in Africa
- Self-sufficient in traditional power generation
- Oil refining capacity: 38 million tons annually
- Over 57 partners in upstream production and exploration
- Significant energy exporter to Africa and Europe
- Renewable energy expansion target: 45,000 megawatts from projects under construction
- Target: 42% of energy from renewable sources by 2030
- Wind power target: 14% by 2035
- Benban Solar Park: 1,600 MW, largest in Africa
- Green hydrogen production emerging as priority
- Local companies expanding wind and solar projects across Africa (Senegal, South Africa)

### 4. ICT and Digital Economy
- Digital Egypt strategy key component of Vision 2030
- Focus: artificial intelligence, digital innovation, professional upskilling, e-services
- Tech sector growth: 16.8% year-over-year
- Rapid rise of e-payments supported by public and private sectors
- Egypt positioning as regional hub for digital payments and fintech

### 5. Manufacturing
- Projected growth: 3.15% during 2024-2029
- Goal: triple exports over next five years
- Key industries: textiles, chemicals, machinery, food products
- Markets: Europe, Africa, MENA
- Export target: surpass $145 billion by 2030
- Textiles historically and strategically important, labor-intensive

### 6. Tourism
- 2024: 15.7 million tourists (record for second consecutive year)
- North Coast drew 104 nationalities in Summer 2024
- Red Sea hubs: Sharm El-Sheikh, Sahl Hasheesh, Hurghada
- Target: 30 million tourists by 2030
- Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM): partial open, official inauguration 2025
- Area around Pyramids undergoing major renovations

### 7. Agriculture
- Land reclamation projects
- Modern irrigation methods required (flood irrigation prohibited)
- Fish farming (aquaculture)
- Large agricultural and mining projects under development

### 8. Mining and Natural Resources
- Petroleum and mineral resources sector active
- Natural salt pans and rock salt activities
- Large mining projects part of national development plan

## Comparative Advantages

1. Strategic location between Europe, Asia, and Africa
2. Large domestic market (115 million consumers)
3. Young, growing workforce (60% under 30)
4. Skilled labor in manufacturing and textiles
5. Rich energy resources (oil, gas, renewables potential)
6. World-class tourism assets
7. Suez Canal logistics advantage
8. Extensive free trade agreement network

## Sector-Specific Investment Themes

- Infrastructure and construction: mega projects and urban expansion
- Energy transition: renewables, green hydrogen, LNG
- Manufacturing export platforms: textiles, chemicals, automotive components
- Technology and fintech: digital payments, e-services, AI
- Tourism and hospitality: cultural tourism, Red Sea resorts, medical tourism
- Agribusiness: land reclamation, aquaculture, food processing
- Logistics: Suez Canal corridor, ports, free zones

## Government Strategy Alignment

- All reforms linked to Egypt Vision 2030 agenda
- Coherent spending to attract private investment
- Focus on making economy more competitive and export-oriented
- Measures for young firms, startups, entrepreneurs, professionals
- Simple integrated tax systems with incentives
- Building trust with investors
