## Chinese Investment in Egypt

### Scale
- Over 2,800 Chinese companies operating in Egypt (as of 2025)
- Total Chinese investment exceeds $8 billion
- More than 2,000 Chinese companies operated in Egypt as of October 2024

### Key Chinese Companies
- **Huawei**: Leading global ICT infrastructure and smart devices provider
- **Haier**: Home appliances and consumer electronics
- **Midea**: Home appliances
- **ZTE**: Telecommunications equipment and networks
- **OPPO**: Smartphones and consumer electronics
- **GAC Motor**: Automotive; $300 million car assembly plant
- **Shanfeng**: Among first beneficiaries of Egypt's "golden license"
- **China Energy**: Managing 12 projects in Egypt worth $2 billion; focusing on renewable energy, green hydrogen, and ammonia; relocating North African headquarters to Egypt

### Major Chinese Projects (September 2024 FOCAC Agreements)
Total agreements: over $1.1 billion, focused on SCZone and strategic locations:

1. **Befar Group** - $500 million chlor-alkali production facility (Egypt's first green chemical plant) using wind, solar, natural gas
2. **China Glass Holding** - $300 million glass factory producing float glass and super-white galvanized glass; 800 tons/day output; expected annual exports up to $120 million
3. **Shandong Tianyi Chemical** - $110 million bromine extraction complex using seawater desalination byproducts
4. **Elite Solar** - $100 million N-type solar cells factory; 2 GW capacity; 600 jobs
5. **Kaks Investment** - $50 million home appliance supply chain support hub
6. **Dahui Glucose and Tiba Starch** - $7.5 million modified starch production facility; 20k tons annual first phase, scaling to 50k tons

## Main Sectors Attracting Foreign Investment

1. **Energy**: Oil and gas traditional focus; increasing renewable energy interest (solar, wind)
2. **Telecommunications**: Large telecom market in Africa and Middle East; digital services growth
3. **Real Estate and Construction**: New cities and large infrastructure projects
4. **Manufacturing**: Automobiles, textiles, food processing
5. **Financial Services**: Banks, insurance, investment funds, fintech
6. **Information Technology**: Software development and digital services
7. **Tourism**: Cultural and historical attractions; long-term attractor
8. **Agriculture**: Food security and agri-business

## Competitive Dynamics

### Strengths Attracting MNCs
- Large domestic market (115 million population)
- Strategic location connecting Africa, Europe, Asia
- Suez Canal logistics advantage
- Young, growing workforce
- Extensive free trade agreement network
- Government incentives and golden licenses
- Improving business climate

### Competitive Intensity
- Strong presence of European, American, Gulf, and Asian multinationals
- Chinese companies rapidly expanding, especially in manufacturing and technology
- Gulf sovereign wealth funds making large strategic investments (e.g., ADQ Ras El Hekma)
- Local conglomerates with strong market positions in traditional sectors

### Implications for New Entrants
- Manufacturing and export-oriented investments face competition but benefit from FTAs and low-cost labor
- Technology and digital services sectors growing rapidly with strong MNC presence
- Energy sector requires partnerships given dominance of international oil companies and state entities
- Real estate and construction opportunities tied to mega projects and urbanization
- Tourism and hospitality recovering with established global brands

## Key Government Engagement
- GAFI targets automotive, electronics, AI, textiles, renewable energy
- Golden license fast-track for strategic ventures
- China-Egypt Investment Forum and China-Africa Investment Forum strengthen bilateral ties
- Egypt's participation in international forums to attract FDI
