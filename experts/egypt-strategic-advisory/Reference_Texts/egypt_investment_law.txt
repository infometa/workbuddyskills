# Egypt Investment Law 72 of 2017: Guarantees, Incentives & GAFI Role
# Source: Consortio Law Firm + GAFI
# Retrieved: 2026-07-09

## Overview

The Egyptian Investment Law (Law No. 72 of 2017) serves as the foundational legal framework governing both local and foreign investment in the Arab Republic of Egypt, regardless of the size of the venture. This legislation provides clarity on guarantees, protections, and general incentives available when entering the market.

Investment projects under the law span a wide range of sectors, including industry, agriculture, commerce, education, health, transport, tourism, housing, construction, sports, electricity, energy, natural resources, water, communications, and technology. The Minister competent for investment affairs may add other sectors based on the national development plan.

## I. Detailed Scope of Eligible Investment Activities

### 1. Industrial Sector
- Manufacturing & Assembly: Transformation of raw materials, mixing, treating, shaping, packaging, and assembly of components to produce intermediate or final products
  - Exclusion: Does not include manufacturing of tobacco, snuff, and alcoholic beverages/liquor
- Industrial Engineering & Design: Design or manufacture of industrial machinery and production lines; factory execution or restructuring; engineering designs, mold/prototype creation, and facility management
- Cinema Industry: Establishing, renting, or operating studios, production labs, or cinemas
- Industrial Zone Development: Integrated development, marketing, or management of industrial zones; economic planning, infrastructure creation, marketing land to investors, building ready-made factories, and facility maintenance

### 2. Agriculture and Animal Production Sector
- Land Reclamation: Reclaiming barren or desert land and equipping it with basic utilities for cultivation
  - Condition: Modern irrigation methods must be used (flood irrigation is prohibited)
- Animal, Poultry, and Fish Production: Breeding animals for dairy, fattening, or meat; poultry farming; horse breeding; fish farming (aquaculture)
- Genetic Engineering: Projects focused on plant and animal genetic engineering

### 3. Trade Sector
- Internal Trade Development: Commercial centers (malls), wholesale trade, retail trade, and supply chains
  - Condition: Must take the form of an Egyptian Joint Stock Company, except for projects in remote areas or new urban communities

### 4. Education Sector
- Establishment, management, or operation of schools, technical education institutes, and universities

### 5. Health Sector
- Hospitals and Medical Centers: Specialized, integrated, or general hospitals, and diagnostic or therapeutic medical centers
  - Condition: Must offer 10% of occupied beds (hospitals) or cases (centers) annually for free

### 6. Transportation Sector
- Mass Transit: Transport within and between cities/new communities
  - Conditions: Minimum 300-seat capacity; new vehicles only; natural gas vehicles (diesel prohibited); garages/maintenance workshops; adherence to routes and schedules
- River, Maritime, and Coastal Transport: Transport of passengers, goods, or containers via river or sea using vessels under Egyptian flag
- Air Transport: Transport of passengers and goods; establishment, management, and maintenance of airports and landing strips
- Land & Cold Chain Transport: Trucking of goods (including cross-border and rail), refrigerated transport/storage for agricultural and industrial products (including grain silos)

### 7. Tourism Sector
- Accommodation: Hotels (fixed and floating), safari yachts, motels, apartment hotels, and tourist villages
  - Condition: Minimum 3-star rating (exceptions for New Valley and specific promising areas). Sales of units must not exceed 50% of total built-up accommodation area
- Tourism Management: Marketing and management of hotels and tourist resorts
- Nile Marinas: Integrated Nile marinas (minimum capacity of 24 floating hotels)
- Recreational Tourism: Yacht marinas, golf courses, and diving centers
- Medical & Eco-Tourism: Medical treatments, eco-lodges, bird-watching sites

### 8. Housing and Construction Sector
- Rental Housing: Housing projects fully leased for non-administrative residential purposes (minimum 50 units)
- Social Housing: Projects aimed at low-income groups
- Real Estate Development: Investment in new cities, remote areas, and areas outside the "Old Valley"
- Infrastructure: Water, sewage, electricity, roads, communications, multi-story garages, and metro lines; B.O.T projects for garages and parking meters
- Urban Planning: Development of industrial zones and new urban communities

### 9. Sports Sector
- All services through sports field, including management, marketing, operation of games, or establishing private clubs, academies, or health/fitness centers
  - Condition: Must take the form of a Joint Stock Company

### 10. Electricity and Energy Sector
- Design, construction, production, management, operation, or maintenance of power generation stations and distribution networks

### 11. Petroleum and Natural Resources Sector
- Support Services: Drilling, exploration support, well maintenance, civil works related to drilling
- Natural Gas: Gas reception stations, distribution networks to cities, and piping (excluding oil transport)
- Salts: Natural/industrial salt pans or rock salt activities

### 12. Water Sector
- Desalination, refining, distribution, transmission networks, and water recycling/treatment stations

### 13. ICT Sector
- IT Industry: Electronics design/manufacturing, data centers, outsourcing, software development, technological education
- Software & Content: Design and production of applications, databases, and electronic content
- Hardware Design: Computer system specification and embedded systems development
- Infrastructure: Design, execution, and management of data transmission networks
- Telecommunications: Internet services, mobile networks, and satellite stations (excluding Radio & TV broadcasting)
- Innovation: Projects investing in Intellectual Property, tech incubators, digitization of scientific/cultural content

## II. Core Guarantees and Investor Protection

Law 72/2017 establishes several non-negotiable protections:

### A) Non-Discrimination and Contract Enforcement
- Invested capital and projects are not subject to arbitrary or discriminatory measures
- State explicitly commits to respecting and enforcing contracts it concludes
- Exception: Projects established through fraud, deception, or corruption are not entitled to guarantees

### B) Protection Against Administrative Seizure
- Investment projects cannot be placed under receivership through administrative means; only via final judicial ruling
- Seizure, confiscation, or freezing of project funds is forbidden except under judicial order or final ruling
- Exception: Tax debts and Employment Law contributions owed to the State may be collected by all types of seizure, provided this does not violate State/investor contracts

### C) Regulatory Stability
- No administrative body may issue general regulatory decisions imposing new financial burdens or procedural requirements on projects under this law without consulting GAFI Board of Directors and obtaining Council of Ministers approval

### D) Financial Repatriation and Residency
- Non-Egyptian investors granted residency in Egypt for the entire duration of the project
- Foreign employees have the right to transfer all or part of their financial entitlements abroad

## III. General Fiscal Benefits (Waivers)

### Five-Year Exemption
- Contracts for incorporation and establishment of companies and enterprises
- Contracts for credit facilities and mortgages related to their activities
- Exempt from stamp duty and documentation/registration fees for five years from Commercial Register registration

### Land Registration
- Contracts for registration of land necessary for establishment of companies and enterprises are exempt from the aforementioned taxes and fees

### Certification Fees Cap
- Signature authentication fee for partners/representatives on incorporation contracts fixed at 0.25% of paid-in capital
- Capped at maximum EGP 10,000 (or equivalent in foreign currency)

## IV. Flexibility in Capital Structure and Currency

### Foreign Currency Capital
- Companies may determine their capital in any convertible currency
- Permitted to prepare and publish financial statements in that currency
- Contingent upon capital subscription having been made in the same foreign currency

### Currency Conversion
- Permitted to change currency denomination of company's capital from Egyptian Pound to any convertible currency
- Based on exchange rate announced by Central Bank of Egypt on date of conversion

## V. Simplified Regulatory Interface: The Role of GAFI

The General Authority for Investment and Free Zones (GAFI) is the competent administrative authority, managing the regulatory relationship with investors primarily through the Investor Service Center (ISC).

### Streamlined Services
- ISC receives all investor applications for licenses, approvals, land allocation, and permits
- ISC rules on applications according to legally stipulated deadlines

### Delegated Authority
- Representatives of specialized governmental bodies within the ISC possess authority to issue necessary approvals, permits, and licenses
- This power is transferred from their original administrative body to the ISC platform to expedite the investor journey

## VI. Additional GAFI Notes

### Investment Law No. 8 of 1997
- Combined incentives, customs exemptions, and investor protections
- Replaced Law 230 of 1989

### Law 72 of 2017
- Current foundational investment law
- Provides guarantees, protections, and general incentives
