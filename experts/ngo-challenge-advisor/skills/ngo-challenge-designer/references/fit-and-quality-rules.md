# Fit and quality rules

## WorkBuddy fit

A challenge fits when its core can reasonably use at least one of these capabilities:
- document or report drafting;
- structured data organization or analysis;
- knowledge retrieval or question answering;
- content assistance;
- repeatable workflow assistance or automation.

## Scope adaptation

Do not claim that WorkBuddy can replace:
- offline staff capacity;
- hardware installation or physical delivery;
- medical, legal, safeguarding, or professional judgment;
- final decisions with material impact on people;
- purely interpersonal conflict resolution.

When such a problem appears, ask whether a supporting subproblem can become the challenge:
- organize materials;
- prepare a first draft;
- retrieve approved knowledge;
- create checklists;
- track a repeatable follow-up process;
- highlight items requiring human review.

If no suitable subproblem exists, set `fit_status: fail` and do not offer publication confirmation.

## Publication gates

Require all of the following:
1. One primary track is confirmed.
2. One primary pain point is confirmed.
3. The current handling method and practical impact are understandable.
4. The desired outcome is expressed as a work result, not a mandated technology.
5. At least one observable success criterion is confirmed.
6. Materials, privacy boundaries, and human review requirements are stated.
7. WorkBuddy fit is `pass`.
8. The NGO has reviewed the final brief.
9. The NGO has explicitly selected `確認提交審批`. This submits the brief for platform review only; it does not directly publish it.

## Evidence rules

- Do not invent numbers, frequency, team size, tools, privacy rules, or examples.
- Label generated choices as possibilities until selected.
- Accept qualitative but observable success criteria.
- Keep the published brief faithful to confirmed information.
