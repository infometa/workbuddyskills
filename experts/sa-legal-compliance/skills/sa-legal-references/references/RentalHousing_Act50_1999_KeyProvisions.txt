RENTAL HOUSING ACT 50/1999

APPLICATION: Residential rental housing (not commercial leases)
RENTAL HOUSING TRIBUNALS: Provincial tribunals resolve landlord-tenant disputes
UNFAIR PRACTICES: Tribunal may declare conduct unfair — overcharging, harassment, illegal eviction
LEASE PROVISIONS: Written lease provisions, receipt for deposits, interest on deposits
INSPECTIONS: Ingoing + outgoing property inspections mandatory
EVICTION: Must follow PIE Act procedure — cannot lock out tenants
RELEVANCE TO BUSINESS: Low — mainly residential; commercial leases under common law
