DESIGNS ACT 195/1993
Assent: 22 Dec 1993 | Commence: 1 May 1995
Amended by: Act 38/1997, Companies Act 71/2008

AESTHETIC DESIGNS: Features appealing to the eye (pattern, shape, ornamentation)
FUNCTIONAL DESIGNS: Features necessitated by function (must be new, not commonplace)
REGISTRATION: 15-year max (aesthetic) / 10-year max (functional); Designs Office registry
CONVENTION APPLICATIONS: Paris Convention priority (6 months)
INFRINGEMENT: Unauthorised making/importing/using/disposing of registered design
LINK TO IP PILLAR: Complements Patents (functional), Trade Marks (brand), Copyright (creative)
