FINANCIAL MARKETS ACT 19/2012
Gazette: GG 36121, 1 Feb 2013 | Assent: 30 Jan 2013 | Commence: 3 Jun 2013
Amended by: Acts 45/2013, 9/2017 (FSR Act)

REGULATES: Exchanges, CSDs, clearing houses, trade repositories (market infrastructures)
INSIDER TRADING: Prohibited with criminal sanctions (Ch VIII/X)
MARKET ABUSE: Prohibition of false/misleading statements, market manipulation
AUTHORISATION: All market infrastructures must be licensed by FSCA
CONDUCT STANDARDS: FSCA may issue conduct standards for regulated persons
NOMINEES: FSCA approval required
LINK TO FSR ACT: Prudential Authority + FSCA under Twin Peaks (ss1A, 6A)

Act No. 19 of 2012 Financial Markets Act, 2012
ACT
To provide for the regulation of ﬁnancial markets; to license and regulate
exchanges, central securities depositories, clearing houses and trade repositories;
to regulate and control securities trading, clearing and settlement, and the custodyand administration of securities; to prohibit insider trading, and other marketabuses; to provide for the approval of nominees; to provide for codes of conduct; toreplace the Securities Services Act, 2004, as amended by the Financial ServicesLaws General Amendment Act, 2008, so as to align this Act with internationalstandards; and to provide for matters connected therewith.
