BANKS ACT 94/1990 (previously Deposit-taking Institutions Act)
Gazette: GG 12617, 11 Jul 1990 | Commence: 1 Feb 1991 (s60: 9 Aug 1991)
Amended by: Act 9/1993, 26/1994, 55/1996, 36/2000, 19/2003, 20/2007, 40/2007,
22/2013, 3/2015, 9/2017 (FSR Act), 18/2019, 23/2021, 11/2022

REGISTRATION (ChIII): All banks must register with PA (Prudential Authority);
branch of foreign institution (s18A); annual licence (s35)
SHAREHOLDING (ChIV): >15% stake requires PA approval (s37); nominee shares (s38)
COMPANIES ACT OVERLAY (ChV): Governance, directors/officers (s60, fit & proper),
compliance function (s60A), audit committee (s64), risk committee (s64A),
remuneration committee (s64C)
PRUDENTIAL (ChVI): Minimum capital + reserves (s70), liquid assets (s72),
concentration risk (s73), returns to PA (s75)
CONDUCT (ChVII): Immovable property/shares limits (s76), related-party limits (s77),
undesirable practices (s78)
ENFORCEMENT (ChVIII): Registrar powers over unregistered persons (ss81-84)
PENALTIES (ChIX): Fines and imprisonment for contraventions
