FINANCIAL ADVISORY AND INTERMEDIARY SERVICES ACT 37 OF 2002
(Assented to 15 November 2002; Published 15 November 2002 in GG 24079)
================================================================================

OVERVIEW: This Act regulates the rendering of financial advisory and
intermediary services to clients in South Africa. It establishes the
fit and proper requirements for financial services providers (FSPs),
codes of conduct, the FAIS Ombud, and enforcement mechanisms.

STRUCTURE: 7 Chapters (Introductory, Authorisation, Representatives,
  Codes of Conduct, Duties of FSPs, Enforcement, Miscellaneous), ss 1-46

KEY CHAPTERS:
  Ch1 Introductory (ss1-4):  Definitions, application, interpretation
  Ch2 Authorisation (ss5-13): FSP licence, fit and proper, suspension,
      lapsing of licence, exemptions
  Ch3 Representatives (ss14-15): Debarment, duties of representatives
  Ch4 Codes of Conduct (ss15-16): Publication by Registrar, principles
  Ch5 Duties of FSPs (ss17-20): Compliance officers, record keeping,
      accounting and audit requirements
  Ch6 Enforcement (ss20A-35): Inspections, penalties, FAIS Ombud,
      civil remedies, offences, right of appeal
  Ch7 Miscellaneous (ss36-46): Disclosure, client education, exemptions
      by Minister, regulations, transitional provisions, short title

KEY INSTITUTIONS CREATED:
  - FAIS Ombud (Office established in s20)
  - Registrar of Financial Services Providers (FSB)
  - Advisory Committee on Financial Services Providers
