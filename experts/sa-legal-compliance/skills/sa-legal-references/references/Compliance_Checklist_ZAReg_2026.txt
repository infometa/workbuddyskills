SA COMPANY SETUP CHECKLIST (Chinese Enterprises)

PHASE 1: INCORPORATION
[ ] CIPC name reservation (R75) + Company reg (Pty Ltd R175)
[ ] MOI tailored to business + Registered SA office address
[ ] SA bank account + B-BBEE EME affidavit

PHASE 2: TAX (within 60 days)
[ ] SARS income tax + VAT (>R1m compulsory / >R50k voluntary)
[ ] PAYE/UIF/SDL registration (if hiring) + Customs (if importing)

PHASE 3: EMPLOYMENT
[ ] COIDA registration (7 days of 1st employee)
[ ] BCEA contracts + OHSA s8 policy + H&S reps/committee
[ ] EMP201 monthly + EMP501 bi-annual

PHASE 4: REGULATORY
[ ] POPIA compliance (IO + PAIA manual + privacy policy)
[ ] CIPC annual return (30 biz days of anniversary) + BO register
[ ] F/S prepared + B-BBEE annual verification

PHASE 5: ONGOING (monthly/quarterly/annual filings tracked above)
