PENSION FUNDS ACT 24/1956
Gazette: GG 5679, 11 May 1956 | Assent: 28 Apr 1956 | Commence: 1 Jan 1958
Amended extensively (30+ amendments, latest: Act 11/2022)

REGISTRATION: All pension funds must register with FSCA (s4)
BOARD (s7A): Fund must have board of trustees; at least 50% member-elected
PRUDENTIAL: Actuarial valuation every 3 years; funding requirements (Reg 28/29)
INVESTMENT: Reg 28 limits (max 75% equities, 25% offshore, 15% private equity etc.)
SURPLUS: Apportionment rules (s15B) — employer surplus account vs member surplus account
ADJUDICATOR (s30C-30T): Pension Funds Adjudicator resolves complaints
(accessible, no cost to complainant, determination binding)
ADMINISTRATOR (s13B): Fund administrator must be FSCA-approved
UNCLAIMED BENEFITS: Financial Sector Conduct Authority rules for tracing members
