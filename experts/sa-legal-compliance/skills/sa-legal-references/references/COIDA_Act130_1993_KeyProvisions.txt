COMPENSATION FOR OCCUPATIONAL INJURIES AND DISEASES ACT 130/1993
Gazette: GG 15158, 6 Oct 1993 | Assent: 24 Sep 1993 | Commence: 1 Mar 1994
Amended by: Act 61/1997, Act 10/2022, NHI Act 20/2023

EMPLOYER OBLIGATIONS: Register with Compensation Fund within 7 days of first employee.
Pay annual assessment levy based on earnings x industry risk class.
REPORTING: W.CI.2 (Employer's Report of Accident), W.CI.4 (First Medical Report),
W.CI.5 (Progress/Final Medical). Online via Compeasy system.
BENEFITS: 75% of earnings for temp disability (first 3mo paid by employer, reclaimed).
>30% perm disability = lifetime monthly pension. <=30% = lump sum.
Death = widow/widower lifetime pension + children <18.
EXCLUSIONS: Wilful negligence, off-duty accidents, 3-day waiting period (medical costs
paid regardless). Claims must be submitted within 12 months of accident.
ADMINISTRATION: Compensation Commissioner under DG of Dept of Employment & Labour.
NATIONAL/PROVINCIAL GOVT: Exempt from assessment payments (s84(1)).

Right of employee to compensation 5 
22. (1) If an employee meets with an accident resulting. in his disablement or 
death such employee or the dependants of such employee shall, subject to the

this Act. · · · 10 
(2) No periodical payments shall be made in respect of temporary total 
disablement or temporary partial disablement which lasts for three days or less. 
(3) (a) If an accident is attributable to the serious and wilful misconduct of the 
employee, no compensation shall be payable in terms of this Act, unless---
(i) the accident results in serious disablement; or 15 
(ii) the employee dies in consequence thereof leaving a dependant wholly 
financially dependent upon him. 
(b) Notwithstanding paragraph (a) the commissioner may, and the employer 
individually liable or mutual association concerned, as the case may be; shall, if

(4) For the purposes of this Act an accident shall be deemed to have arisen out

take place in the course of such employee's employment.
