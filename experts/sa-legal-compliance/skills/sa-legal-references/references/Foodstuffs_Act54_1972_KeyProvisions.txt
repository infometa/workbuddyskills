FOODSTUFFS, COSMETICS AND DISINFECTANTS ACT 54/1972
Administered by: Department of Health

REQUIREMENTS:
- All foodstuffs, cosmetics, disinfectants sold in SA must comply
- Mandatory registration of products with Director-General
- Labelling: ingredients, expiry date, batch number, manufacturer details
- Advertising restrictions: no false/misleading claims
- Import controls: imported products must meet SA standards
- Inspectors have powers of entry, search, seizure, sampling
- Offences: sale of prohibited/contaminated products, false labelling
- Penalties: fines and/or imprisonment
OVERLAP: Consumer Protection Act 68/2008 covers labelling/marketing aspects
