CASELAW / PRECEDENT - IMPORTANT NOTE

South Africa is a common law jurisdiction. Court precedent is binding:
- Constitutional Court: highest on constitutional matters
- Supreme Court of Appeal (SCA): highest on non-constitutional matters
- Labour Court/Labour Appeal Court: specialist labour precedents

Key source: SAFLII (saflii.org) - free full-text SA case law
Commercial databases: LexisNexis South Africa, Juta Law

For any legal question, check: (1) statute text in this corpus, (2) case law on SAFLII.
This corpus contains statutes only - case law requires live SAFLII search.
