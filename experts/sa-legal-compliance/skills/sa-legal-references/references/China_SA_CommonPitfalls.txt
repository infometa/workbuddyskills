CHINESE ENTERPRISE TOP 10 SA COMPLIANCE PITFALLS

1. B-BBEE: Foreign companies NOT exempt. Get EME affidavit Day 1.
2. Labour: Dismissal = 12m salary CCMA awards. Always follow LRA s188.
3. Forex: All foreign loans >R500M need SARB approval. All loans reportable.
4. VAT: Can't claim input VAT until registered. Register at R1m or lose 15%.
5. BO: CIPC blocks annual return without BO filing since Jul 2024.
6. Independent Contractor: Misclassification = back-tax + 200% penalties.
7. IP Transfer: Parent-SA IP transfers need Exchange Control approval.
8. Mining: Mining Charter III requires 30% B-BBEE equity (non-transferable).
9. COIDA: 7-day registration deadline. One accident = R2-5M direct liability.
10. POPIA: Information Regulator enforcing. R10M fines + imprisonment.
