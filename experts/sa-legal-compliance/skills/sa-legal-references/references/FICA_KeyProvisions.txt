KEY POINTS FOR BUSINESS:


FICA 38/2001 — Financial Intelligence Centre Act

PURPOSE: Combat money laundering + terrorist financing. Establishes FIC.

ACCOUNTABLE INSTITUTIONS (Schedule 1):
  Banks, mutual banks, Postbank, Ithala Development Finance Corp
  Life insurers, financial services providers, collective investment managers
  Pension fund administrators, forex dealers
  Estate agents, attorneys (certain transactions), trust company service providers
  Casinos/gambling institutions
  Motor vehicle dealers, Krugerrand dealers
  High-value goods dealers (>R100,000)

CORE OBLIGATIONS:
s21  — CUSTOMER DUE DILIGENCE (CDD):
       • Identify and verify client identity before transacting
       • Identify beneficial owner (natural person behind legal entity)
       • Ongoing monitoring of business relationship
       • Enhanced due diligence for: prominent persons, high-risk countries
s22  — Record keeping: keep CDD records 5 years after relationship ends
s28  — Report CASH transactions > R49,999 (aggregate) to FIC
s29  — Report SUSPICIOUS/UNUSUAL transactions to FIC (within prescribed period)
s29A — Report property connected to terrorist/proliferation financing
s34  — Compliance officer must be appointed
s42  — Informal requests → FIC can request information
s45  — PENALTIES: fine up to R100M or 15 years imprisonment

WHEN IT MATTERS:
- Any entity in Schedule 1 must register with FIC
- Must implement Risk Management and Compliance Programme (RMCP)
- Fail to report suspicious Tx = criminal offence
- Client identity must be verified — cannot transact with anonymous clients
- Beneficial ownership register required

NOTES:
2001 original Gazette. FIC Amendment Act 1/2017 introduced risk-based approach, beneficial ownership, enhanced due diligence, UN targeted financial sanctions — check updated version.
