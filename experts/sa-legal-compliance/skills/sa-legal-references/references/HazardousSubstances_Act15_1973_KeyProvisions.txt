HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES ACT 15/1973
Administered by: Department of Health

CLASSIFICATION: Substances grouped by hazard class (Group I-IV)
LICENSING: Import, manufacture, sale, storage, transport all require licenses
ELECTRONIC PRODUCTS: X-ray equipment, lasers, UV equipment regulated
RADIATION: Group III hazardous substances (radioactive materials)
CHEMICALS: Group I/II substances require special storage/handling
IMPORT CONTROLS: Prior approval needed for all hazardous substance imports
INSPECTORS: Powers of entry, inspection, sampling, seizure
PACKAGING & LABELLING: Specific requirements for containers and warnings
PENALTIES: Fines and/or imprisonment for contraventions
OVERLAP: NEMA 107/1998 (environmental aspects), OHSA 85/1993 (workplace safety)

ACT 
 
 
To provide  for the control  of substances  which  may cause  injury  or ill-health  to or death  of human  beings by  
reason  of their toxic,  corrosive,  irritant,  strong ly sensitizing  or flammable  nature  or the generation  of 
pressure  thereby  in certain  circumstances,  and for the control  of certain  electronic  products;  to provide  for 
the division  of such  substances  or products  into groups  in relation  to the degree  of danger;  to provide  for 
the prohibitio n and contro l of the importation , manufact ure, sale, use, operation , application , modification, 
disposal  or dumping  of such  substances  and products;  and to provide  for matters  connected  therewith.
