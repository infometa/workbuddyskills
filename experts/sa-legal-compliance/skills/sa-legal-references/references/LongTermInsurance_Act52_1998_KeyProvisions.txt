LONG-TERM INSURANCE ACT 52/1998
Gazette: GG 19276, 23 Sep 1998 | Assent: 15 Sep 1998 | Commence: 1 Jan 1999
Amended by: Act 17/2003, 34/2005, 22/2008, 27/2008, 45/2013, 18/2017

REGISTRATION: All long-term insurers must register with PA/FSCA
CAPITAL: Capital adequacy requirements (Schedule 3)
GOVERNANCE: Board, audit committee, actuarial function, fit & proper directors
POLICYHOLDER PROTECTION: Policy benefits, disclosure, cooling-off, surrender values
INTERMEDIARIES: Must be licensed FAIS representatives
DEMUTUALISATION: Framework for converting mutual to public companies
LINKS: FAIS Act, FSR Act (Twin Peaks), Pension Funds Act
