SACU/AfCFTA/AGOA REGIONAL TRADE SUMMARY

SACU: SA+Botswana+Lesotho+Namibia+Eswatini. CET, duty-free internal trade.
AfCFTA: SA ratified 2019. 90% tariff elimination. Single African market.
AGOA: US preference for 40+ African countries. SA: 6,000+ products duty-free.
EUSADC EPA: SA-EU duty-free. Rules of origin apply.
Source: SARS/dtic/AU.
