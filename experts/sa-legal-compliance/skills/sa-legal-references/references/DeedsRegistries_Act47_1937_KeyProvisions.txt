DEEDS REGISTRIES ACT 47/1937
Assent: 19 May 1937 | Administered by: Department of Agriculture, Land Reform & Rural Development

DEEDS OFFICES: Registry of deeds in each province; Deeds Registrars appointed
REGISTRATION OF LAND: All transfers, mortgages, servitudes, leases >10 years must be registered
TRANSFER PROCESS: Deed of transfer executed by conveyancer, examined by Registrar, registered
MORTGAGE BONDS: Registration of mortgage bonds over immovable property; cancellation procedures
SERVITUDES: Praedial and personal servitudes registered against title deeds
SECTIONAL TITLES LINK: Sectional Titles Schemes Management Act (separate Act)
CONVEYANCER: Only admitted conveyancers may prepare deeds
PUBLIC ACCESS: Deeds registry is public — anyone can search title deeds
CRITICAL FOR: Property transactions, mortgage lending, security over immovable assets
