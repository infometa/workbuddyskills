IMMIGRATION ACT 13 OF 2002 — FULL TEXT WITH KEY PROVISIONS
(Assented to 30 May 2002; Published 31 May 2002)
================================================================================

OVERVIEW: This Act regulates admission of persons to, their
residence in, and their departure from South Africa. It establishes
the immigration control framework including visa/permit categories,
enforcement mechanisms, Immigration Courts, employer obligations,
and offences/penalties for non-compliance.

STRUCTURE: 55 sections in 10 chapters + 3 schedules

KEY CHAPTERS:
  Ch 1 (ss 1-8):  Objectives and Structures of Immigration Control
  Ch 2 (s 9):     Admission and Departure
  Ch 3 (ss 10-24): Temporary Residence Permits (15 categories)
  Ch 4 (ss 25-28): Permanent Residence
  Ch 5 (ss 29-31): Exclusions and Exemptions
  Ch 6 (ss 32-37): Enforcement and Monitoring
  Ch 7 (ss 38-45): Duties and Obligations
  Ch 8 (s 46):    Immigration Practitioners
  Ch 9 (ss 49-50): Offences
  Ch 10 (ss 51-55): Transitional Provisions

SCHEDULES:
  Schedule 1: Offences per s28(a)/(b) — crimes making person prohibited
  Schedule 2: Offences per s28(b) — crimes for withdrawal of PR
  Schedule 3: Laws repealed or amended
