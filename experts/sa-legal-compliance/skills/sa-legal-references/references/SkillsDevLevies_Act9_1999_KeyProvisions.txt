SKILLS DEVELOPMENT LEVIES ACT 9/1999
Commence: 30 Apr 1999 | Amended by TAA and Revenue Laws Acts (2000-2024)

LEVY: 1% of employee remuneration payable monthly by employer
ADMINISTRATION: Collected by SARS, distributed to SETAs (80%) + National Skills Fund (20%)
THRESHOLD: Employers with annual payroll > R500,000 (excludes govt, public benefit orgs)
PAYMENT: Within 7 days after month-end via SARS
MANDATORY GRANT: Employers submit Workplace Skills Plan (WSP) + Annual Training Report (ATR)
to claim back portion of levy from SETA
PENALTIES: Late payment interest + penalties under Tax Administration Act 28/2011

Recovery of levy
14. (1) A levy payable by an employer in terms of section 7(1) to a SETA or its

(2) If an employer—
(a) fails to submit a statement in respect of the amount of levies due as

(b) submits a statement reflecting an amount which, in the opinion of the
executive officer of the SETA or approved body, as the case may be, is less
than the amount which is due in terms of this Act,
the executive officer of the SETA or approved body, as the case may be, may estimate the

the area in which the person liable for the levy, interest or penalty carries on business.
