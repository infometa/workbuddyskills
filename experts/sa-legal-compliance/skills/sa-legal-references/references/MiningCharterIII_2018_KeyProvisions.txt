MINING CHARTER III (2018) — BROAD-BASED SOCIO-ECONOMIC EMPOWERMENT CHARTER FOR MINING AND MINERALS INDUSTRY
Gazette: GG 41934, GN 1002, 27 Sep 2018
Issued under: s100(2) of MPRDA 28/2002
Minister: Gwede Mantashe

KEY ELEMENTS:/n1. OWNERSHIP: New mining rights require 30% B-BBEE shareholding (5% non-transferable carried interest for communities + 5% for employees)
2. PROCUREMENT: 70% local content on mining goods, 80% services from B-BBEE compliant companies
3. EMPLOYMENT EQUITY: Targets for black representation at board/executive/senior/middle/junior management levels
4. HUMAN RESOURCE DEVELOPMENT: 5% of annual payroll on skills development (including bursaries/learnerships)
5. MINE COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT: Social Labour Plans (SLP) mandatory for all mining rights
6. HOUSING & LIVING CONDITIONS: Upgrade of hostels, home ownership schemes
7. SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT: Environmental compliance, rehabilitation as per MPRDA
PENALTY: Non-compliance = mining right may be suspended or cancelled
