NEMA 107/1998 — National Environmental Management Act

PURPOSE: Framework for environmental management; EIA requirements; duty of care;
environmental principles; enforcement.

NATIONAL ENVIRONMENTAL MANAGEMENT PRINCIPLES (s2):
- Environmental management must place people and their needs at the forefront
- Development must be socially, environmentally, and economically sustainable
- Environmental impact assessments (EIA) are required for listed activities
- Polluter pays principle
- Precautionary principle
- Public participation in environmental decision-making

DUTY OF CARE (s28):
- Every person has a duty of care to prevent significant pollution or
  environmental degradation
- If it occurs: take reasonable measures to minimise and rectify
- Director-General / MEC can direct any person to take specific measures
- Failure to comply: authority may take measures and recover costs

EIA REQUIREMENTS (s24):
- Minister/MEC identify activities that require environmental authorisation
- Listed activities (GN R983/R984/R985): Basic Assessment or Full EIA
- No listed activity may commence without environmental authorisation
- Application → Basic Assessment Report OR Scoping → EIA → Decision
- Timeframes: Basic Assessment 30 days, EIA 106 days review

ENVIRONMENTAL MANAGEMENT PROGRAMME (s24N):
- Required for mining/prospecting operations under MPRDA
- Must describe: environmental impact; mitigation measures; rehabilitation plan;
  financial provision for closure

ENFORCEMENT (s31A-31Q):
- Compliance notices: inspector may issue if non-compliance
- Abatement costs can be recovered
- Environmental Management Inspectors (EMIs): designated officials with powers
  to enter, inspect, seize, arrest
- Director of Public Prosecutions may prosecute violations

OFFENCES AND PENALTIES (s34):
- Fine up to R5 million or up to 5 years imprisonment
- For 2nd conviction: fine up to R10 million or up to 10 years

FINANCIAL PROVISION (s24PA):
- Applicants for mining/environmental authorisations must provide financial
  provision for rehabilitation and closure
- Reviewed annually
