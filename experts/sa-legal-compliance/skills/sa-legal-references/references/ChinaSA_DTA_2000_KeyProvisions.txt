CHINA-SOUTH AFRICA DOUBLE TAXATION AGREEMENT (DTA)
Signed: 25 Apr 2000, Pretoria | In force: 2001

KEY WITHHOLDING RATES:/n- Dividends: 5% (>=10% shareholding) / 10% (portfolio)
- Interest: 10%
- Royalties: 7% (patents/copyright/know-how) / 10% (film/TV/equipment adjusted: 10% x 70%)
- Pensions: 0% (only taxable in residence country)

PERMANENT ESTABLISHMENT: Building/construction site >12 months = PE
BUSINESS PROFITS: Only taxable in source country if attributable to PE
SHIPPING/AIR: Profits from international traffic only taxable in residence country
CAPITAL GAINS: Immovable property taxable where located; shares in property-rich companies taxable where property located
NON-DISCRIMINATION: SA may impose branch profits tax up to 5% above standard corporate rate on Chinese company PE
EXCHANGE OF INFORMATION: Full exchange per OECD standard
MLI IMPACT: PPT (principal purpose test) applies — treaty benefits denied if obtaining benefit was a principal purpose of arrangement
SOURCE: SARS.gov.za / chinatax.gov.cn
