MALAYSIA FLEXIBLE WORKING ARRANGEMENTS (FWA) GUIDELINES 2024
Comprehensive Summary for HR & Employer Compliance
================================================================================
Source: MOHR Garis Panduan AKF 2024, Skrine, HR Online, OmniHR
Effective: December 2024 / Implementation: 2025
================================================================================

1. LEGAL BASIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Employment Act 1955 (Amendment 2022), Section 60P and 60Q:
- Section 60P: Employee has RIGHT TO APPLY for flexible working arrangements
- Section 60Q: Employer must RESPOND IN WRITING within 60 DAYS
- Employer may REFUSE on specified grounds (see Section 5)
- MOHR Guidelines (December 2024): Implementation framework and best practices

2. TYPES OF FLEXIBLE WORKING ARRANGEMENTS (FWA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
As defined in MOHR Guidelines:

2.1 Flextime
- Employee chooses START and END times
- Core hours may be specified (e.g. 10am-4pm)
- Total daily/weekly hours unchanged
- "9-5" becomes optional, not fixed

2.2 Compressed Work Week
- Same total hours compressed into fewer days
- Example: 40 hours in 4 days (10-hour days)
- Must comply with Employment Act daily hour limits (max 8 hrs/day normally)

2.3 Flexible Work Location (Hybrid/Remote)
- Working from home, co-working space, or any location
- NOT requiring physical presence at office
- MUST set clear expectations for availability, communication,
  data security, and equipment

2.4 Job Sharing
- Two or more employees share ONE full-time role
- Pro-rated salary and benefits
- Work coordination and handover arrangements essential

2.5 Reduced Work Hours / Part-Time
- Fewer hours than full-time schedule
- Pro-rated salary and statutory benefits
- May be temporary or permanent arrangement

2.6 Staggered Hours
- Different employees have different start/end times
- Reduces office crowding during peak hours
- Common post-pandemic arrangement

3. EMPLOYEE APPLICATION PROCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Employee submits WRITTEN APPLICATION to employer
  - Must specify: type of FWA requested, proposed schedule,
    start date, duration (temporary/permanent)
Step 2: Employer ACKNOWLEDGES receipt
Step 3: Employer has 60 DAYS to respond IN WRITING
Step 4: If approved: both parties agree on terms
  - Document the arrangement (variation letter/agreement)
  - Specify trial period (e.g. 3 months) if new
Step 5: If refused: employer MUST state GROUNDS for refusal

4. VALID GROUNDS FOR EMPLOYER REFUSAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Under Section 60Q, employer may refuse FWA if:
a) FWA would result in SIGNIFICANT COST increase to employer
b) FWA would have DETRIMENTAL IMPACT on:
   - Business productivity
   - Quality of output
   - Customer/client service delivery
c) Employee's role is NOT SUITABLE for FWA (e.g. frontline service,
   physical operations with no remote option)
d) FWA would affect WORKPLACE SAFETY or regulatory compliance
e) FWA not practical due to ORGANISATIONAL STRUCTURE (small team,
   specialized interdependent roles)

5. EMPLOYER OBLIGATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Accept and acknowledge ALL FWA applications
- RESPOND IN WRITING within 60 days
- If refusing: state GROUNDS clearly
- If approving: establish clear performance expectations
- Treat ALL employees EQUALLY in FWA consideration
- DO NOT discriminate (gender, race, disability, etc.)
- Maintain records of ALL FWA applications and outcomes

6. BEST PRACTICES FOR IMPLEMENTATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Develop WRITTEN FWA POLICY before receiving applications
- Include: eligibility criteria, application process, approval matrix,
  trial periods, performance metrics, data security protocols
- Set up IT infrastructure for remote work (VPN, collaboration tools)
- Train managers on managing remote/hybrid teams
- Regular review of FWA effectiveness (quarterly)
- Communicate policy to ALL employees
- Trial period (3-6 months) with KPIs before permanent adoption

7. SPECIAL CONSIDERATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 Data Security
- Remote workers MUST comply with PDPA 2010
- Company data must be accessed via SECURE connections
- Avoid public WiFi for sensitive work
- Company equipment policy (BYOD vs company-provided)

7.2 Performance Management
- Shift from "hours present" to "OUTPUT-BASED" evaluation
- Set clear KPIs and deadlines
- Regular check-ins (weekly 1:1 virtual meetings)

7.3 Health and Safety
- Employer still has duty of care for home-based workers
- Provide ergonomic guidance for home workstations
- Mental health check-ins important for isolated remote workers

8. PENALTIES AND ENFORCEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- No specific penalties prescribed for FWA non-compliance
- General Employment Act penalties apply
- Refusal without valid grounds may be used as evidence in
  constructive dismissal claims
- Directorate General of Labour may investigate complaints
