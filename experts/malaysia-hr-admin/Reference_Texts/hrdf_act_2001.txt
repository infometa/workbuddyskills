Comprehensive Summary for HR & Employer Compliance

1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HRDF (Human Resources Development Fund), officially rebranded as HRD Corp
(Human Resources Development Corporation) in 2021, is a government statutory
body under the Ministry of Human Resources. It is governed by the Pembangunan
Sumber Manusia Berhad Act 2001 (PSMB Act). The fund collects a mandatory levy
from registered employers to finance training and development of the Malaysian
workforce.

2. WHO MUST REGISTER AND CONTRIBUTE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MANDATORY Registration: Employers with 10 OR MORE Malaysian employees
  - Applies to ALL industries under First Schedule of PSMB Act 2001 (amended 2021)
  - Must register via e-TRiS portal

OPTIONAL Registration: Employers with 5 to 9 Malaysian employees
  - Voluntary registration to access training grants

EXEMPTED entities:
  - Government agencies
  - Statutory bodies
  - Local authorities
  - Non-profit organizations (NGOs)

3. LEVY RATES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mandatory contributors (≥ 10 employees): 1.0% of monthly wages
Voluntary contributors (5-9 employees):   0.5% of monthly wages

Levy calculated on: Basic Salary + Fixed Allowances
Levy excludes: Overtime, bonuses, commissions, variable pay

Employer ONLY pays — CANNOT deduct from employee wages.

4. REGISTRATION PROCESS (e-TRiS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Check eligibility (First Schedule, PSMB Act 2001)
Step 2: Prepare documents:
  - Form 1 (Employer Registration Form)
  - Certificate of Incorporation (Form 9 / Section 17)
  - SSM Business Registration (Form D)
  - Latest EPF Statement (Form A)
Step 3: Submit online at https://eis.hrdcorp.gov.my
Step 4: Await approval (receive HRDF Employer Code and login credentials)

5. PAYMENT DEADLINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Levy payments must be submitted by the 15th of the FOLLOWING month.
Example: October wages levy due by November 15th.

6. TRAINING GRANT SCHEMES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For Current Employees:
  - Skim Bantuan Latihan (SBL) / SBL-Khas: Public/in-house training
  - Skim Latihan Bersama (SLB): Joint training with other employers
  - On-the-Job Training (OJT)
  - Computer-Based Training (CBT)
  - Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)

For Future Workers:
  - Industrial Training Scheme (ITS)
  - Future Workers Training (FWT)

For Internal Training Infrastructure:
  - IT Equipment Purchase (ALAT)
  - Training Facilities & Renovation

IMPORTANT: All training must be PRE-APPROVED and conducted by HRDF-REGISTERED
training providers to qualify for claims.

7. CLAIM PROCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Apply for training grant BEFORE training begins
2. HRD Corp approves based on training relevance and provider registration
3. Conduct training with registered provider
4. Submit claim with attendance records, invoices, evaluation forms
5. HRD Corp processes and reimburses to employer

8. PENALTIES FOR NON-COMPLIANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Fine up to RM 10,000
- Imprisonment up to 1 YEAR
- OR BOTH
- Late payment: 10% annual interest applied DAILY after the 15th deadline
- Non-registration: can trigger prosecution

9. BENEFITS OF COMPLIANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Training budget funded by your own levy contributions
- Upskilled workforce → higher productivity
- Tax-deductible training expenses
- Competitive advantage through skilled employees
- Reduced dependency on foreign labour

10. LEVY CALCULATION EXAMPLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Company with 15 employees, total monthly wages RM 45,000:
  HRDF Levy = RM 45,000 x 1% = RM 450/month
  Annual levy = RM 5,400

Company with 7 employees (voluntary), total monthly wages RM 21,000:
  HRDF Levy = RM 21,000 x 0.5% = RM 105/month
  Annual levy = RM 1,260

These funds go into the company's HRDF account and can be claimed back for
approved training — effectively a TRAINING BUDGET, not a cost.
