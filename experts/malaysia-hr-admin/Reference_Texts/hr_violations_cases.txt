MALAYSIA HR COMPLIANCE — COMMON VIOLATIONS & ENFORCEMENT CASES
Practical Reference for HR Risk Awareness

1. WAGE & OVERTIME VIOLATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Paying Below Minimum Wage
- VIOLATION: Basic salary < RM 1,700/month
- PENALTY: Fine up to RM 10,000 PER EMPLOYEE (first offence);
  repeat: RM 20,000 per employee OR imprisonment
- CASE: JTKSM 2024 raids on 1,200+ employers recovered RM 15M+ in back wages
- FIX: Audit all salaries quarterly; note that ALLOWANCES don't count toward basic wage

1.2 Not Paying Overtime
- VIOLATION: Employer claims "you're on fixed salary, no OT pay"
- Employment Act Section 60A: OT is MANDATORY for employees earning ≤ RM 4,000/month
- RATE: 1.5x weekdays, 2x rest days, 3x public holidays
- PENALTY: Compound fine + back-pay orders from Labour Court
- FIX: Implement proper time-tracking; calculate OT per Section 60I formula

1.3 Impermissible Deductions
- VIOLATION: Deducting "uniform fees," "breakage costs," or "training bonds" without written consent
- Employment Act Section 24: ONLY deductions listed in Act are legal
- ILLEGAL deductions: uniform cost, cash register shortages (unless written consent),
  employer EPF portion, levy, recruitment fees
- FIX: Get WRITTEN consent for any voluntary deduction; keep signed forms

2. EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT VIOLATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 No Written Contract
- VIOLATION: Verbal agreement only, no written terms
- Employment Act Section 10: Written contract REQUIRED for employment > 1 month
- RISK: Industrial Court will interpret contract in FAVOUR of employee if no contract exists
- FIX: Issue written contracts BEFORE or ON first day of work

2.2 Excessive Probation
- VIOLATION: Probation extended beyond 6 months without justification
- Industrial Court precedent: Probationers have SAME rights as confirmed staff
- RISK: Dismissal without proper procedure → unfair dismissal claim
- FIX: Default 3 months; maximum 6 months; extend ONLY with written justification

2.3 Unilateral Contract Changes
- VIOLATION: Changing salary, hours, location without employee consent
- RISK: Constructive dismissal claim (employee resigns = employer liable)
- FIX: Get WRITTEN consent for any material change to employment terms

3. TERMINATION VIOLATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 No Show-Cause Letter Before Dismissal
- VIOLATION: "You're fired, leave now"
- Employment Act Section 14(1): "Due inquiry" before dismissal
- INDUSTRIAL COURT OUTCOME: Near-automatic ruling for employee
- FIX: ALWAYS issue show-cause letter → evaluate response → DI if needed → decision

3.2 Dismissal During Maternity Leave
- VIOLATION: Terminating pregnant employee or during 98-day maternity leave
- Employment Act Section 42: PROTECTED from termination during maternity leave
- INDUSTRIAL COURT OUTCOME: Reinstatement + back wages; pregnancy discrimination = constitutional violation
- FIX: Cannot dismiss for any reason related to pregnancy/maternity

3.3 Retrenchment Without JTKSM Notification
- VIOLATION: Mass layoff without Form PK submission
- Required: Form PK 30 days BEFORE termination
- PENALTY: Fine + employees may claim for procedural unfairness
- FIX: Submit Form PK online at jtksm.mohr.gov.my

3.4 Foreign Workers First (LIFO Violation)
- VIOLATION: Retrenching locals while keeping foreign workers in same role
- National Employment Policy: Foreign workers should be retrenched BEFORE locals
- Industrial Court precedent: Employer must justify any deviation from this rule
- FIX: Review workforce composition before retrenchment selection

4. LEAVE & HOLIDAY VIOLATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Not Granting Annual Leave
- VIOLATION: "We don't give annual leave to probationers" / "No leave in first year"
- Employment Act Section 60E: Leave accrues FROM FIRST MONTH (pro-rated)
- RATE: 8 days (< 2 years), 12 days (2-5 years), 16 days (> 5 years)
- FIX: Pro-rate leave; cannot deny leave until "confirmed"

4.2 Not Paying PH If Falls on Rest Day
- VIOLATION: PH + Sunday = "too bad"
- Employment Act Section 60D(1A): If PH falls on rest day, NEXT WORKING DAY is substitute holiday
- FIX: Calendar all 11 gazetted PHs at start of year; note substitutions

4.3 Forcing Annual Leave for Sick Days
- VIOLATION: "You've used up sick leave, we'll deduct from annual leave"
- Employment Act: Separate entitlements — CANNOT substitute one for the other
- FIX: Separate tracking for medical leave and annual leave

5. FOREIGN WORKER VIOLATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Keeping Worker's Passport
- VIOLATION: Employer holds foreign worker's passport "for safekeeping"
- Passport Act 1966: ONLY the passport holder may possess passport
- EXCEPTION: Worker gives WRITTEN consent for passport renewal/endorsement (max 14 days)
- PENALTY: Fine + potential human trafficking charges (ATIPSOM 2007)
- FIX: Return passport immediately after visa endorsement; give worker a copy of work permit

5.2 Not Registering Foreign Worker with EPF
- VIOLATION: "Foreign workers don't need EPF"
- EPF Act: Foreign workers MANDATORY at 2% employer contribution (since Oct 2025)
- PENALTY: Fine up to RM 10,000; imprisonment up to 3 years
- FIX: Register all foreign workers with EPF; remit 2% monthly

5.3 Act 446 Hostel Violations
- VIOLATION: 15 workers in a 3-bedroom house
- ACT 446: 3.6 sqm per worker space standard
- PENALTY: Fine up to RM 50,000 per offence; PLKS renewal BLOCKED
- FIX: Calculate capacity = floor area / 3.6 sqm; apply for JTKSM Certificate

6. SOCIAL SECURITY VIOLATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Not Registering with SOCSO
- VIOLATION: Cash-only employment, no SOCSO registration
- SOCSO Act: ALL employees earning ≤ RM 6,000 must be registered
- PENALTY: Fine + employer personally liable for workplace injury compensation
- FIX: Register ALL employees day one; remit monthly

6.2 Late EPF Payment
- VIOLATION: "We'll pay the EPF next month"
- EPF Act: Due by 15th of following month
- PENALTY: Dividend-rate interest on outstanding amount, DAILY
- CASE: Some employers accumulated RM 500K+ in EPF arrears interest
- FIX: Automate EPF payment; never delay

7. RECORD-KEEPING VIOLATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 No Itemised Payslip
- VIOLATION: Cash payment with "trust me" accounting
- Employment Act Section 20(1): MUST provide itemised payslip showing
  basic wage, allowances, OT, all deductions, net pay
- FIX: Use payroll software; print or email payslips monthly

7.2 No Attendance Records
- VIOLATION: No clock-in, no OT records
- Employment Act: Employer MUST maintain attendance records for 6 years
- JTKSM audit: Without records, JTKSM will accept employee's claims at face value
- FIX: Digital attendance system with daily records

8. ANTI-HARASSMENT VIOLATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.1 No Anti-Sexual Harassment Policy
- VIOLATION: Company has no written policy, no complaint mechanism
- Anti-Sexual Harassment Act 2022: Employer MUST display policy and have procedures
- RISK: Tribunal complaint → compensation up to RM 250,000
- FIX: Draft policy, display in workplace, train all employees, appoint complaint handlers
