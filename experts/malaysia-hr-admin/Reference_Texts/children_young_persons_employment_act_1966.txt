MALAYSIA CHILDREN AND YOUNG PERSONS (EMPLOYMENT) ACT 1966 (Act 350)
Comprehensive Summary for HR & Employer Compliance
================================================================================
Source: AGC, JTKSM, MOHR, ILO Conventions
Last Amended: Revised 1988 (reprint), 2025 alignment updates
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Children and Young Persons (Employment) Act 1966 regulates the employment
of minors in Malaysia. It defines two protected categories:

CHILD: Person under the AGE OF 15 YEARS
YOUNG PERSON: Person who has completed 15 years but UNDER 18 YEARS

2. CHILDREN (Under 15) — EMPLOYMENT RESTRICTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GENERAL RULE: Children under 15 CANNOT be employed.

EXCEPTIONS (Section 2(2)):
a) Light work in FAMILY ENTERPRISES (family farm, family shop)
b) Approved APPRENTICESHIP programmes under government schemes
c) Public entertainment: film, theatre, circus (with Minister's LICENCE)
d) Work sponsored by government or recognised school

3. YOUNG PERSONS (Age 15 to Under 18) — EMPLOYMENT RESTRICTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Working Hours (Section 5)
- MAXIMUM 6 HOURS PER DAY (not 8)
- Cannot work between 8:00 PM and 6:00 AM (NO NIGHT WORK)
- Must have at least 30-minute break after 4 continuous hours
- Not more than 7 consecutive working days

3.2 Days of Work (Section 4)
- Cannot work on Sundays (or designated rest day)
- Cannot work on public holidays

3.3 Prohibited Employment (Sections 2(4), 3, 6)
Minister may prohibit young persons from engaging in:
- Hazardous work (chemicals, heavy machinery, extreme temperatures)
- Underground work (mines, tunnels)
- Work involving exposure to toxic substances
- Entertainment work deemed inapprioprate
- Any employment contrary to OSHA 1994

4. INDUSTRIAL WORK (Special Restrictions)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Factories and Machinery Act 1967 Compliance
Young persons must NOT work with or near:
- Dangerous machinery
- Electrical installations (unless supervised)
- Conveyor belts and moving equipment
- Ladders and scaffolding above 2 metres

4.2 Registration
Employer MUST notify JTKSM within 30 DAYS of employing a young person in
industrial establishment. Register must be maintained.

5. EMPLOYER OBLIGATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Verify age of ALL job applicants (check MyKad/birth certificate)
- Post age verification if young employee appears under 18
- Maintain separate register for young employees
- Ensure rostering does not exceed 6 hours/day
- Provide adequate supervision and safety training
- Obtain parental consent in writing
- Report any accident involving young employee to JTKSM within 48 hours

6. PENALTIES (Section 9)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Employing child without licence: fine up to RM 50,000 or
  imprisonment up to 2 years, or BOTH
- Employing young person in prohibited work: fine and/or imprisonment
- Exceeding working hours: compound fine
- Night work: compound fine
- Failure to maintain register: compound fine

7. APPRENTICESHIP PROVISION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Young persons may serve as APPRENTICES under approved schemes:
- Industrial Training Institutes (ILP)
- National Dual Training System (SLDN)
- Companies registered with Department of Skills Development
Apprenticeship contracts must be in WRITING and LODGED with JTKSM.

8. RELATIONSHIP WITH OTHER LAWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Education Act 1996: School attendance compulsory until Form 5 (age 17)
Employment Act 1955: General labour protections ALSO apply to young workers
OSHA 1994: Young workers = "vulnerable workers" requiring enhanced protection
UN Convention on Rights of the Child: Malaysia ratified; Act 350 implements it

9. PRACTICAL COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Policy explicitly states minimum hiring age: 18 (recommended, even though 15 legal)
[ ] Age verification process during onboarding (photocopy MyKad)
[ ] Separate attendance record for employees under 18
[ ] No night shifts for employees under 18
[ ] No hazardous assignments for employees under 18
[ ] Parental consent forms for employees under 18
[ ] JTKSM notification within 30 days for industrial employment of under-18s
