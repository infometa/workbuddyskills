================================================================================
MALAYSIA TRADE UNIONS ACT 1959 (Act 262)
Comprehensive Summary for HR & Employer Compliance
================================================================================
Source: OSHWARE.com, Ministry of Human Resources (JHEKS)
Last Amended: Act A1700 effective 15 September 2024
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Trade Unions Act 1959 governs the formation, registration, administration,
and dissolution of trade unions in Malaysia. It applies to Peninsular Malaysia
(1959), Sabah (1965), and Sarawak (1965). Enforcement is by the Department of
Trade Union Affairs under the Minister of Human Resources.

2. UNION REGISTRATION (PART III)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Registration Requirement (Section 8)
- Every union must apply for registration within ONE month of formation
- DG of Trade Union Affairs may extend to total maximum of SIX months
- Failure to register = union is ILLEGAL

2.2 Application Requirements (Section 10)
- Prescribed form signed by AT LEAST SEVEN MEMBERS
- Union rules printed in BAHASA MALAYSIA
- Statement of: member names/occupations/addresses, union name and address,
  officers' titles/names/ages/addresses/occupations

2.3 Grounds for REFUSAL (Section 12)
DG MUST refuse if:
- Union rules conflict with any law
- Union name is identical to existing union or misleading
- Union name incites hostility between races, religions, or nationalities

2.4 Registration Certificate (Section 13)
- Certificate is CONCLUSIVE EVIDENCE of valid registration

2.5 Cancellation of Registration (Section 15)
DG may cancel if:
- Union dissolves voluntarily
- Registration obtained by FRAUD or MISTAKE
- Executive committee composition violates Section 28
- Union funds used for purposes harmful to security/public order
- Union ceases to exist
- Union convicted under Fourth Schedule offences

PROCEDURAL SAFEGUARD: DG must give 30 days written notice with reasons.
Union may submit written objections within 30 days.

2.6 Consequences of Non-Registration/Cancellation (Section 19)
- Union becomes ILLEGAL ORGANISATION
- Loses all rights, immunities, and privileges
- Cannot participate in trade disputes, strikes, or lockouts
- Union MUST DISSOLVE, funds disposed as prescribed
- No person may act on behalf of the union

3. MEMBERSHIP RULES (PART V)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Age Requirements (Section 26)
- MINIMUM AGE: 15 years
- Under 21: Cannot hold office as executive committee member or trustee
- Under 18: Cannot vote on strikes, levies, dissolution, or rule changes
- Students: CANNOT join unless genuinely employed AND at least 18 years old

3.2 Membership Restrictions
- ONLY workers or employers in the relevant trade/industry
- Cannot restrict membership by RACE, RELIGION, or NATIONALITY (Section 26(1C))
- Dismissed/retrenched workers: may retain membership but CANNOT vote on strikes

3.3 Public Sector Restrictions (Section 27)
ABSOLUTELY PROHIBITED from union membership:
- Royal Malaysia Police
- Prison Service
- Armed Forces
- Public officers in confidential/security positions
- Managerial and Professional Group (unless exempted by Chief Secretary)

Statutory body employees:
- Union membership limited to that specific statutory body ONLY
- Cannot affiliate with other unions without Minister's WRITTEN APPROVAL

4. UNION OFFICERS (Section 28)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DISQUALIFIED from being executive committee member:
- NON-CITIZENS (unless union represents non-residents and Minister approves)
- Political party office-bearers
- Convicted of criminal breach of trust, extortion, or intimidation
- Convicted under Sections 49, 50, 50A of TU Act
- Undischarged bankrupts
- Former officers of unions cancelled under Section 15(1)(b)(vi) or (d)

5. STRIKES AND LOCKOUTS (Section 25A)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LEGAL CONDITIONS — ALL MUST BE MET:
a) Secret ballot: at least 60% of eligible members must vote,
   with OVER HALF voting in favour
b) SEVEN WORKING DAYS must pass after submitting ballot results to DG
c) Ballot not invalidated under Section 40 provisions
d) Must comply with union rules
e) Cannot involve matters where Minister has already decided
f) Must not violate any other law

PENALTIES for Illegal Strikes:
- Union and executive committee: fine up to RM 5,000 + RM 200/day
- Participating workers: IMMEDIATELY lose membership; BANNED from any
  union membership without DG's prior written approval
- Union must remove member from register, notify DG and member,
  post removal notice at registered office
- Union failing to comply: fine up to RM 1,000 + RM 100/day

BURDEN OF PROOF: On the union/officer/member to prove legality.

6. RIGHTS AND LIABILITIES (PART IV)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Immunity from Civil Suit (Section 21)
- No civil action for acts done "in contemplation or furtherance of a trade
  dispute" that involve breach of employment contract or interference with trade

6.2 Tort Liability (Section 22)
- Generally immune from tort claims
- EXCEPTION: actions involving union property

6.3 Contractual Liability (Section 23)
- Union liable for contracts made by or on its behalf

6.4 Restraint of Trade (Section 24)
- Union purposes NOT illegal simply because they are in restraint of trade

7. MINISTERIAL SUSPENSION POWER (Section 18)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Minister may SUSPEND any union or class of unions for up to SIX MONTHS if:
- Used for purposes harmful to national security or public order
- Requires consent of Minister responsible for internal security

During suspension:
- Registration certificate ceases to have effect
- Union prohibited from ALL activities
- No person may manage or act on its behalf

PENALTY for violation: Fine up to RM 10,000
Minister's decision is FINAL and CONCLUSIVE.

8. NAME CHANGE AND MERGER (Sections 31-33)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Name change: Majority member consent required
Merger: At least 50% of voting members must participate with 60% approval
Business transfer: Requires special resolution (2/3 majority of attending members)

9. IMPORTANT NOTE ON 2024 AMENDMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Trade Unions Act 2024 amendments (effective 15 Sept 2024) align Malaysian
labour law with ILO standards, specifically the ILO Declaration on Fundamental
Principles and Rights at Work.
