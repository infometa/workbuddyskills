================================================================================
MALAYSIA FOREIGN WORKER LEVY RATES 2026
Comprehensive Summary for HR & Employer Compliance
================================================================================
Source: MOHA, Immigration Department, KosPekerja
Last Updated: 2026-06-02
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The foreign worker levy is an ANNUAL government charge employers pay for each
foreign worker employed. It is the employer's responsibility — it CANNOT be
deducted from the worker's wages. Levy rates vary by SECTOR and REGION.

2. 2026 LEVY RATES (ANNUAL, PER WORKER)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sector                  Peninsular Malaysia    Sabah/Sarawak
Manufacturing           RM 1,850              RM 1,010
Construction            RM 1,850              RM 1,010
Services                RM 1,850              RM 1,490
Plantation              RM 640                RM 590
Agriculture             RM 640                RM 410
Mining & Quarrying      RM 1,850              Not published

NOTE: Sarawak adds an additional RM 1,854 FWTA (Foreign Workers Transformation
Approach) fee ON TOP of the basic Sabah/Sarawak levy.

3. WHY THE LEVY DIFFERS BY SECTOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Manufacturing, Construction, Services = HIGHER band (RM 1,850):
  Government policy steering toward AUTOMATION and reduced foreign-labour
  dependency

- Plantation, Agriculture = LOWER band (RM 640):
  Supports labour-intensive food and commodity production

4. OTHER RECURRING COSTS (Beyond Levy)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Employer costs in addition to levy:
- EPF for foreign workers: 2% of wages (mandatory since Oct 2025)
- SOCSO (First Category): 1.75% of wages (Employment Injury Scheme only)
- FOMEMA annual medical screening
- SPIKPA + FWIG insurance
- PLKS pass renewal
- Accommodation costs (Act 446 compliance)
- Recruitment agent fees (one-time)
- Visa with Reference (one-time)
- Security bond (refundable, one-time)

5. MULTI-TIER LEVY (MTLM) 2026
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The proposed multi-tier levy system has NOT YET been gazetted. If implemented:
- Levy would SCALE with company's foreign-worker dependency
- Higher foreign-worker ratio = higher per-worker levy
- Until gazetted, current FLAT rates remain in force

6. EMPLOYER OBLIGATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Pay levy ANNUALLY for each foreign worker
- Ensure levy paid BEFORE PLKS renewal
- Cannot deduct levy from worker's wages
- Maintain records of levy payments
- Comply with sector-specific quota limits

7. SECTOR-SPECIFIC QUOTAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Male workers: allowed in ALL sectors
- Female workers: generally for domestic helper, manufacturing, services
- Quotas set by Ministry of Home Affairs (MOHA)
- May vary by state and sector
- Freeze periods may be imposed based on labour market conditions

8. COLLECTION AGENCY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Levy collected by the Immigration Department of Malaysia (Jabatan Imigresen)
via designated banks and online payment portals.
