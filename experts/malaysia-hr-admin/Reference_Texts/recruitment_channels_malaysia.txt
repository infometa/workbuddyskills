| Role Level | Typical Fee (% of annual guaranteed package) | Typical Retainer Model |
|-----------|---------------------------------------------|----------------------|
| C-Suite / Director | 20-25% | 1/3 retainer + 1/3 shortlist + 1/3 placement |

## 4. RECRUITMENT ADVERTISING COMPLIANCE

### Anti-Discrimination (under Employment Act 1955 + Federal Constitution Art 8)
Job advertisements MUST NOT contain:
- Age restrictions ("below 30 only" unless BFOQ — bona fide occupational qualification)
- Gender preference ("male only" / "female preferred") unless BFOQ
- Race/religion preference ("Chinese only" / "Muslim only")
- Marital status ("single preferred")
- Language: English + BM both acceptable; specifying "Chinese-speaking" is acceptable IF role genuinely requires it (e.g., China market liaison)

### Penalties
- Discriminatory ad: complaint to Labour Department; can be evidence in Industrial Court
- Best practice: use gender-neutral language, focus on skills/qualifications

## 5. INTERVIEW COMPLIANCE (面试合规)

### Prohibited Questions (under EA 1955, PDPA 2010, Industrial Court norms)
- Marital status / plans for marriage
- Pregnancy / plans to have children
- Religion / place of worship
- Race / ethnicity (except where BFOQ applies, e.g., halal food handling)
- Political affiliation
- Age (unless verifying legal working age: 15+)
- Health status beyond job-related fitness (PDPA — sensitive personal data)
- Previous salary (some states/discussions; not yet prohibited in Malaysia but increasingly questioned; better to ask "salary expectation")

### Allowed Focus Areas
- Qualifications, skills, experience
- Work authorisation (citizenship, valid work permit)
- Job-related physical requirements (e.g., lifting 20kg for warehouse role)
- Availability to start, willingness to travel/relocate
- Reference checks (with candidate consent)

## 6. BACKGROUND CHECK NORMS

| Check | Common Practice | Cost (approx.) |
|-------|----------------|---------------|
| Employment verification | Last 2 employers; email/phone | In-house or MYR 100-200 via vendor |
| Education verification | Degree/diploma from issuing university | MYR 150-300 per check |
| Criminal record | Not routine; require candidate consent + apply to PDRM (Certificate of Good Conduct) | MYR 20 (PDRM fee) |
| Credit check (CTOS/CCRIS) | Only for finance/treasury roles; need consent | MYR 25-50 |
| Bankruptcy/Winding-up | MDI search (free) + SSM search | Free (MDI) + MYR 10 (SSM) |

### PDPA Compliance
- Must obtain candidate's written consent before background check
- Must inform candidate of scope and purpose
- Results must be securely stored and destroyed when no longer needed

## 7. RECRUITMENT TIMELINE BENCHMARKS

| Stage | Typical Duration |
|-------|-----------------|
| Job posting to first applicant | 24-72 hours (varies by platform) |
| CV screening & shortlist | 3-5 working days |
| First round interview | Within 1 week of shortlist |
| Second round / assessment | Within 1 week of first round |
| Offer & negotiation | 2-5 working days |
| Notice period (incoming) | 1-3 months (standard for professionals) |
| **Total time-to-fill** | **4-8 weeks (junior), 8-16 weeks (senior)** |
