1. MARKET OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Unemployment rate: 3% (historic lows)
- Labour force participation: 71%
- Typical job-change salary increase: 15-20% (niche roles: 30-40%)
- Key growth sectors: Digital/Tech, Financial Services, Engineering/Manufacturing
- Shared Services Centers (SSCs) expanding rapidly
- Candidate-driven market with high talent mobility

3. HOT SKILLS & TRENDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- ESG reporting specialists: 30% premium on job change

8. IMPORTANT NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Salaries are ANNUAL GROSS (before tax, EPF, SOCSO)
- Ranges represent 25th-75th percentile
- Actual salary depends on: company size, industry, location, experience
- KL/Selangor typically 10-20% higher than other states
- MNCs typically pay 15-30% above local SMEs
- These are GUIDELINES, not legal requirements
