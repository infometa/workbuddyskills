MALAYSIA OCCUPATIONAL SAFETY AND HEALTH ACT 1994 (Act 514)
Comprehensive Summary for HR & Employer Compliance
================================================================================
Source: DOSH, Nexustac, MyGOV
Last Amended: Occupational Safety and Health (Amendment) Act 2022
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OSHA 1994 is the primary legislation governing workplace safety and health in
Malaysia. It is administered by the Department of Occupational Safety and Health
(DOSH) under the Ministry of Human Resources. The Act applies to ALL industries
except armed forces and domestic services.

2. OBJECTIVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Secure safety, health, and welfare of persons at work
- Protect others against risks to safety/health from work activities
- Establish effective workplace safety systems through self-regulation
- Provide framework for consultation between employers and employees

3. WHO MUST COMPLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ALL industries including:
- Manufacturing, Construction, Agriculture and plantations
- Hospitality, Education and healthcare
- Government and statutory bodies
- Mining and quarrying, Transportation, Retail and offices

4. EMPLOYER DUTIES (Core Obligations)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 General Duties
- Provide and maintain safe systems of work
- Provide safe machinery and equipment
- Ensure safe use, handling, storage and transport of substances
- Provide information, instruction, training, and supervision
- Maintain safe workplace including access and exits
- Provide adequate welfare facilities

4.2 Safety and Health Committee
MANDATORY for companies with MORE THAN 40 EMPLOYEES
- Must facilitate communication on safety matters
- Must meet regularly and keep minutes

4.3 Risk Assessment
- Identify workplace hazards
- Assess associated risks
- Implement control measures
- Use HIRARC (Hazard Identification, Risk Assessment, Risk Control)

4.4 Notification and Reporting
Must report to DOSH:
- Any workplace accident causing death or serious injury
- Any occupational disease or poisoning
- Dangerous occurrences (equipment failure, fire, etc.)
- Report per OSH (Notification of Accident...) Regulations 2004

5. EMPLOYEE RIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Work in an environment free of recognized hazards
- Receive adequate safety training
- Be consulted on safety and health matters
- REFUSE UNSAFE WORK if reasonable belief of imminent danger
- PROTECTED from retaliation for raising OSH concerns

6. PENALTIES (2022 Amendments — SIGNIFICANTLY INCREASED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Failure to ensure safety, health and welfare:
  Fine up to RM 500,000 OR imprisonment up to 2 years OR both

Non-compliance with improvement/prohibition notices:
  Fine up to RM 500,000 OR imprisonment up to 2 years OR both

Failure to establish Safety and Health Committee:
  Fine up to RM 100,000 OR imprisonment up to 1 year OR both

Failure to appoint Safety and Health Officer:
  Fine up to RM 50,000 OR imprisonment up to 6 months OR both

Failure to notify DOSM of workplace occupation:
  Fine up to RM 100,000 OR imprisonment up to 1 year OR both

7. SAFETY AND HEALTH OFFICER (SHO)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Required for certain high-risk industries (construction, major factories, etc.)
- Must be registered with DOSH
- Must hold valid SHO competency certificate
- Responsible for advising employer on OSH compliance

8. DOSH ENFORCEMENT POWERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Conduct workplace inspections at any reasonable time
- Issue Improvement Notices (compliance within specified time)
- Issue Prohibition Notices (immediate cessation of dangerous activity)
- Seize equipment, take samples, interview persons
- Compound offences (offer fines in lieu of prosecution)

9. PRACTICAL STEPS FOR COMPLIANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Appoint competent OSH Coordinator or Officer
- Develop written OSH Policy (mandatory for companies with 5+ employees)
- Conduct regular safety audits and inspections
- Train employees regularly on OSH topics
- Implement Emergency Response Plans
- Keep records of safety training, incidents, and corrective actions

10. RELATED REGULATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- OSH (Safety and Health Committee) Regulations 1996
- OSH (Safety and Health Officer) Regulations 1997
- OSH (Control of Industrial Major Accident Hazards) Regulations 1996
- OSH (Notification of Accident...) Regulations 2004
- OSH (Noise Exposure) Regulations 2019
