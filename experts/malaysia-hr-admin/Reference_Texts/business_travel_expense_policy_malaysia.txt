# Malaysia Business Travel & Expense Policy
## Source: LHDN Public Ruling on Travelling Expenses; IRB entertainment & travel deductibility guidelines; industry benchmarks
## Validity: 2026

---

## 1. MILEAGE CLAIM (里程报销)

### IRB Standard Rates (2026)
| Vehicle Engine Capacity | Rate per KM (MYR) |
|------------------------|-------------------|
| ≤ 1,500cc | 0.50 |
| 1,501-2,000cc | 1.00 |
| > 2,000cc | 1.50 |

- Claims must be supported by mileage log: date, starting point, destination, purpose, odometer reading, km travelled
- Home-office commute is NOT claimable (private expense)
- Toll and parking: reimbursed at actual cost (receipts required)
- Receipts for petrol if using company fuel card; mileage rate covers all vehicle costs

### Company Car vs Personal Car
- If employee uses personal car for work: mileage claim at IRB rate
- If company provides car: no mileage claim; BIK applies (see lhdn_bik_public_ruling_2019.txt)

## 4. ENTERTAINMENT & HOSPITALITY (招待费)

### IRB Deductibility Rules
- Entertainment to clients/customers: 50% deductible (with receipts)
- Entertainment to employees: 100% deductible (e.g., annual dinner, team building)
- Entertainment to suppliers with existing business relationship: 100% deductible
- Entertainment to prospective customers: 50% deductible
- Must record: date, place, purpose, persons entertained, amount, receipt

### Company Policy Guidelines
| Entertainment Type | Max per Person | Approval |
|-------------------|---------------|---------|
| Business lunch | MYR 80-150 | Line Manager |
| Business dinner | MYR 150-350 | Department Head |
| Client gifts | MYR 200-500 (annual per client) | Department Head |
| Team building | MYR 200-500 per pax | CEO/MD |

## 5. EXPENSE CLAIM PROCESS (报销流程)

### Standard Flow
1. Employee submits claim form with original receipts within 30 days of expense
2. Line Manager approves
3. Finance verifies receipts, policy compliance, budget
4. Payment: monthly payroll cycle OR ad hoc reimbursement (next payment run)
5. Late claims (>90 days): rejected unless CEO approval

### Required Supporting Documents
- Original receipts (thermal receipts: photocopy + attach, as they fade)
- For credit card: statement showing charge + merchant slip
- For foreign currency: Bank Negara Malaysia exchange rate on date of expense (or company-designated rate, e.g., monthly average)
- Missing receipts: statutory declaration + manager approval; capped at MYR 200 per item

### Digital Claims
- Mobile apps: SAP Concur, Expensify, Xero Expenses
- Photo of receipt sufficient for digital claims; originals retained for audit
- GST/SST not claimable as input tax unless company is SST-registered and expense is business-related

---

## 6. TAX IMPLICATIONS FOR EMPLOYEES

- Reimbursement of business expenses (supported by receipts): NOT taxable to employee
- Fixed allowance (no receipts): TAXABLE as employment income
- Fixed meal/entertainment allowance: TAXABLE
- Per diem (travel allowance for away-from-base): generally NOT taxable if reasonable and supported by travel records
- See lhdn_bik_public_ruling_2019.txt for full BIK rules

## 7. COMPANY CREDIT CARD (公司信用卡)

### Policy Elements
- Issued to: Directors, Head of Department, frequent travellers
- Credit limit: based on role and travel frequency
- Personal expenses on company card: strictly prohibited → deduction from salary + disciplinary action
- Monthly reconciliation: statement must be reconciled within 14 days with receipts
- Lost/stolen: report to bank immediately; employee liable for unauthorised charges if negligence proven
