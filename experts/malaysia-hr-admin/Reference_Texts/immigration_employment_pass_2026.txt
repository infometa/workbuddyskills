1. LEGAL FRAMEWORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Immigration Act 1959/63 (Act 155): Principal legislation for entry, stay,
  and employment of non-citizens
- Immigration Regulations 1963: Empowers DG of Immigration to issue passes
- Section 6(1)(c): Non-citizens MUST have valid pass; entering without is an offence

2. EMPLOYMENT PASS (EP) — THREE CATEGORIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Category I (Senior Expatriates)
- Minimum salary: RM 10,000+/month
- Validity: Up to 5 years
- Eligible positions: Directors, C-suite, senior management
- Perks: Can hire domestic helper, bring dependants, longer validity
- Family: Dependant Pass available for spouse and children < 18

2.2 Category II (Mid-Level Professionals)
- Minimum salary: RM 5,000 - RM 9,999/month
- Validity: Up to 2 years
- Eligible positions: Managers, professionals, technical specialists
- Family: Dependant Pass available

2.3 Category III (Skilled Technical Staff)
- Minimum salary: RM 3,000 - RM 4,999/month
- Validity: Up to 1 year (renewable max 2 times)
- Eligible positions: Skilled workers, technicians
- Restrictions: More limited, shorter validity, fewer family benefits

3. JUNE 2026 EP THRESHOLD INCREASE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Effective 1 June 2026: HIGHER salary thresholds for ALL new and renewal EP
applications. HR teams MUST audit expatriate roles now and align compensation
offers BEFORE the deadline. Specific new thresholds to be confirmed from
Immigration Department circular.

4. APPLICATION PROCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All EP applications through Expatriate Services Division (ESD):
esd.imi.gov.my

Required documents:
- Expatriate's passport (copy)
- Academic and professional certificates
- Employment contract
- Proof of insurance
- Industry-specific approvals (if applicable)
- Company registration documents

5. RESIDENCE PASS-TALENT (RP-T)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For highly skilled expatriates seeking LONG-TERM career in Malaysia:
- Must have worked in Malaysia ≥ 3 years under EP
- Minimum salary: RM 15,000/month
- Valid for 10 YEARS (renewable)
- Can switch jobs or start businesses WITHOUT fresh approvals
- Family: Dependant Pass aligned with RP-T duration
- Administered by TalentCorp Malaysia

6. PROFESSIONAL VISIT PASS (PVP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For short-term assignments:
- Duration: Up to 12 months
- Purpose: Consultants, trainers, technical experts for limited engagements
- Applicant remains employed by FOREIGN company
- Only authorized for temporary work in Malaysia

7. DEPENDANT PASS (DP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Available for EP and RP-T holders:
- Spouse (legally married)
- Children under 18 years old
- Required documents: passports, marriage/birth certificates (translated to
  English), photographs, principal pass holder's documents
- Valid for same period as principal pass
- Dependants CANNOT work or study without their own permits

8. INVESTOR PASS (2025)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Introduced April 2025:
- Multiple-entry pass
- Stay up to 6 months per entry
- Focus: business-related mobility, NOT residence/retirement
- For global investors and businesspeople

9. GOLDEN VISA SCHEMES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9.1 Malaysia Premium Visa Programme (PVIP)
- 20-year renewable visa
- Requires RM 1 million fixed deposit
- Offshore income: RM 40,000/month

9.2 Malaysia My Second Home (MM2H)
- 5-20 year visas based on fixed deposits and property
- Popular among retirees and families
- Does NOT lead to citizenship

10. EMPLOYER OBLIGATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- SPONSOR expatriate employees through ESD
- Ensure EP applications submitted BEFORE expatriate starts work
- Maintain records of all foreign employees and pass validity
- Renew passes BEFORE expiry
- Report changes in employment status
- Comply with ESD audit requirements
