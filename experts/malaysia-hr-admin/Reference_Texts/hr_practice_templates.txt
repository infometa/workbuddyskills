1. EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT — ESSENTIAL 15 CLAUSES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A legally sound Malaysian employment contract should include:

[1] PARTIES: Full legal name, MyKad number, address of both parties
[2] POSITION & DEPARTMENT: Job title, reporting line, department
[3] DATE OF COMMENCEMENT: Start date (DD/MM/YYYY)
[4] PROBATION PERIOD: ___ months (max 6); confirmation criteria
[5] BASIC SALARY: RM ________ (gross, per month)
[6] ALLOWANCES: Itemised: transport/housing/shift/meal/phone/etc.
[7] WORKING HOURS: ___ hours/day, ___ days/week (max 45 hrs/week)
[8] OVERTIME RATE: 1.5x (weekdays), 2x (rest days), 3x (public holidays)
[9] REST DAY: ________ (one rest day per week)
[10] ANNUAL LEAVE: ___ days (minimum: 8/<2yr, 12/2-5yr, 16/>5yr)
[11] SICK LEAVE: ___ days (minimum: 14/<2yr, 18/2-5yr, 22/>5yr)
     Hospitalisation: 60 days including sick leave
[12] PUBLIC HOLIDAYS: 11 gazetted holidays per year
[13] TERMINATION NOTICE: ___ weeks (minimum: 4/<2yr, 6/2-5yr, 8/>5yr)
[14] STATUTORY CONTRIBUTIONS: EPF, SOCSO, EIS, PCB as per law
[15] CONFIDENTIALITY: Non-disclosure of company information during and after employment

2. SHOW-CAUSE LETTER — STANDARD TEMPLATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[COMPANY LETTERHEAD]
Ref: _________
Date: _________

To: [Employee Name]
    [Position]
    [Department]

RE: SHOW CAUSE LETTER — ALLEGATION OF MISCONDUCT

Dear [Name],

1. ALLEGATION(S)
It has come to our attention that on [DATE] at approximately [TIME], at [LOCATION],
you allegedly [describe specific misconduct clearly with factual details].

2. COMPANY RULES BREACHED
This conduct, if proven, constitutes a breach of the following:
(a) Company Handbook, Section __, Clause __ — [relevant rule]
(b) [Other relevant policies]

3. POTENTIAL CONSEQUENCE
If this allegation is established, you may face disciplinary action up to and
including DISMISSAL.

4. YOUR RESPONSE
You are required to submit a WRITTEN EXPLANATION within [7] days from the date
of this letter, addressing each allegation specifically.

You may provide any DOCUMENTS, WITNESS STATEMENTS, or EVIDENCE in support of
your explanation.

5. SUSPENSION (if applicable)
Pending investigation, you are SUSPENDED from duty on HALF PAY with effect from
[DATE]. This is an administrative measure, not a finding of guilt. If you are
found not guilty, all withheld wages will be restored.

Signed,
[Name]
[Position: HR Manager / Department Head]

CC: Personnel File

3. TERMINATION LETTER — RETRENCHMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[COMPANY LETTERHEAD]
Ref: _________
Date: _________

To: [Employee Name]

RE: NOTICE OF RETRENCHMENT

Dear [Name],

1. RETRENCHMENT
This letter serves as formal notice that your position as [Position] will be
made redundant effective [DATE].

2. REASON
Due to [business restructuring / economic downturn / closure of department],
the Company has determined that your role is no longer required.

3. ALTERNATIVES EXPLORED
The Company has considered [reduced hours / redeployment / VSS] but was unable
to find a viable alternative.

4. FINANCIAL ENTITLEMENTS
Your final entitlements have been calculated as follows:
(a) Salary up to [date]: RM ________
(b) Accrued annual leave (__ days): RM ________
(c) Notice pay in lieu (__ weeks): RM ________
(d) Retrenchment benefit (__ years × __ days): RM ________
(e) EPF/SOCSO/EIS contributions: as applicable
TOTAL NET PAYMENT: RM ________

5. RETURN OF COMPANY PROPERTY
Please return all company property (laptop, access card, mobile phone, etc.)
to the HR Department by [date].

6. CP22A/CP21
For [foreign employees / employees leaving Malaysia], LHDN tax clearance
will be processed.

The Company thanks you for your contributions and wishes you the best.

Signed,
[Name]
[Position: HR Director]

CC: JTKSM (Form PK attached)

4. DOMESTIC INQUIRY — NOTICE TEMPLATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[COMPANY LETTERHEAD]
Ref: _________
Date: _________

To: [Employee Name]

RE: NOTICE OF DOMESTIC INQUIRY

Dear [Name],

1. DOMESTIC INQUIRY
A Domestic Inquiry will be held to investigate the charges against you as
set out in our Show Cause Letter dated [date].

2. DETAILS
Date: _________
Time: _________
Venue: _________

3. CHARGES
[Repeat each charge exactly as stated in show-cause letter]

4. WITNESSES
The following witnesses will be called by the Company:
[1] [Name], [Position]
[2] [Name], [Position]

5. YOUR RIGHTS
You have the right to:
(a) Be present throughout the proceedings
(b) Cross-examine Company witnesses
(c) Present your own evidence and call your own witnesses
(d) Be represented by a COLLEAGUE or UNION representative
    (NOTE: Legal representation is not permitted)

Please provide your witness list at least [3] days before the hearing.

Signed,
[Name]
[Position: Panel Chairperson]

5. PROBATION REVIEW — CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before confirming or extending probation, review:

[ ] Attendance record — any pattern of absence/lateness?
[ ] Performance against KPIs — quantitative and qualitative
[ ] Feedback from direct supervisor — formal written evaluation
[ ] Training completed — any mandatory training outstanding?
[ ] Disciplinary incidents — any warnings issued during probation?
[ ] Cultural fit — team feedback, values alignment
[ ] Confirmation recommendation: CONFIRM / EXTEND (max __ months) / NOT CONFIRM

If NOT CONFIRMING: Issue termination letter with notice pay; ensure valid
reason documented (performance + evidence of coaching/PIP).

6. EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING — 20-POINT CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before Day 1:
[ ] Signed employment contract received
[ ] MyKad/passport copy obtained
[ ] TP1/TP2/TP3 forms collected
[ ] Bank account details for salary
[ ] Emergency contact information

Day 1:
[ ] Office tour and team introduction
[ ] IT setup: email, laptop, system access
[ ] Employee handbook issued (with signed acknowledgment)
[ ] EPF registration (must complete within 7 days)
[ ] SOCSO/EIS registration
[ ] Attendance system setup

Week 1:
[ ] Role-specific training plan issued
[ ] Probation objectives and KPIs communicated in writing
[ ] Buddy/mentor assigned
[ ] Company policies briefing (IT, confidentiality, social media, harassment)

Week 4:
[ ] First 30-day check-in with manager
[ ] Address any concerns or questions
[ ] Confirm probation timeline

Before Confirmation:
[ ] Formal performance review
[ ] Confirmation letter issued (or probation extended with reasons)
[ ] Salary adjustment (if applicable, per contract)
