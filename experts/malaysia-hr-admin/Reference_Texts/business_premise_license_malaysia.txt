# Malaysia Business Premise License & Permits Guide
## Source: DBKL, MBPJ, MBSA official portals; Local Government Act 1976 (Act 171); Street, Drainage and Building Act 1974; Fire Services Act 1988 (Act 341); Uniform Building By-Laws 1984
## Validity: 2026

---

## 1. BUSINESS PREMISE LICENSE (Lesen Premis Perniagaan)

Every business operating from a physical premise in Malaysia MUST obtain a premise license from the local council (Pihak Berkuasa Tempatan / PBT). Operating without one is an offence under the Local Government Act 1976.

### Key Councils & Application Portals

| Council | Coverage | Portal |
|---------|---------|--------|
| DBKL (Dewan Bandaraya Kuala Lumpur) | Kuala Lumpur | www.dbkl.gov.my |
| MBPJ (Majlis Bandaraya Petaling Jaya) | Petaling Jaya | www.mbpj.gov.my |
| MBSA (Majlis Bandaraya Shah Alam) | Shah Alam | www.mbsa.gov.my |
| MPSJ (Majlis Perbandaran Subang Jaya) | Subang Jaya | www.mpsj.gov.my |
| MBPP (Majlis Bandaraya Pulau Pinang) | Penang Island | www.mbpp.gov.my |
| MBJB (Majlis Bandaraya Johor Bahru) | Johor Bahru | www.mbjb.gov.my |
| MBSB (Majlis Bandaraya Seberang Perai) | Seberang Perai, Penang | www.mbsp.gov.my |
| MPK (Majlis Perbandaran Klang) | Klang | www.mpklang.gov.my |

### Application Process
1. Submit application with Form Borang A (varies by council)
2. Required documents:
   - Copy of tenancy agreement or Sale & Purchase Agreement (proof of occupancy)
   - SSM business registration (SSM Form 9 / Certificate of Incorporation)
   - IC of directors/business owner
   - Layout plan of premise
   - Fire Department approval (Bomba) — for certain categories
   - Landlord consent letter
   - Photos of premise (exterior + interior)
3. Inspection by council enforcement officer
4. Approval & payment: RM200-RM2,000/year depending on business type and size
5. Renewal: annually, usually December for the following year

### Processing Time
- Standard: 2-4 weeks
- F&B / high-risk: 4-8 weeks (requires Health Dept + Bomba clearance)
- Rejection reasons: zoning conflict, neighbors objection, incomplete documents

## 2. SIGNBOARD LICENSE (Lesen Papan Iklan / Papan Tanda)

Required by all local councils. Key rules:

### DBKL Signboard Requirements
- Bahasa Malaysia must be on the signboard; other languages allowed but BM text must be at least equal in prominence
- Maximum size: regulated by council (varies by zone)
- Signboard fee: RM200-1,000/year based on size
- Illuminated signboard: additional approval
- Must display business premise license number on signboard

### Common Violations
- Signboard without license: fine up to RM2,000
- BM text too small relative to English/Chinese: council can order removal
- Obstructing public walkway or traffic visibility

## 3. FIRE DEPARTMENT (BOMBA) APPROVAL

Required under Fire Services Act 1988 (Act 341) for:
- Premises with occupancy >40 persons
- Premises handling flammable materials
- F&B with kitchen exhaust/ducting
- Manufacturing / warehouse
- Childcare / education centres

### Process
1. Appoint registered architect/engineer to prepare fire safety plan
2. Submit to Bomba for review
3. Bomba inspection (FC — Fire Certificate issuance)
4. Annual renewal of Fire Certificate (for designated premises under Act 341)

### Key Requirements
- Fire extinguishers (2kg or 4kg ABC type, min 1 per 100sqm)
- Emergency exit signs & lighting
- Smoke detectors
- Sprinkler system (for buildings >18m height or large floor area)
- Annual fire drill for all staff
- Fire Certificate displayed prominently

## 4. MANUFACTURING LICENSE (MIDA)

For manufacturing companies with:
- Shareholders' funds ≥ RM2.5 million, OR
- Full-time employees ≥ 75

Application through MIDA (Malaysian Investment Development Authority):
- www.mida.gov.my
- Manufacturing License under Industrial Coordination Act 1975
- Processing: 4-6 weeks
- Must be obtained BEFORE commencing manufacturing operations
- Exemption: companies below thresholds only need to submit declaration

## 5. LOCAL COUNCIL-SPECIFIC ADDITIONAL LICENSES

### DBKL Additional Requirements
- Zoning confirmation letter (Pengesahan Zoning) before lease signing
- Parking bay allocation (if more than 10 employees, may need dedicated bays)
- Lift inspection permit (JKKP/DOSH) — for buildings with lifts

### Penang (MBPP/MBSB) Additional
- Heritage zone restrictions (George Town UNESCO zone): strict signboard and facade rules
- Coastal zone: EIA may be required

### Johor Additional
- Iskandar Puteri (Medini): special zone rules, often simplified licensing

---

## 7. STATUTORY DISPLAY REQUIREMENTS

The following must be physically displayed at the business premise:
- Certified true copy of Certificate of Incorporation (S30, Companies Act 2016)
- Business premise license (original)
- Signboard license
- Fire Certificate (if applicable)
- EPF/SOCSO/EIS registration certificates
- WRT License (if foreign-owned)
- Minimum Wage Order 2026 notice
- Occupational Safety & Health (OSH) policy (if >5 employees)
- Anti-Sexual Harassment notice (mandatory under Act 840)
