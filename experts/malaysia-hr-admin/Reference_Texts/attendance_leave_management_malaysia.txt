# Malaysia Attendance & Leave Management
## Source: Employment Act 1955 (Act 265), Employment (Amendment) Act 2022, Sabah Labour Ordinance (Cap 67), Sarawak Labour Ordinance (Cap 76)
## Validity: 2026

---

## 1. EMPLOYMENT ACT 1955 COVERAGE

The Employment Act 1955 applies to:
- ALL employees (since 1 Jan 2023 amendment — coverage extended from "≤RM2,000" to all employees)
- EXCEPTION: employees earning >RM4,000/month — certain provisions (overtime, shift work, termination benefits) may not apply (but leave, public holidays, sick leave apply to ALL)
- Sabah: Sabah Labour Ordinance (Cap 67)
- Sarawak: Sarawak Labour Ordinance (Cap 76)
- In practice, companies extend same treatment to all regardless; statutory minimums are the floor

---

## 2. ANNUAL LEAVE (年假) — EA 1955 S60E

| Length of Service | Minimum Days |
|-------------------|-------------|
| < 2 years | 8 days |
| 2-5 years | 12 days |
| > 5 years | 16 days |

- Accrues monthly; pro-rated if service <1 year
- Unused leave: carried forward (company policy; statutory allows max 2 years' carry forward before forfeiture)
- Leave forfeiture upon termination: must be paid in lieu (pro-rated) — EA 1955 S60E(2B)
- Notice: reasonable notice required; company handbook can specify minimum (e.g., 3 days for 1-2 days leave, 1 week for >3 days)

---

## 3. SICK LEAVE (病假) — EA 1955 S60F

| Length of Service | Days (Outpatient) | Hospitalisation Days |
|-------------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| < 2 years | 14 days | 60 days |
| 2-5 years | 18 days | 60 days |
| > 5 years | 22 days | 60 days |

- Total hospitalisation cannot exceed 60 days (includes outpatient sick leave taken in same year)
- Must be certified by registered medical practitioner (or dental surgeon for dental)
- Panel clinic vs non-panel: employer may designate panel clinic; if employee uses non-panel, employer may reimburse at panel rate only
- MC verification: EA 1955 does not mandate MC for 1-day absence (company policy may differ); 2+ consecutive days generally requires MC
- Company policy must be reasonable: demanding MC for 1 day is common but must be stated in handbook

---

## 4. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS (公共假期) — EA 1955 S60D

- Minimum 11 gazetted public holidays per year (5 compulsory + 6 elected)
- Compulsory 5: National Day (31 Aug), Yang di-Pertuan Agong's Birthday, Ruler/Governor's Birthday (state), Labour Day (1 May), Malaysia Day (16 Sep)
- Payment: employee absent without leave on working day immediately before/after public holiday → not entitled to holiday pay
- Working on public holiday: 2x ordinary rate (for < half day), 3x ordinary rate (for > half day)

### State-Level Variations
- Different states observe different holidays (e.g., Thaipusam only in Selangor/Penang/Perak/Johor/N9)
- Sabah/Sarawak have additional state holidays under their Labour Ordinances
- Company must display list of gazetted holidays at beginning of each year

---

## 5. MATERNITY LEAVE (产假) — EA 1955 S37-44 (Amended 2022)

- 98 consecutive days (increased from 60 in 2022)
- Eligibility: employed ≥ 90 days before confinement; employed at any time in 4 months before confinement (for subsequent pregnancies)
- Pay: maternity allowance at ordinary rate of pay for the period
- Cannot terminate a pregnant employee except for: wilful breach of contract, misconduct, or closure of business
- Maternity leave may start 30 days before expected delivery (no earlier)
- Employer may not assign work for 98 days after delivery

---

## 6. PATERNITY LEAVE (陪产假) — EA 1955 S60FA (New — 2022)

- 7 consecutive days for married male employees
- Conditions: married to the mother, employed ≥ 12 months, notified employer ≥ 30 days before expected delivery (or as soon as practicable)
- Limited to 5 confinements (regardless of number of spouses)

---

## 7. UNPAID LEAVE (无薪假)

- No statutory entitlement — entirely company discretion
- Common practice: personal/compassionate leave up to 7-14 days/year (discretionary)
- Impact:
  - EPF: no contribution for month with zero salary (unless employee opts to contribute voluntarily)
  - SOCSO: coverage suspended for unpaid leave month
  - Annual leave accrual: some companies pro-rate; policy must be clear
- Long-term unpaid leave (e.g., >1 month study leave): letter agreement clarifying return terms

## 8. ATTENDANCE SYSTEMS

### Options
| System | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| Biometric (fingerprint) | Accurate, hard to buddy-punch | PDPA concerns, hygiene post-COVID |
| Facial recognition | Contactless, fast | Cost, lighting issues |
| Card (RFID/NFC) | Cheap, easy | Buddy punching possible |
| Mobile app GPS | Remote/hybrid friendly | Privacy concerns (continuous tracking) |
| Manual sign-in | Zero tech cost | Fraud prone, no audit trail |

### PDPA Compliance for Biometric Data
- Biometric data = sensitive personal data under PDPA 2010
- Must obtain explicit written consent
- Must state purpose (attendance only, not surveillance)
- Must destroy within reasonable period after employment ends
- Must NOT share with third parties without consent

## 9. MC VERIFICATION & SICK LEAVE ABUSE

### Company Policy Options
- Panel clinic system: employees visit designated clinics; employer pays directly
- Non-panel: employee pays first, employer reimburses (may cap at panel rate)
- MC verification for frequent short absences: engage Occupational Health Doctor (OHD) for assessment
- "Weekend MC" pattern (Friday/Monday MC): HR may flag as pattern → Domestic Inquiry for misconduct

### Medical Examination by Employer
- EA 1955 S40A: employer may direct employee to undergo medical examination by registered medical practitioner at employer's expense
- Refusal without reasonable excuse = misconduct

## 10. ATTENDANCE ENFORCEMENT

### Tardiness (迟到)
- Deduction from salary only for time actually absent (EA 1955 S24)
- Cannot deduct more than actual time lost
- Repeated lateness: progressive discipline — verbal → written warning → show cause → DI

### Absenteeism (无故缺勤)
- 2 consecutive days absent without notice = deemed to have broken contract (S15(2) EA 1955)
- BUT: Industrial Court requires employer to prove intention to abandon employment
- Process: issue "show cause" letter → if no response, issue reminder → if still no response, domestic inquiry in absentia → termination

### Flexi-Time (灵活工作时间)
- Core hours: 10am-4pm (example); flexi-band: 7am-10am start, 4pm-7pm end
- Weekly cap: max 45 hours (EA 1955 S60A: <5 day week = 9hrs/day, 5 day = 9hrs/day, 6 day = 8hrs/day)
- Overtime: payable only for hours >48/week (or >45 in some interpretations) and at employer's direction
