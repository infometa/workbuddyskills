MALAYSIA PAYROLL COMPLIANCE CALENDAR — ANNUAL HR DEADLINES
Comprehensive Checklist for HR & Payroll Teams

1. MONTHLY OBLIGATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
By 7th:  EPF contribution submission via i-Akaun (recommended early)
By 15th: EPF contribution PAYMENT (mandatory)
By 15th: SOCSO + EIS contribution PAYMENT via PERKESO Assist
By 15th: PCB/MTD tax remittance to LHDN via e-PCB/e-CP39
By 15th: HRDF levy PAYMENT via HRD Corp e-TRiS (if applicable)
Within month: Zakat deductions (if payroll-managed)
Within month: Salary advance/loan deductions

2. QUARTERLY OBLIGATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
By 14th of following month after quarter-end:
- CP39 quarterly summary if applicable
- SOCSO quarterly return (if applicable)

3. ANNUAL OBLIGATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
January:
[ ] Issue EA Forms to ALL employees (deadline: 28 February)
[ ] Prepare EC Forms for non-resident employees (deadline: 28 February)
[ ] Review minimum wage compliance (RM 1,700/month from Feb 2025)
[ ] Update employee TP1 relief forms for new tax year
[ ] Review EPF contribution rates (especially for employees turning 60)
[ ] Update SOCSO contribution table (any changes?)

February:
[ ] Submit Form E (Employer Annual Return) to LHDN by 31 MARCH
    - Form E contains: number of employees, total remuneration,
      PCB remitted, total EPF/SOCSO
[ ] LAST DAY: Submit EA Forms (28 Feb)
[ ] LAST DAY: Submit EC Forms (28 Feb)

April:
[ ] Form E deadline: 31 MARCH (for manual) / 30 APRIL (e-filing)
[ ] Employee personal tax filing deadline: 30 APRIL (if employment income only) /
    15 MAY (e-filing, employment income only)

December:
[ ] Prepare for upcoming year's tax changes (new relief structures, PCB tables)
[ ] Budget for annual increments effective January
[ ] Review all employee files for completeness

4. SPECIAL EVENTS — TRIGGER OBLIGATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NEW HIRE:
[ ] Register with EPF within 7 days
[ ] Register with SOCSO/EIS
[ ] Collect TP1/TP2/TP3 forms
[ ] Verify MyKad (age, citizenship)

RESIGNATION/TERMINATION:
[ ] Final salary calculation (accruals, notice pay, benefits)
[ ] Form CP22A/CP21 for foreign employees (30 days before departure)
[ ] SOCSO cancellation
[ ] EPF withdrawal (employee applies directly)
[ ] Record-keeping: 7 years minimum

FOREIGN WORKER:
[ ] EPF at 2% (mandatory since Oct 2025)
[ ] SOCSO First Category only (Employment Injury)
[ ] Annual levy payment (based on sector)
[ ] PLKS renewal (before expiry)
[ ] FOMEMA medical (annual)
[ ] Insurance (SPIKPA + FWIG)

5. RECORD-KEEPING REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Document              Retention Period    Governing Law
Payroll records       7 years             Income Tax Act 1967
EPF records           6 years             EPF Act 1991
Employment contracts  Duration + 6 years  Employment Act 1955
DI records            7 years             Best practice
Leave records         6 years             Employment Act 1955
OT records            6 years             Employment Act 1955
