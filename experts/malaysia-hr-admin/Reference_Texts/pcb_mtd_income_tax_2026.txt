1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PCB (Potongan Cukai Berjadual = Scheduled Tax Deduction), also called MTD
(Monthly Tax Deduction), is Malaysia's mandatory employer withholding tax
system. Employers deduct income tax from employees' salaries monthly and remit
to LHDN by the 15th of the following month.

PCB is NOT an extra tax — it is an ADVANCE PAYMENT against annual income tax,
spread across 12 months.

2. YA 2025 INCOME TAX BRACKETS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Chargeable Income (RM)    Rate    Cumulative Max Tax
0 - 5,000                 0%      RM 0
5,001 - 20,000            1%      RM 150
20,001 - 35,000           3%      RM 600
35,001 - 50,000           8%      RM 1,800
50,001 - 70,000           14%     RM 4,600
70,001 - 100,000          21%     RM 10,900
100,001 - 250,000         24%     RM 46,900
250,001 - 400,000         24.5%   RM 83,650
400,001 - 600,000         25%     RM 133,650
600,001 - 1,000,000       26%     RM 237,650
1,000,001 - 2,000,000     28%     RM 517,650
Above 2,000,000           30%     RM 517,650 + 30% on excess

NOTE: Chargeable income = Gross salary - EPF - Personal reliefs

3. PCB CALCULATION FORMULA (LHDN 2026 Specification)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MTD for current month = [(P - T) - (Z + X)] / (n + 1)

Where:
P  = Annual tax on estimated chargeable income (using tax brackets above)
T  = Total PCB paid year-to-date (previous months)
Z  = Total Zakat paid year-to-date
X  = Tax rebate (RM 400 if P < RM 35,000; additional RM 400 for spouse)
n  = Remaining months in the year

Net MTD = MTD - current month Zakat

4. TP1, TP2, TP3 FORMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TP1 (Relief Declaration): Submit at start of year or when reliefs change
  - Personal relief: RM 9,000 (automatic)
  - Spouse relief: RM 4,000 (spouse with no income)
  - Child relief: RM 2,000/child under 18; RM 8,000/child in tertiary education
  - Lifestyle relief: RM 2,500 (books, sports, internet)
  - Life insurance + EPF: combined RM 7,000
  - Medical insurance: RM 3,000 (self/spouse/children)
  - Education fees: RM 7,000 (skills/higher education)
  - SSPN savings: RM 8,000

TP2 (Exempt Items): For tax-exempt allowances and benefits-in-kind

TP3 (Previous Employment Income): For employees joining mid-year from
  another employer — must be submitted within 30 DAYS of joining

5. EMPLOYER OBLIGATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Calculate and deduct PCB from employee salaries
- Remit PCB to LHDN by 15th of following month
- Issue Form EA (Borang EA) to ALL employees by 28 FEBRUARY
  (annual summary of salary + PCB + EPF)
- Maintain payroll records for at least 7 years
- Register with LHDN for e-PCB/e-Data PCB submission

6. PAYMENT PORTALS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
e-CP39:    cp39.hasil.gov.my (manual upload via spreadsheet)
e-PCB:     mytax.hasil.gov.my (online manual entry)
e-Data PCB: For payroll software direct upload (larger employers)

7. PENALTIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Late payment: 10% penalty surcharge + interest
- Non-submission: prosecution under Section 107(C) Income Tax Act 1967
- Non-issuance of Form EA: penalties apply
- Incorrect PCB deductions: employer liable for shortfall

8. SPECIAL SITUATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.1 Non-Resident Employees (< 182 days in Malaysia):
  - FLAT 30% withholding tax on GROSS income
  - No personal reliefs apply

8.2 CP38 (LHDN Instruction Order):
  - LHDN issues CP38 to deduct SPECIFIC additional amounts
  - For settlement of outstanding tax arrears
  - Employer MUST comply by law

8.3 Voluntary Extra PCB:
  - Employee may request employer to deduct EXTRA PCB
  - Useful for employees with additional income sources
