# Malaysia Admin & Procurement SOP Essentials
## Source: Companies Act 2016 (Act 777); Malaysian Code on Corporate Governance 2021 (MCCG); PDPA 2010; Treasury Circular PK 2.3 (government procurement benchmark); industry best practices
## Validity: 2026

---

## 1. PROCUREMENT POLICY (采购制度)

### Quotation Thresholds (Recommended for Private Sector, adapted from MOF guidelines)

| Purchase Value | Method | Approval Level |
|---------------|--------|---------------|
| < RM5,000 | Direct purchase (at least 1 quotation) | Department Head |
| RM5,001 - RM50,000 | Minimum 3 written quotations | Department Head + Finance Manager |
| RM50,001 - RM500,000 | Tender / RFP (min 3 bids) | HOD + Finance + CEO/MD |
| > RM500,000 | Open tender (min 5 bids) | Board resolution |

### Vendor Registration & Due Diligence
- SSM check: verify company is active (not dissolved/struck off)
- Bank account verification: company bank account in company name (not personal)
- Track record: last 3 projects/clients (for large contracts)
- Conflict of interest declaration: signed by approver and vendor
- Anti-bribery: vendor must acknowledge Anti-Corruption policy (MACC Act 2009 / S17A corporate liability)
- Blacklist check: MOF blacklist, MACC conviction list

### Purchase Requisition Flow
1. Purchase Requisition (PR) raised by requesting department → line manager approval
2. PR forwarded to Procurement/Admin
3. Quotations sourced and compared (use Quotation Comparison Sheet)
4. Purchase Order (PO) issued → vendor confirmation
5. Goods/services received → Delivery Order (DO) checked against PO
6. Invoice matched to PO + DO (3-way match) → payment processing
7. Payment: standard 30-day credit term; early payment discounts where available
8. Filing: PO + DO + Invoice + Payment Voucher for 7 years (Companies Act 2016 S245)

## 2. FIXED ASSET MANAGEMENT (固定资产管理)

### Asset Register Requirements
Every company MUST maintain a Fixed Asset Register with:
- Asset description and unique ID tag
- Date of acquisition
- Cost (including freight, installation, duties)
- Depreciation method and rate
- Location / assigned custodian
- Disposal date and method (if applicable)

### Depreciation Rates (Common Practice)
| Asset | Useful Life | Annual Rate |
|-------|------------|-------------|
| Office renovation / fit-out | 5-10 years | 10-20% |
| Office furniture & fittings | 5-10 years | 10-20% |
| Computer & IT equipment | 3-5 years | 20-33% |
| Motor vehicles | 5 years | 20% |
| Plant & machinery | 10-20 years | 5-10% |
| Software (off-the-shelf) | 3-5 years | 20-33% |

### Physical Verification
- Annual stock-take (recommended): match physical assets to register
- Missing assets: board report + insurance claim if applicable
- Disposal: board approval for assets > RM10,000 book value; competitive bidding for scrap

### Capitalisation Threshold
- Company policy to set minimum: typically RM2,000-5,000
- Below threshold: expense directly to P&L
- Group purchases (e.g., 20 chairs = RM8,000 total): capitalise as group

## 3. COMPANY STAMP, SEAL & LETTERHEAD (公章管理)

### Common Seal
- Companies Act 2016 S61: optional since 2017 (can execute documents by director + secretary signatures)
- If used: must be kept securely (locked cabinet), custodian appointed
- Usage: register (Common Seal Register) recording date, document type, signatories
- Must be affixed in presence of: 2 directors, OR 1 director + company secretary

### Company Stamp / Rubber Stamp
- Used for routine documents (quotations, letters, minor agreements)
- Types: company name stamp, "Certified True Copy" stamp, "Received" stamp
- Custodian: Admin/Company Secretary
- NOT to be used for contracts requiring board authority

### Official Letterhead
- Must include: company name, registration number (e.g., 202401000001 (1234567-A)), registered office address
- Optional: phone, email, website, logo
- Board resolution issued letterhead should state "By Order of the Board"
- Digital letterhead: use consistent template; protect against unauthorised use

## 4. DOCUMENT RETENTION & ARCHIVING (文档保存)

### Companies Act 2016 S245 — 7-Year Retention Rule
| Document | Minimum Retention | Authority |
|----------|------------------|-----------|
| Accounting records | 7 years from end of financial year | CA 2016 S245 |
| Board & shareholder meeting minutes | Permanently | CA 2016 S343 |
| Register of members/directors/secretaries | While company exists | CA 2016 |
| Employment records | 7 years after employment ends | EA 1955 |
| Tax records | 7 years | Income Tax Act 1967 |
| SST records | 7 years | SST Act 2018 |
| EPF/SOCSO records | 7 years | EPF Act 1991 / SOCSO Act 1969 |
| PDPA-related consents | As long as data used | PDPA 2010 |

### Filing System
- Physical: labelled arch boxes, fireproof cabinet for critical documents
- Digital: named folders (YYYY-MM-DD_DocumentType), cloud backup
- Shredding: confidential documents via cross-cut shredder; engage licensed shredding vendor for bulk

## 5. VISITOR MANAGEMENT & OFFICE SECURITY

### Visitor Policy
- All visitors: sign in at reception (Visitor Log Book)
- Visitor passes: colour-coded (Red = temporary, Blue = contractor, Green = guest)
- ID deposit: exchange for visitor pass (IC/driving license/Passport)
- Host escort: visitors must be accompanied in restricted areas
- Photography: prohibited without prior approval

### Access Control
- Staff access card / biometric: issue upon onboarding, deactivate immediately upon termination
- Zones: general office, server room, finance storage, CEO office — tiered access
- CCTV: common areas; not in private offices/toilets (PDPA compliance)
- Key register: record of who holds keys to which rooms/cabinets
- Fire warden appointment: 1 per floor/zone; annual fire drill

## 6. COMPANY VEHICLES (公司车辆管理)

### Policy Elements
- Eligible employees: typically Sales, General Manager and above
- Petrol card: set monthly limit; personal mileage deduction by payslip
- Maintenance: authorised service centre only; pre-approval for repairs >RM500
- Accident procedure: police report within 24 hours → insurance claim notification within 7 days
- Toll & parking: claim via expense report with receipts
- Mileage log: record business vs personal km for BIK calculation (see lhdn_bik_public_ruling_2019.txt)

### BIK on Company Vehicles (LHDN)
- Car: RM3,000-25,000/year BIK value depending on vehicle cost (see Public Ruling 2/2019)
- Fuel: if company pays for fuel, additional BIK applies (RM600-2,500/year)

## 7. OFFICE SUPPLIES & PANTRY MANAGEMENT

### Procurement
- Monthly stock-take of stationery and pantry items
- Par stock levels: maintain minimum quantities
- Preferred vendor list: pre-approved for common items
- Sustainability: prefer refillable/recyclable; minimise single-use plastic

### Pantry Maintenance
- Daily cleaning schedule
- Halal separation: separate cutlery/storage if halal certification sought
- Pest control: quarterly service for pantry/kitchen areas
