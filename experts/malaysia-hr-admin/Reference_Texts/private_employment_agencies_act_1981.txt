================================================================================
MALAYSIA PRIVATE EMPLOYMENT AGENCIES ACT 1981 (Act 246)
Comprehensive Summary for HR & Employer Compliance
================================================================================
Source: AGC, JTKSM, MOHR
Last Amended: Act A1670 (2024 Amendments, effective Feb 2025)
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Private Employment Agencies Act 1981 regulates the operation of private
employment agencies in Malaysia. All recruitment firms, headhunters, and job
placement agencies must be LICENSED under this Act. 2024 amendments (Act A1670)
INTRODUCED FEES for overseas recruitment.

2. WHO MUST BE LICENSED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Act requires licensing for:
- Local employment agencies (placing workers in Malaysia)
- Overseas employment agencies (placing Malaysian workers abroad)
- Recruitment firms of any size (sole proprietor to large agencies)
- Online job platforms that match employers and employees

Enforcement: JTKSM (Department of Labour Peninsular Malaysia)

3. 2024 AMENDMENTS — OVERSEAS RECRUITMENT FEES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Effective 1 February 2025, Act A1670 introduced:

3.1 Employer-Paid Levy
Employers hiring foreign workers through licensed agents must pay a
STATUTORY LEVY. Amount varies based on:
- Worker's country of origin
- Industry sector
- Number of workers recruited

3.2 Fee Structure Transparency
- Agencies must disclose ALL fees to employer before engagement
- Itemised fee breakdown required
- Maximum fees may be prescribed by Minister
- NO hidden charges permitted

3.3 Worker Protection Provisions
- Agencies CANNOT charge workers for placement (employer pays)
- Passport retention by agency is PROHIBITED (unless worker consents and
  for passport renewal/endorsement purposes only, max 14 days)
- Agencies must verify employer's business legitimacy before placement
- Agencies liable for worker welfare until placement confirmed

4. LICENSING REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To obtain/maintain licence, agency must:
- Register with SSM (Companies Commission)
- Have registered office in Malaysia
- No criminal record of agency directors (fraud, trafficking, etc.)
- Maintain proper placement records
- Display licence at place of business
- Renew licence ANNUALLY
- Pay prescribed licence fee
- Comply with Anti-Trafficking in Persons and Anti-Smuggling of Migrants
  Act 2007 (ATIPSOM)

5. AGENCY OBLIGATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Maintain REGISTER of employers and workers placed
- Provide CONTRACT between employer and worker
- Ensure employment terms comply with Labour Ordinance/Employment Act
- Report to JTKSM if employer breaches terms
- Cooperate with Labour Department inspections
- Do NOT misrepresent job terms to workers
- Do NOT place workers in prohibited sectors

6. EMPLOYER CONSIDERATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When using private employment agencies as an EMPLOYER:
- ALWAYS verify agency licence with JTKSM website
- Get written service agreement with fee breakdown
- Clarify who pays: employer or employee (employer SHOULD pay)
- For foreign workers: ensure agency handles proper documentation
  (work permit, medical, levy)
- Keep records of all payments to agency
- Report unethical agencies to JTKSM

7. PENALTIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Operating WITHOUT licence:
- Fine up to RM 200,000 OR imprisonment up to 3 years OR both

Misrepresenting job terms to workers:
- Fine up to RM 50,000 per offence

Charging workers illegal fees:
- Fine up to RM 50,000
- Licence may be REVOKED

Cancellation of licence:
- Any breach of Act conditions
- Conviction under ATIPSOM 2007
- Protecting public interest

8. RECENT ENFORCEMENT TRENDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Authorities increasingly targeting UNLICENSED foreign worker recruitment
- Joint operations by JTKSM, Immigration, and Police (Ops Patuh)
- Technology being used to track agencies (online portal verification)
- Focus on worker protection — agencies held accountable for worker
  welfare during recruitment process
