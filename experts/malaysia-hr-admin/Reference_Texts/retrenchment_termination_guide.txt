================================================================================
MALAYSIA RETRENCHMENT AND TERMINATION GUIDE
Comprehensive Summary for HR & Employer Compliance
================================================================================
Source: JTKSM, Employment Act 1955, Industrial Court precedents, MEF guidelines
Last Updated: 2026-06
================================================================================

1. TYPES OF TERMINATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Retrenchment (Redundancy)
- Position ceases to exist due to RESTRUCTURING, DOWNSIZING, BUSINESS CLOSURE
- Employee not at fault
- Requires JTKSM notification
- Entitled to termination benefits

1.2 Dismissal for Misconduct
- Employee at fault
- Requires Domestic Inquiry
- Must follow due process
- May or may not have termination benefits (depends on case)

1.3 Poor Performance Termination
- Requires documented performance management process
- PIP (Performance Improvement Plan) strongly recommended
- Must give employee reasonable opportunity to improve

1.4 Frustration of Contract
- Contract becomes IMPOSSIBLE to perform
- Examples: employee imprisoned, licence revoked, death
- No party at fault

2. RETRENCHMENT — EMPLOYER OBLIGATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 JTKSM Notification (MANDATORY)
- Must submit Form PK (Borang Pemberhentian Kerja) within 30 DAYS BEFORE
  intended termination date
- Provide: company name, affected positions, number of employees,
  termination date, reason for retrenchment

2.2 Selection Criteria
Must be FAIR and OBJECTIVE:
- "Last In, First Out" (LIFO) — PRESUMPTIVE RULE
- Employer may deviate from LIFO if:
  - Specific SKILL SET needed for business continuity
  - Performance-based selection (with DOCUMENTATION)
  - Foreign workers first (National Employment Policy)
- Selection criteria must be COMMUNICATED to employees

2.3 Consultation
- Consult affected employees REGARDING retrenchment BEFORE implementation
- For unionised workplaces: consult UNION
- Explore ALTERNATIVES to retrenchment:
  - Reduced working hours
  - Voluntary separation scheme (VSS)
  - Temporary layoff
  - Salary freeze/reduction (with employee consent)
  - Redeployment to other positions/departments

3. TERMINATION BENEFITS (Retrenchment Package)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Employment (Termination and Lay-Off Benefits) Regulations 1980:

Years of Service        Benefit (Month's Wages per Year)
Less than 2 years       10 days' wages
2 - 5 years             15 days' wages
5 years and above       20 days' wages

For incorporated businesses with ≤ 3 employees:
Max 10 days' wages per year of service.

"Wages" = basic salary + fixed allowances (NOT including overtime/commissions)

CONTRACTUAL BENEFITS: If employment contract or collective agreement provides
MORE generous terms, those apply instead.

4. RETRENCHMENT PROCESS — CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Business case documented (financial records, declining revenue, etc.)
[ ] Explored ALL alternatives to retrenchment (documented)
[ ] Selection criteria defined and justified (LIFO or documented exceptions)
[ ] Employee consultation conducted (union if applicable)
[ ] Form PK submitted to JTKSM (30 days before)
[ ] Termination letters prepared with:
    - Reason for retrenchment
    - Effective date
    - Benefits entitlement calculation
    - Appeal process (if any)
[ ] Final salary including:
    - Wages up to termination date
    - Accrued annual leave encashment
    - Retrenchment benefits
    - Notice pay (or notice period served)
[ ] Exit interview conducted
[ ] Return of company property (laptop, access card, etc.)
[ ] Form CP22A/CP21 submitted to LHDN (tax clearance for foreign employees
    or employees leaving Malaysia)

5. NOTICE PERIOD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Employment Act 1955, Section 12:

If EMPLOYMENT LENGTH:            MINIMUM NOTICE:
Less than 2 years                4 weeks
2 - 5 years                      6 weeks
More than 5 years                8 weeks

Contract may specify longer notice period. Employer may pay salary IN LIEU
of notice (payment instead of employee working). If employee waives notice,
employer must STILL pay notice indemnity.

6. TAX CLEARANCE (LHDN)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For employees leaving Malaysia (foreign workers, expatriates):
- Employer must submit Form CP22A at least 30 DAYS before departure
- LHDN issues tax clearance letter
- Employer must WITHHOLD all payments until clearance obtained
- Final PCB calculation and settlement

7. RETRENCHMENT INSURANCE (EIS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Retrenched employees may claim EIS (Employment Insurance System):
- Job Search Allowance: 3-6 months (80%→30% of salary)
- Early Re-employment Allowance: 25% bonus if re-employed
- Training Allowance
- Employee must register with MYFutureJobs

8. VOLUNTARY SEPARATION SCHEME (VSS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Alternative to compulsory retrenchment:
- Employer offers enhanced benefits package for voluntary resignation
- EMPLOYEE CHOOSES to accept (not forced)
- Benefits typically more generous than mandatory retrenchment
- Tax exemption on VSS payments (up to RM 10,000 per completed year,
  restricted to periods ending 31 Dec 2022; after which taxed normally)
- Reduces negative morale and litigation risk

9. MUTUAL SEPARATION SCHEME (MSS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Similar to VSS but employer may INVITE specific employees:
- Selected employees offered package
- Employee may accept or decline
- If declining, may be retained (or later retrenched)
- Document as MUTUAL AGREEMENT with Release & Discharge clause
