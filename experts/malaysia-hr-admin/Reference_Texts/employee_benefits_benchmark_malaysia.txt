## 2. MARKET-PRACTICE BENEFITS (市场通行福利)

These benefits are NOT legally required but are VERY common in the market. Absence may make hiring harder.

### Medical Coverage (Group Hospitalisation & Surgical — GHS)
| Market Penetration | Details |
|-------------------|---------|
| Large MNC (>500 employees) | 100% provide GHS; typically MYR 50,000-100,000 annual limit |
| Mid-sized (100-500) | ~90% provide GHS; MYR 30,000-80,000 |
| SME (<100) | ~60% provide GHS; MYR 15,000-40,000 |
| Startup (<30) | ~30% provide GHS; MYR 10,000-30,000 or outpatient only |

### Outpatient Medical (Clinic Visits)
| Market Norm | Details |
|------------|---------|
| Panel clinic coverage | ~85% of companies provide; unlimited or capped (MYR 600-1,500/year) |
| Non-panel reimbursement | Usually capped at panel rate |
| Dental | MYR 200-500/year (standard); MYR 1,000+ (top-tier) |
| Optical | MYR 150-400/year (standard) |
| Health screening | MYR 200-500/year for employees >40 years old |

### Group Insurance (Life + Personal Accident)
- Group Term Life: 24-36 months' salary (standard); 48-60 months (top-tier MNC)
- Group Personal Accident: same range
- Market penetration: ~80% of professional employers

## 4. BONUS & VARIABLE PAY (花红与浮动薪酬)

### Guaranteed / Contractual Bonus
- 13th month salary (1 month): ~60% of companies provide
- Chinese New Year bonus (ang pow): common in Chinese-owned businesses

### Performance Bonus
| Level | % of Annual Salary (typical) |
|-------|---------------------------|
| Junior (1-3 yrs) | 0.5-2 months |
| Senior / Professional | 1-3 months |
| Manager | 1.5-4 months |
| Senior Manager / Director | 2-6 months |
| Top management | 3-12 months |

### Industry Variance
| Industry | Bonus Range |
|----------|------------|
| Banking & Finance | 3-12 months (highest) |
| Technology (MNC) | 1-4 months |
| Manufacturing | 1-3 months |
| Professional Services (Big 4, law) | 1-3 months |
| Retail / FMCG | 0.5-2 months |
| GLC / Government-linked | 1-3 months |

## 7. TOTAL REWARD COST BREAKDOWN (典型比例)

For a typical professional employee in KL (MYR 6,000/month base):

| Cost Component | MYR/month | % of Base |
|---------------|-----------|-----------|
| Base salary | 6,000 | 100.0% |
| EPF (employer 13%) | 780 | 13.0% |
| SOCSO (employer) | 105 | 1.75% |
| EIS (employer) | 12 | 0.2% |
| HRDF levy (service, 1%) | 60 | 1.0% |
| Medical (GHS + outpatient) | ~150 | 2.5% |
| Group Insurance (GTL + GPA) | ~30 | 0.5% |
| Annual leave 12 days (monetised) | ~277 | 4.6% |
| Public holiday 11 days (monetised) | ~254 | 4.2% |
| Sick leave provision (notional) | ~50 | 0.8% |
| **Total employer cost** | **~7,718** | **~128.6%** |

### Key Insight
- Statutory costs add ~14-15% on top of base salary
- Benefits (medical, insurance, leave) add ~12-15%
- Total employer cost typically 125-135% of base salary for professional employees
