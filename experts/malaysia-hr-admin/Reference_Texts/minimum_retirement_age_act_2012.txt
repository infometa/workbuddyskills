================================================================================
MALAYSIA MINIMUM RETIREMENT AGE ACT 2012 (Act 753)
Comprehensive Summary for HR & Employer Compliance
================================================================================
Source: JTKSM, AGC, AJOBTHING
Effective: 1 July 2013
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Minimum Retirement Age Act 2012 (MRAA) sets the minimum retirement age for
private-sector employees at 60 YEARS OLD. Employers CANNOT require employees to
retire before age 60, unless specific exceptions apply.

2. PROHIBITION OF PREMATURE RETIREMENT (Section 5)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Employers CANNOT force employees to retire before age 60 UNLESS:
- BOTH employer AND employee MUTUALLY AGREE
- The mandatory retirement is consistent with the employment contract
  (contract MUST specify retirement age ≥ 60)

Forcing early retirement WITHOUT mutual consent is ILLEGAL.

3. EXCEPTIONS (EXEMPTED CATEGORIES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MRAA does NOT apply to:
- Federal/State Government, statutory body, and local authority employees
- Apprentices or employees on probationary terms
- NON-CITIZEN employees
- Domestic workers
- Part-time employees working < 70% of full-time hours
- Students employed on temporary basis
- Fixed-term contract employees ≤ 24 months (including extensions)
- Fixed-term contract ≤ 60 months with basic wages RM 20,000+
- Individuals who retired at 55+ before the Act and were re-employed

4. SECTOR EXEMPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Employers in industries where fixed retirement age is impractical may apply
to the Ministry of Human Resources for exemption. This allows flexibility for
physically demanding jobs or roles with special requirements.

5. EARLY RETIREMENT BY EMPLOYEE CHOICE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Employees MAY request early retirement for personal reasons:
- Must negotiate with employer
- Requires MUTUAL AGREEMENT
- Terms should be CLEARLY documented in writing
- Employer should review: employee's reasons, financial impact, business impact

6. PENALTIES FOR NON-COMPLIANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Fine up to RM 10,000 per offence
- Employees may file complaints with JTKSM
- Industrial Court may order reinstatement or compensation

7. KEY CASE LAWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Yeoh Yin Ying v. Saatchi & Saatchi (2013):
  If retirement age NOT specified in contract, employee can work until age 60

- Colgate Palmolive (M) v. Yap Kok Foong (1998):
  Without specified retirement age, employee can work until typical retirement age

8. EMPLOYER OBLIGATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Ensure employment contracts specify retirement age ≥ 60
- DO NOT force employees to retire before 60
- Handle early retirement requests with proper documentation
- Review company HR policies to align with MRAA
- Budget for employees working until 60

9. FUTURE DEVELOPMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Public discussions ongoing about potentially extending retirement age beyond 60
due to longer life expectancy and rising living costs. As of 2026, NO official
changes have been made. The government continues to monitor economic and social
factors.
