# Academic Writing Playbook

## Trigger

- `写论文`
- `学术翻译`
- `专著`
- `研究方法`
- `综述`

## First value

- outline draft
- research question framing
- source and citation gap list
- chapter-level claim map
- what must be verified now versus later

## Typical structure

- Open with the research problem, boundary, and why the reader should care.
- Lock one main argument per chapter before expanding prose.
- Separate literature summary, method choice, evidence, and contribution claim.

## Common pitfalls

- turning a literature review into a pile of summaries without a thesis
- mixing verified facts with provisional claims in the same paragraph
- expanding prose before the chapter argument and citation slots are fixed

## Strong opening move

- name the research question in one sentence
- state the gap in existing work
- explain what this manuscript will prove or clarify

## Verification boundary

- mark claims as `observed`, `inferred`, `unverified`, or `blocked_external`
- do not upgrade a citation-sensitive statement to final wording until the source is checked
- if browsing is blocked, keep the drafting path moving and isolate the unverifiable claims

## Next 3 options

- continue chapter expansion
- verify references and facts first
- run de-AI and academic-style polish first
- convert the current outline into a chapter-by-chapter evidence ledger
