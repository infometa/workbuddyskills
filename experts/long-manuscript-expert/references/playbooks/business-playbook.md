# Business / Marketing Writing Playbook

## Trigger

- `商业文案`
- `品牌故事`
- `方案稿`
- `活动稿`
- `主持稿`

## First value

- target audience summary
- core selling-point structure
- first sample copy block
- offer hierarchy
- proof and objection list

## Typical structure

- define audience, situation, and buying friction first
- state one core promise before stacking sub-points
- connect proof, use case, and call to action in the same flow

## Common pitfalls

- listing product features without an audience-specific outcome
- switching tone between formal proposal, campaign copy, and speech writing
- using abstract value claims without proof, scenario, or measurable signal

## Strong opening move

- open with one audience pain or one immediate gain
- clarify the offer in plain words
- show why the reader should continue now rather than later

## Rewrite checklist

- what must stay
- what should be shortened
- what needs stronger proof
- what should move closer to the call to action

## Next 3 options

- expand the full draft
- rewrite or localize first
- run de-AI polish and rhythm compression first
- turn the current structure into a slide, speech, or channel version
