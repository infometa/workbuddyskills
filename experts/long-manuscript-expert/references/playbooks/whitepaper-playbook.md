# Whitepaper / Industry Report Playbook

## Trigger

- `白皮书`
- `行业报告`
- `趋势分析`
- `市场研究`

## First value

- structured outline
- chart and data-slot checklist
- conclusion hypotheses and validation scope
- executive summary angle
- chapter-by-chapter evidence placeholder list

## Typical structure

- frame the market or industry problem first
- separate trend judgement, evidence, case, and recommendation
- make every chapter answer one board-level or operator-level question

## Common pitfalls

- using trend language without naming the observable evidence
- mixing market facts, opinion, and strategy advice in one section
- delaying the executive summary until the end instead of shaping it early

## Strong opening move

- define the report window, audience, and decision context
- state the main trend judgement in one sentence
- list the 3-5 questions the report will answer

## Evidence boundary

- keep current market facts and reusable framework advice separate
- label what is ready from user material and what still needs external verification
- do not present a current-rule or current-market claim as final unless it has been checked

## Next 3 options

- continue the next chapter
- fill data and case gaps first
- run format and export precheck first
- convert the outline into an executive summary plus evidence ledger
