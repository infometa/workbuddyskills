Update on new regulations in April 2025
Our Newsletter #33 would lead you on the following:
LEGAL UPDATE
I. Issuance of environmental permits
On 06 January 2025, the Government issued
Decree No. 05/2025/ND-CP
amending, supplementing several provisions of Decree No. 08/2022/ND-CP prescribing some provisions of the Law on Environmental Protection (“
Decree 05
”), which takes effect on 06 January 2025, with several important provisions regarding the issuance of environmental permits as follows:
1. Decentralization of the authority for the appraisal of environmental protection impact assessment reports, and the issuance of environmental permits under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment
Decree 05 has supplemented provisions on the delegation of authority to provincial-level People’s Committees for the appraisal of environmental impact assessment reports and the issuance of environmental permits (if the project is subject to environmental permitting) for investment projects that fall under the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment’s jurisdiction for environmental impact assessment appraisal, (except for projects in the following cases: (a) the project is located in areas spanning two or more provincial-level administrative units; (b) the project is located in a marine area where the responsibility for administrative management has not yet been assigned to any provincial-level People’s Committee; (c) the project discharges wastewater into an inter-provincial surface water source that has been publicly announced by the Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment in accordance with regulations on water resources) as follows:
Public investment projects not under the authority of the National Assembly or the Prime Minister to decide or approve investment guidelines, except for projects involving waste recycling or treatment services;
Livestock farming project;
Investment projects for the business operation of livestock and poultry slaughtering facilities;
Projects classified solely based on the criterion of requiring conversion of land use purpose of wet rice cultivation land used for two or more crops per year;
Projects classified solely based on the criterion of requiring conversion of land use purposes for land and water surface areas within nature reserves, natural heritage sites, biosphere reserves, important wetlands, special-use forests, protection forests, and natural forests, and not under the authority of the National Assembly or the Prime Minister to decide or approve investment guidelines;
Investment projects located within concentrated production, business, and service zones or industrial clusters, excluding projects involving hazardous waste treatment services; projects importing scrap from abroad for use as production materials; other projects that fall under production, business, or service types with high risk of environmental pollution and with large capacity as specified in Column (3) of Appendix II of Decree No. 08/2022/ND-CP; and expansion projects of operating facilities that are exempt from wastewater connection requirements under legal provisions and have wastewater volumes subject to periodic environmental monitoring; and
Hydropower projects not under the authority of the National Assembly or the Prime Minister to decide or approve investment guidelines.
2. Additional cases requiring project owners or facilities to adjust environmental permits
Decree 05 has supplemented several cases in which investment project owners or facility operators are required to adjust their environmental permits within the remaining validity period of the permits when falling under one of the following circumstances:
Changes falling under Article 44.2.a of Law on Environmental Protection but not within the scope of Article 30.2 as amended and supplemented by Article 1.12 of Decree 05, except for cases involving a reduction in the permitted environmental content or a change in the volume or type of generated hazardous waste. In cases where the permitted content is reduced, the adjustment shall be carried out upon the request of the investment project owner or facility operator;
Investment projects, facilities, concentrated production, business, and service zones, or industrial clusters undergo changes that increase the scale or production capacity, or modify production technology, resulting in modifications to the permitted contents, except where such changes lead to an increase in adverse environmental impacts;
Operating concentrated production, business, and service zones or industrial clusters add new industries or business lines not included in the list of production, business, and service types posing a risk of environmental pollution as prescribed in Appendix II attached to Decree 05; and
Other changes, except for cases subject to reissuance of the environmental permit.
3. Addition of entities exempted from environmental registration
According to Decree 05, entities exempted from environmental registration include investment projects upon commencement of operation and production, business, or service establishments that either do not generate any waste or simultaneously meet all the following criteria:
Regularly generate less than 20 kg of hazardous waste per month or less than 240 kg of hazardous waste per year;
Regularly generate less than 100 kg of normal industrial solid waste per month or less than 1,200 kg of normal industrial solid waste per year;
Generate less than 300 kg of domestic solid waste per day; and
Generate less than 05 m3 of wastewater per day or less than 50 m3 of emissions per hour, which are treated using on-site treatment facilities or managed in accordance with the regulations of the local government.
II. Bank guarantee activity
On 31 December 2024, the State Bank of Vietnam issued
Circular No. 61/2024/TT-NHNN
prescribing bank guarantee (“
Circular 61
”), which takes effect on 01 April 2025, with some important contents regarding bank guarantee activity as follows:
1. Bank guarantee fees
According to the Circular 61, the bank guarantee fees are regulated as follows:
The credit institutions, and the foreign bank branches shall agree on the bank guarantee fee with customers and relevant parties (if any), and must publicly disclose the applicable guarantee fees;
In case of co-guarantee, the parties participating in the co-guarantee shall agree on the guarantee fee for each co-guarantor;
In case of credit institutions or foreign bank branches guarantee a joint obligation, it shall agree with each customer on the payable fee based on the corresponding joint obligation of each customer, unless otherwise agreed upon by the parties;
If the guarantee is denominated in a foreign currency, the parties may agree to charge the guarantee fee in that foreign currency or convert it into Vietnamese dong at the selling exchange rate applied by the guarantor at the time the fee is charged or at the time of the fee notification; or
The parties may agree to adjust the guarantee fee.
2. Supplementary provisions on dispute resolution in guarantee transactions
Circular No. 61 introduces an additional provision allowing the parties to agree to apply international commercial practices as the basis for determining the applicable law and dispute resolution forum in guarantee transactions.
Specifically
:
While
Circular No. 11/2022/TT-NHNN
only permitted the parties to agree on the governing law and dispute resolution authority if the guarantee transaction involves a foreign element;
Circular 61 expands the scope by allowing the parties to reach such agreements based on the application of international commercial customs, even in cases where the transaction does not involve any foreign element.
3. Validity period of the guarantee agreement and commitment letters for issuance of a guarantee in respect of future-formed residential housing
Circular 61 stipulates the validity period of the guarantee agreement and the commitment letter for the issuance of guarantees for future-formed residential housing as follows:
The guarantee issuance agreement shall be valid from the date of signing until the guarantee obligations of all guarantee letters issued to the buyers have been fully discharged in accordance with regulations, and all obligations of the investor to the guarantor under the guarantee issuance agreement have been fulfilled, unless the parties agree to terminate the agreement earlier; and
The commitment letter for the issuance of the guarantee shall be valid from the date of signing until (a) the completion of the issuance of guarantee letters to all homebuyers prior to the deadline for handover under the sale or lease-purchase agreement; or (b) the expiration of the guarantee issuance agreement (whichever occurs first).
III. Adjustment of monthly salary and income, on which social insurance contributions have been made
On 10 January 2025, the Minister of Labour, War Invalids and Social Affairs issued
Circular No. 01/2025/TT-BLDTBXH
providing regulations on the adjustment rates for salaries and monthly income, on which social insurance contributions have been made (“
Circular 01
”). Circular 01 takes effect from 28 February 2025, and includes several key provisions regarding the adjustment rates applicable to monthly salary and income on which social insurance contributions have been made, as follows:
1. Applicable subjects
Circular 01 provides for the adjustment of monthly salary and income used as the basis for social insurance contributions in the following cases:
The subjects eligible for the adjustment of salary, on which social insurance contributions have been made include:
Employees subject to the salary regime regulated by the State, who commenced participation in social insurance from 01 January 2016 and who are entitled to a retirement pensions or lump-sum allowance upon retirement or lump-sum social insurance allowance, lump-sum social insurance, or whose relatives are entitled to a lump-sum death benefit between 01 January 2025 and 31 December 2025; and
Employees participating in social insurance under a salary regime determined by the employer, who are entitled to a retirement pension, lump-sum retirement allowance, lump-sum social insurance, or whose relatives are entitled to a lump-sum death benefit between 01 January 2025 and 31 December 2025.
The subjects eligible for the adjustment of monthly income on which social insurance contributions have been made include individuals participating in voluntary social insurance who are entitled to a retirement pension, lump-sum retirement allowance, lump-sum social insurance, or whose relatives are entitled to a lump-sum death benefit between 01 January 2025 and 31 December 2025.
