I. SOME IMPORTANT LEGAL ISSUES RELATED TO FINANCIAL STATEMENTS WHICH ARE REQUIRED TO BE AUDITED

1. What is a financial statement audit? What is an audit report?

Pursuant to the Law on Independent Auditing 2011, financial statement auditing is the practice of practicing auditors, auditing firms, and branches of foreign auditing firms in Vietnam checking and giving opinions on the truthfulness and reasonable in material aspects of the audited entity's financial statements according to the provisions of auditing standards.

An audit report is a document prepared by a practicing auditor, an auditing firm, or a branch of a foreign auditing firm in Vietnam after completing the audit, giving opinions on the financial statements and other matters. Other content has been audited according to the audit contract.

2. Financial reports are required to be audited

Pursuant to the Law on Independent Auditing 2011, financial reports that are required to be audited include:

(1) Annual financial report;

(2) Completed project settlement report;

(3) Financial information of the audited unit includes:

- Enterprises and organizations whose annual financial statements are required by law to be audited by auditing firms or branches of foreign auditing firms in Vietnam, including:

+) Enterprises with foreign investment capital;

+) Credit institutions established and operating under the Law on Credit Institutions 2010;

+) Financial institutions, insurance businesses, insurance brokerage businesses.

+) Public companies, issuing organizations and securities trading organizations.

- Enterprises and organizations must be audited by auditing firms and branches of foreign auditing firms in Vietnam, including:

+) State-owned enterprises, except state-owned enterprises operating in the field of state secrets as prescribed by law, must have their annual financial statements audited;

+) Enterprises and organizations implementing important national projects and group A projects using state capital, except for projects in the field of state secrets that must be audited according to the law, completed project settlement report;

+) Enterprises and organizations with State capital contributions and other projects using state capital prescribed by the Government must have their annual financial statements or completed project settlement reports audited;

+) Auditing firms and branches of foreign auditing firms in Vietnam must be audited for annual financial statements.

Thus, for reports falling into the above cases, mandatory audits must be conducted according to the provisions of the law.

3. Regulations on professional examination of the organization

Pursuant to Decree No. 17/2012/ND-CP, auditing organizations are social and professional organizations of auditors, professional auditors, and auditing firms throughout the country. And the professional organization examined:

(1) Architectural training for audit staff and practicing auditors;

(2) Carry out research, editing and updating the Vietnamese auditing standards system on the basis of the international auditing standards system according to the process of developing, promulgating and working on Vietnamese Auditing Standards Nam, submitted to the Ministry of Finance for promulgation;

(3) Participate in organizing exams;

(4) Coordinate with the main inspection of the Ministry of Finance and control the quality of service inspection.

Thus, the auditing practice organization has been established, operates according to the provisions of social law and has a sense of responsibility according to the provisions of law on independent auditing.

IV. SOME QUESTIONS RELATED TO FINANCIAL STATEMENTS WHICH ARE REQUIRED TO BE AUDITED

Question 1: What is the latest deadline to conclude a financial statement audit contract?

Reply:

Pursuant to the Law on Independent Auditing 2011, contracts to audit annual financial statements of enterprises and organizations required to audit financial statements must be concluded no later than 30 days before the end of annual accounting the period. .

Question 2: What principles should be followed when conducting independent audit activities?

Reply:

Pursuant to the Law on Independent Auditing 2011, when conducting audit activities, the person conducting them must comply with the following principles:

(1) Comply with the law and be responsible before the law for professional activities and audit reports;

(2) Comply with auditing standards and professional ethical standards of accounting and auditing in Vietnam; For audit work under an audit contract that requires the application of other audit standards, that audit standard must be complied with;

(3) Independence, honesty, objectivity;

(4) Information security.

Thus, when conducting independent audit activities, the auditor must ensure full compliance with the principles prescribed by law.

Question 3: What kind of people are not allowed to register to practice as auditors?

Reply:

Pursuant to the Law on Independent Auditing 2011, the following people are not allowed to register to practice auditing:

(1) Officials, civil servants and public employees;

(2) People in the following cases:

- Being banned from practicing auditing under a legally effective court judgment or decision;

- Being prosecuted for criminal liability;

- Have been convicted of one of the economic or position-related crimes related to finance or accounting but the sentence has not been expunged;

- People who are being subjected to administrative measures of education in communes, wards, towns, sent to medical treatment facilities, or sent to educational facilities.

(3) People with a criminal record for serious economic crimes or more;

(4) People who violate the laws on finance, accounting, auditing and economic management shall be administratively sanctioned within one year from the date of the sanctioning decision;

(5) Persons suspended from practicing auditing.

Thus, if an individual falls into one of the above cases, he or she will not be qualified to register to practice auditing according to the provisions of law.
