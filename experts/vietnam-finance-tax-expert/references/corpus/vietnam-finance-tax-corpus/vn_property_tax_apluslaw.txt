What is Property Tax in Vietnam?
Currently, Vietnamese law does not have specific regulations regarding a Property Tax within its legal system. However, taxes related to property have been implemented for many years, including:
Non-Agricultural Land Use Tax
: Regulated by the
Law on Non-Agricultural Land Use Tax 2010
, applied to individuals and organizations using non-agricultural land. This tax is levied based on the area and value of the land used.
Personal Income Tax on Real Estate Transfers
: According to the
Law on Personal Income Tax 2007 (amended and supplemented in 2014)
, individuals who transfer real estate are required to pay personal income tax.
Registration Fee
: Under
Decree No.10/2022/ND-CP
, the registration fee is applicable when registering ownership of property (such as real estate or vehicles).
Although there is no formal property tax system in place, Vietnam is considering the implementation of a property tax, particularly on real estate and high-value assets.

Where to pay Property Tax in Vietnam?
Individuals and organizations are required to pay property taxes or taxes related to property at the local tax authorities where the property is located, or where the individual or organization resides or operates. These authorities may include:
District Tax Office
: This authority is responsible for collecting taxes related to property, such as non-agricultural land use tax and personal income tax on real estate transfers.
State Treasury
: In certain cases, taxes may be paid directly to the State Treasury.
General Department of Taxation’s Electronic Portal
: Nowadays, online tax payment has become common, making the process more efficient and saving time for taxpayers.
General Department of Taxation’s Electronic Portal.
What is the penalty for not paying Property Tax in Vietnam?
According to the provisions of the
Law on Tax Administration 2019
, if individuals or organizations fail to pay taxes related to property on time, they will be subject to administrative penalties or tax enforcement measures, including:
Late payment penalty
: According to
Article 59 of the Law on Tax Administration 2019
, the penalty for late payment is
0.03%
of the outstanding tax amount for each day of delay.
Administrative penalties for tax violations
: A penalty ranging from
10% to 20%
of the unpaid tax amount will be imposed if there is an act of underreporting the tax payable, as stipulated in
Article 141 of the Law on Tax Administration 2019
.
Tax enforcement
: Enforcement measures such as deducting funds from bank accounts, seizing assets, and suspending the use of invoices are stipulated in
Articles 124 and 125 of the Law on Tax Administration 2019
. These measures are applied when the taxpayer deliberately fails to fulfill their tax obligations.
