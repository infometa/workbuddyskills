Abstract
This Law, consisting of 80 articles divided into five Chapters, provides the thrift practice and waste prevention in: the management and use of state budget, state capital, state property, labour and working time in the state sector; the management, exploitation and use of resources; production, business and consumption activities of organizations, households and individuals. This Law applies to agencies, organizations, households and individuals managing, exploiting and using resources, as well as other organizations, households and individuals. It aims at the reduction of waste use of capital, property, labour, working time and resources while the set targets are still achieved.
