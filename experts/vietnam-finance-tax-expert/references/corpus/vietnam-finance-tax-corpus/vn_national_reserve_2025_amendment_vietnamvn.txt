Announcement of the President's Order on 12 Laws
On the morning of January 6th, at the Presidential Palace, the Office of the President held a press conference to announce the President's Order regarding 12 laws that were passed by the 10th Session of the 15th National Assembly.

These 12 laws include: the Personal Income Tax Law; the Tax Administration Law; the Law amending and supplementing certain articles of the Value Added Tax Law; the Law on Saving and Combating Waste; the Planning Law; the Investment Law; the National Reserve Law; the Law amending and supplementing certain articles of the Public Debt Management Law; the Law amending and supplementing certain articles of the Statistics Law; the Law amending and supplementing certain articles of the Price Law; the E-commerce Law; and the Law amending and supplementing certain articles of the Insurance Business Law.
Deputy Head of the Office
of the President
, Assistant to the President Can Dinh Tai, chaired the press conference.
Taxpayer classification
The Personal Income Tax Law consists of 4 chapters and 29 articles, and will take effect from July 1, 2026; however, the provisions related to income from business activities and salaries and wages of resident individuals will apply from the 2026 tax year.
Regarding new points, the Law stipulates a personal allowance of VND 15.5 million per month for the taxpayer themselves, and a allowance of VND 6.2 million per month for each dependent. At the same time, it mandates the Government to submit to the National Assembly Standing Committee proposals to adjust this allowance based on fluctuations in prices and income (no longer requiring adjustments only when the consumer price index fluctuates by more than 20%) to ensure flexibility and suitability to the
socio
-economic situation in each period.
The law has amended regulations on personal income tax for individual businesses, adjusting the tax-exempt revenue threshold for household and individual businesses from VND 200 million/year to VND 500 million/year and allowing this amount to be deducted before calculating tax based on a percentage of revenue.
Notably, the Law has added a method for calculating income tax (revenue - expenses) for household and individual businesses. Accordingly, household and individual businesses with annual revenue from over 500 million VND to 3 billion VND will apply a tax rate of 15% on income; household and individual businesses with annual revenue from over 3 billion VND to 50 billion VND will apply a tax rate of 17% on income; and household and individual businesses with annual revenue over 50 billion VND will apply a tax rate of 20% on income. Individuals with annual revenue from over 500 million VND to 3 billion VND can choose to have their tax calculated based on a percentage of revenue or based on income.
Individuals who rent out real estate, excluding accommodation businesses, pay personal income tax determined by multiplying the portion of annual revenue exceeding 500 million VND by a tax rate of 5%. Additionally, the tax rate for certain income from providing digital content products and services related to entertainment and video games has been adjusted from 2% to 5%.
Also effective from July 1, 2026, the Law on Saving and Combating Waste consists of 6 chapters and 38 articles; aiming to create a comprehensive, unified, clear, and effective legal framework for saving and combating waste; contributing to preventing, deterring, and handling wasteful acts. At the same time, it aims to raise awareness and a sense of responsibility in saving and combating waste and build a culture of saving throughout society. This will improve the efficiency of managing and utilizing national resources, contributing to achieving socio-economic development goals, improving people's lives, and building a prosperous nation. May 31st each year is designated as "National Saving and Combating Waste Day".
The Law on Tax Administration comprises 9 chapters and 53 articles. The regulations on taxpayer classification are one of the important new points of the Law; it adds the principle that tax authorities manage taxpayers according to their classification. This is an important legal basis for tax authorities to shift from the previous functional tax management method to a management method based on taxpayers combined with functions. At the same time, the Law adds provisions for household businesses and individual businesses to declare and calculate taxes based on a percentage of revenue, tax rates, and tax calculation methods as stipulated in tax laws, with support from tax authorities in tax declaration based on the tax authority's database and information provided by taxpayers. It also adds provisions on the principle of international cooperation and international tax management to meet the requirements of international cooperation and protect Vietnam's legitimate tax rights… The Law takes effect from July 1, 2026. Specifically, the provisions in Article 13 and the use of electronic invoices by household businesses and individual businesses in Article 26 of this Law shall take effect from January 1, 2026.
Effective from January 1, 2026, the Law amending and supplementing several articles of the Value Added Tax Law consists of two articles. The amended Value Added Tax Law aims to improve regulations on value added tax policies for agricultural products, animal feed, and conditions for value added tax refunds, thereby removing bottlenecks and immediately addressing urgent issues, obstacles, and inadequacies arising from practice. This will encourage and create momentum for the development of businesses, especially those in the agricultural sector, contributing to economic development.

Ensuring the economy operates stably and efficiently.
The Planning Law comprises 6 chapters and 58 articles. The law has refined regulations on the planning system, the relationship between different types of planning, and the handling of conflicts between plans; strengthened decentralization and delegation of authority in planning activities; simplified processes and procedures in planning activities; improved the content of planning; and addressed difficulties and obstacles related to assessing the conformity of investment projects with planning...
Notably, regarding planning, the Law delegates to the Ministry the authority to organize the preparation of national marine spatial planning, national land use planning, and regional planning; it also delegates to the Government the authority to identify areas requiring regional planning to shorten the time required for regional planning. Regarding plan approval, the Government stipulates the authority to approve sectoral plans to ensure flexibility in the Government's direction and management. It delegates to the Minister the authority to approve detailed sectoral plans within their scope of management; and delegates to the Chairman of the Provincial People's Committee the authority to approve provincial plans and detailed sectoral plans within their province.
The Investment Law comprises 7 chapters and 52 articles. Regarding prohibited and conditionally permitted investment and business sectors, the Law has added provisions prohibiting the business of e-cigarettes and heated tobacco products, and assigns the Government the authority to regulate the handling of investment projects for the production of electronic devices for e-cigarettes and heated tobacco products in Vietnam solely for export, which have been registered or approved in writing by competent state agencies in accordance with the law before January 1, 2025, in order to implement Resolution No. 173/2024/QH15 dated November 30, 2024, of the National Assembly.
The law has reviewed and reduced 39 conditional investment and business sectors that do not meet the criteria and conditions stipulated in Article 7 of the Investment Law, and amended the scope of 20 conditional investment and business sectors to shift strongly from a "pre-approval" to a "post-approval" mechanism, ensuring the freedom of investment and business for enterprises.
Both laws will come into effect on March 1, 2026.
The Law on National Reserves comprises 6 chapters and 36 articles, effective from July 1, 2026; provisions on strategic reserves will take effect from January 1, 2027. Notably, regarding the objectives of national reserves, the Law adds the following goals: ensuring social security, serving socio-economic development and the activities of the Party and State; mobilizing, exploiting, and effectively utilizing the resources of the economy to implement national strategic autonomy; and serving as a tool to regulate the market to ensure the economy operates stably and efficiently according to market laws and socialist orientation.
The Law on National Reserves has added provisions stipulating that units, organizations, and enterprises participating in strategic reserves are entitled to tax incentives, credit, and other preferential policies as prescribed by the Government in each period; ensuring the legitimate rights and interests of the State and all participants in strategic reserves…
The Law amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Law on Public Debt Management and the Law amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Law on Statistics will both take effect from January 1, 2026.
To further implement the policy of decentralization and delegation of power, coupled with the streamlining of organizational structures and improved governance efficiency, the Law amending and supplementing several articles of the Law on Public Debt Management has stipulated that the Prime Minister and the Ministry of Finance are empowered to handle certain debt management tasks, enhancing the proactive role and accountability of local governments in deciding and utilizing their budgets. This includes clarifying the duties and powers of the President and the Government, and amending and supplementing the powers and duties of the Prime Minister and the Ministry of Finance…
Similarly, the drafting of the Law amending and supplementing certain articles of the Statistics Law aims to create a legal framework for statistical activities; provide accurate, objective, and timely statistical information that correctly and fully reflects the socio-economic situation in each period; assist in analyzing, planning, and managing macroeconomic policies; and ensure international comparability in various fields…
Protecting the legitimate interests of businesses and consumers.
The Law on E-commerce consists of 7 chapters and 41 articles, and will come into effect on July 1, 2026. The law is developed with a focus on fostering development, enhancing management efficiency, and protecting the legitimate interests of businesses and consumers.

Press conference announcing the President's Order on 13 laws.
On the afternoon of January 5th, at the Presidential Palace, the Office of the President held a press conference to announce the President's Order regarding 13 laws that were passed by the 10th Session of the 15th National Assembly.
Notably, the Law adds regulations on livestreaming sales and affiliate marketing – new provisions aimed at filling legal gaps. Accordingly, platform owners are responsible for electronically verifying the identity of livestreamers, establishing a mechanism for receiving and resolving complaints throughout the livestreaming process, and storing image and audio data as required. Livestreamers are prohibited from providing false or misleading information about the quality or uses of goods. For affiliate marketing activities, service providers must verify the identity of marketers and are responsible for blocking links to goods and services that violate the law.
To strengthen the management of cross-border e-commerce activities, the Law stipulates that foreign e-commerce platforms using the Vietnamese language, with a “.vn” domain name, or reaching a certain transaction threshold in Vietnam must establish a legal entity or appoint an authorized representative in Vietnam. For foreign platform owners without a presence in Vietnam, the Law requires them to deposit funds in a bank to ensure compensation for consumer damages and to fulfill all financial obligations to the State, ensuring fairness between domestic and foreign entities, in accordance with international practices and commitments…
The Law amending and supplementing several articles of the Law on Prices consists of two articles. Regarding price stabilization, to ensure consistency with the two-tiered local government model, the amended Law on Prices transfers the authority to organize and implement price stabilization from the district-level People's Committee (based on the assignment of the provincial-level People's Committee) to the commune-level People's Committee. The Law on Prices and Government Decree 85/2024/ND-CP have detailed the procedures for organizing and implementing price stabilization at the local level for each specific case, with the primary responsibility for advising the provincial-level People's Committee resting with the relevant sectoral management department. Therefore, the decentralization mechanism in price stabilization under the Law creates flexibility and proactiveness for localities in implementing price stabilization within their areas, and is consistent with local conditions and resources.
The Law amending and supplementing several articles of the Law on Insurance Business consists of 3 articles. The Law has reduced and simplified business conditions and administrative procedures to create a transparent business environment and encourage the development of the private economy; amendments and supplements are made to suit practical realities and resolve difficulties encountered in the past. The amendments and supplements are also made to ensure consistency and uniformity with related legal systems...
Both laws will come into effect on January 1, 2026.
