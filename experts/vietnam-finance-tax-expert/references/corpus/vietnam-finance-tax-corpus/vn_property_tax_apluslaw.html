Property Tax in Vietnam

Although Property Tax is a prevalent tax in the United States and other countries, Vietnam Law does not incorporate this tax. Nevertheless, the tax paid per unit of products sold is diverse, including Registration Fee, Value-added Tax, Excise Tax, etc. Learn about this special tax now with Apluslaw!

What is Property Tax in Vietnam?

Currently, Vietnamese law does not have specific regulations regarding a Property Tax within its legal system. However, taxes related to property have been implemented for many years, including:

  • Non-Agricultural Land Use Tax: Regulated by the Law on Non-Agricultural Land Use Tax 2010, applied to individuals and organizations using non-agricultural land. This tax is levied based on the area and value of the land used.
  • Personal Income Tax on Real Estate Transfers: According to the Law on Personal Income Tax 2007 (amended and supplemented in 2014), individuals who transfer real estate are required to pay personal income tax.
  • Registration Fee: Under Decree No.10/2022/ND-CP, the registration fee is applicable when registering ownership of property (such as real estate or vehicles).

Although there is no formal property tax system in place, Vietnam is considering the implementation of a property tax, particularly on real estate and high-value assets.

Property Tax Rates

Tax rates on property depend on the specific tax related to the property that the owner is required to pay, as follows:

Non-Agricultural Land Use Tax: According to Article 7 of the Law on Non-Agricultural Land Use Tax 2010, the tax rate ranges from 0.03% to 0.15% of the taxable land value, depending on the land’s purpose of use (residential land, commercial land, services, etc.).

Specifically, in Article 7 of the Consolidated Document No. 10/VBHN-BTC of the Circular guiding the non-agricultural land use tax, the rates are as follows:

“Article 7. Tax Rates

1. Residential land: 

a) For residential land, including land used for business purposes, the progressive tax rate schedule is as follows:

TAX BAND TAXABLE LAND AREA (m²) TAX RATE (%)
1 Area within the limit 0.03
2 Area exceeding the limit by up to 3 times 0.07
3 Area exceeding 3 times the limit 0.15

b) For residential land in multi-storey buildings, multi-household buildings, apartment buildings, and underground constructions, the applicable tax rate is 03%.

2. Non-agricultural production and business land, and non-agricultural land as prescribed in Article 2 of this Circular used for business purposes, are subject to a tax rate of 0.03%.

3. Land used for improper purposes or land not used in accordance with regulations is subject to a tax rate of 0.15%.

4. Land in investment projects with phased registration as approved by the competent state authority is subject to a tax rate of 0.03%.

5. Encroached or occupied land is subject to a tax rate of 0.2%.”

Personal Income Tax on Real Estate Transfers: The fixed tax rate is 2% of the real estate transfer contract value, according to Article 17 of Circular 92/2015/TT-BTC, guiding the implementation of several provisions of the Law on Personal Income Tax and Decree 65/2013/ND-CP.

Registration Fee: For real estate, the registration fee is 0.5% of the asset value. For vehicles such as cars and motorcycles, the registration fee can be up to 2% of the vehicle’s value (Article 8 of Decree 10/2022/ND-CP).

“Article 8. Registration fee rates (%)

1. House, land: 0.5%.

2. Hunting rifles; guns for sports training and competition: 2%.

3. Watercraft, including also barges, canoes, tugboats, pushers, submarines and submersibles; boats, including also yachts; aircraft: 1%.

4. Motorcycles: 2%

5. For automobiles, trailers or semi-trailers towed by automobiles or vehicles similar to automobiles: 2%.”

Where to pay Property Tax in Vietnam?

Individuals and organizations are required to pay property taxes or taxes related to property at the local tax authorities where the property is located, or where the individual or organization resides or operates. These authorities may include:

  • District Tax Office: This authority is responsible for collecting taxes related to property, such as non-agricultural land use tax and personal income tax on real estate transfers.
  • State Treasury: In certain cases, taxes may be paid directly to the State Treasury.
  • General Department of Taxation’s Electronic Portal: Nowadays, online tax payment has become common, making the process more efficient and saving time for taxpayers.

What is the penalty for not paying Property Tax in Vietnam?

According to the provisions of the Law on Tax Administration 2019, if individuals or organizations fail to pay taxes related to property on time, they will be subject to administrative penalties or tax enforcement measures, including:

  • Late payment penalty: According to Article 59 of the Law on Tax Administration 2019, the penalty for late payment is 0.03% of the outstanding tax amount for each day of delay.
  • Administrative penalties for tax violations: A penalty ranging from 10% to 20% of the unpaid tax amount will be imposed if there is an act of underreporting the tax payable, as stipulated in Article 141 of the Law on Tax Administration 2019.
  • Tax enforcement: Enforcement measures such as deducting funds from bank accounts, seizing assets, and suspending the use of invoices are stipulated in Articles 124 and 125 of the Law on Tax Administration 2019. These measures are applied when the taxpayer deliberately fails to fulfill their tax obligations.

Property Tax consulting service for foreigners

Foreign investors in Vietnam or those who own property in Vietnam must comply with property tax regulations similar to those applicable to Vietnamese nationals.
