VIETNAM 2022 PETROLEUM LAW - TAX INCENTIVES (Deloitte)

Vietnam's Petroleum Law (no. 12/2022/QH15) was officially approved at the 4th Session, 15th National Assembly on 14 November 2022. It contains 11 chapters and 69 articles and is effective as from 1 July 2023.

KEY TAX INCENTIVES:
- Incentivized petroleum contract: CIT rate 32%, crude oil export tax 10%, cost recovery up to 70%
- Special investment incentive petroleum contract: CIT rate 25%, crude oil export tax 5%, cost recovery up to 80%
- Contract term: Normal 30 years (exploration ≤5 years); Incentivized 35 years (exploration ≤10 years); Extension up to 5 years
- PSC remains primary contract type, but other types allowed
- New mechanism: full exploitation, synchronous chain, basic petroleum investigation
- PVN (PetroVietnam) rights clearly stated as contractor
- Accounting/auditing per petroleum law + international practices
- CIT determined based on petroleum contracts regardless of enterprise business results

PETROLEUM CONTRACT TERMS:
- Normal petroleum project contracts: 30 years, exploration ≤5 years
- Incentivized petroleum projects: 35 years, exploration ≤10 years
- Extension: up to 5 years (inclusive exploration phase)
- PSC contract: production sharing, contractor bears all financial/technical risk
- Synchronous chain: may build outside initial contract area to improve efficiency
- Full exploitation: PM decides mechanism; PVN responsible

BASIC PETROLEUM INVESTIGATION:
- MOIT prime responsibility, coordinates with MONRE
- Funding: state budget, PVN budget, organizations/individuals funds

ACCESS RIGHTS AND SHARING:
- Contractors may access existing infrastructure
- Must share infrastructure with third parties per contracts/agreements

PVN RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS:
- Clearly stated to determine PVN's roles in performing government tasks
- Participating in petroleum contract as contractor

DECENTRALIZATION:
- MOIT and PVN approve deployment stages
- PVN members' council approves use of capital
