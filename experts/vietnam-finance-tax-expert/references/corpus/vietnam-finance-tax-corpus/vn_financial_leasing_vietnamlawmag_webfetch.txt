NEW REGULATION ON OPERATION OF FINANCE AND FINANCIAL LEASING COMPANIES
Source: Vietnam Law Magazine - vietnamlawmagazine.vn
Published: Thursday 07/03/2014

Decree No. 39/2014/ND-CP of May 7, 2014

FINANCE COMPANY OPERATIONS:
- Banking operations (reflected in SBV license)
- Issuance of deposit certificates, promissory notes, bills or bonds
- Borrowing from domestic/overseas credit institutions
- Borrowing from SBV (refinancing)
- Provision of loans (consumer loans, installment loans)
- Provision of bank guarantees
- Issuance of credit cards
- Financial leasing
- Other operations

GENERAL CONDITIONS FOR BANKING OPERATIONS:
(i) Banking operations reflected in SBV license
(ii) Adequate qualified staff, facilities, technologies, instruments and internal rules
(iii) Compliance with foreign exchange management regulations for FX-related operations
(iv) Satisfaction of professional requirements prescribed by SBV

FINANCIAL LEASING COMPANY OPERATIONS:
May conduct all operations specified in Articles 112-116 of the Law on Credit Institutions and this Decree.

DETAILED PROVISIONS ON FINANCIAL LEASING:
- Rights and obligations of parties involved in financial leasing
- Termination of financial leasing contracts ahead of time
- Registration of financial leasing contracts
- Certificates of ownership of leased assets

EFFECTIVE DATE: June 25, 2014
REPLACES: Decrees 79/2002, 81/2008, 16/2001, 65/2005, 95/2008

KEY DEFINITIONS (Article 3):
- General financial company: may perform all activities under Law on Credit Institutions
- Specialized financial companies: factoring, consumer credit, financial leasing
- Financial leasing: medium/long-term credit based on financial leasing contracts; lessor buys assets at lessee's request, holds ownership during lease term
- Purchase and sub-lease: lessor purchases assets from lessee and sub-leases them back
- Financial leasing debit balance must be at least 70% of total credit balance for financial leasing companies

RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF LESSOR:
- Owns leased assets during lease term (not affected by lessee bankruptcy/dissolution)
- May require deposit and security measures
- May inspect management and use of leased assets
- May transfer rights/obligations to another financial leasing party
- Must assess financial capacity/reputation of provider and leased assets
- Must buy and import leased assets as agreed
- Must register ownership and buy insurance

RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS OF LESSEE:
- May receive and use leased assets
- May decide to purchase or continue leasing after lease term
- Must use assets for proper purpose
- Must pay rent and other expenses
- Bears risk of loss of leased assets
- Must not use leased assets for mortgages or securing other obligations

EARLY TERMINATION CONDITIONS:
1. Lessee does not pay rental or breaches conditions
2. Lessee is declared bankrupt or dissolves
3. Lessor breaches conditions
4. Leased assets are lost/damaged and cannot be restored
5. Lessee pays rental for remaining term
