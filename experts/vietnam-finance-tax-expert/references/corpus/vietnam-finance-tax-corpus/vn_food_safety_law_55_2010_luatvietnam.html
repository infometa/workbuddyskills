Short trading of VAT invoices may be criminally handled

Thông tư Liên tịch số 21/2004/TTLT-BCA-TANDTC-VKSNDTC-BTP ngày 23/01/2004 của Bộ Công an - Bộ Tư pháp Hướng dẫn việc truy cứu trách nhiệm hình sự đối với các hành vi mua, bán, sử dụng trái phép hóa đơn giá trị gia tăng (GTGT) (3 tr.)
Posted: 29/7/2010 9:01:00 AM | Latest updated: 9/7/2013 9:36:18 AM (GMT+7) | LuatVietnam: 1997

Those who commit acts of buying value-added invoices and illegally using such invoices to make false dossiers of appropriation of value-added tax refunds in one of the following cases shall be examined for penal liability for the crime of "fraudulently appropriating property" according to Article 139 of the Penal Code:

A/ Value-added tax refunds of five hundred thousand VND or more;

B/ The value-added tax refund is less than five hundred thousand VND, but it causes serious consequences or has been administratively sanctioned for the act of appropriation or has been convicted of the crime of appropriating property, but has not yet had its criminal record expunged but still commits the violation

Effective notes
Expired from 15/8/2013, replaced by Joint Circular No. 10/2013/TTLT-BTP-BCA-TANDTC-VKSNDTC-BTC dated 26/6/2013.
