Business License Fee
Being considered as annual registration fee and paid in commencing of the year, Business License Fee (BLF) is required to be declared and paid when the company’s establishment is completed.
BUSINESS LICENSE FEE LEVEL
BLF level in Vietnam is determined based upon its registered charter capital, with details are elaborated as below:
Level
Charter Capital

PRACTICES
Having released recently in 2020 by Vietnam Government (*), positive news is that newly established enterprise is exempted from BLF payment in the first year of establishment e.g. from 1 January to 31 December, following Decree 22/2020 dated 25 Feb 2020.
Newly set-up firm is required to declare and submit BLF return
once
via tax portal. Failing to comply will result in penalty from the authority. Only BLF payment is required for next consecutive years.
If there is any change in registered charter capital leads to the change in BLF rate, the company is required to re-submit BLF return and pay BLF according to new corresponding level.
Deadline for BLF settlement is 30 January on annual basis.
UPDATE FROM 2026
As a nice statement from Vietnam Government in supporting economy growth, BLF is no longer applied for Vietnam-based enterprise, effective from 1 Jan 2026 following provision in Resolution 198/2025/QH15.
