What is IFRS?

IFRS (International Financial Reporting Standards) is a set of standards issued by the IASB to unify how financial statements are prepared and presented across countries. Adopting IFRS makes financial statements transparent, reliable and comparable on a global scale.

Why do businesses need IFRS?

When and why enterprises should prepare to adopt IFRS.

Raising international capital

Foreign investors and lenders require IFRS-based reporting.

Multinational groups

Consolidating reports with overseas parent or subsidiaries.

Listing & M&A

Preparing for IPO, international listing or M&A transactions.

Transparency & governance

Improving financial information quality and governance.

IFRS adoption roadmap in Vietnam

Under Decision 345/QD-BTC of the Ministry of Finance.

  1. 2020 – 2021

    Preparation phase

    Building the legal framework, training resources and publishing IFRS translations.

  2. 2022 – 2025

    Voluntary adoption

    Eligible enterprises may voluntarily apply IFRS for consolidated reports.

  3. After 2025

    Mandatory adoption

    Mandatory for enterprise groups defined by the Ministry of Finance.
