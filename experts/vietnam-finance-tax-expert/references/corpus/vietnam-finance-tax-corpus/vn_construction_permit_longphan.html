Comprehensive service for applying for construction permits for businesses

Construction permits for businesses are a required legal document when implementing a construction project serving production and business activities. Construction licensing procedures require businesses to prepare complete documents and comply with strict procedures as prescribed by law. The article below will provide detailed information about the process and procedures for applying for construction permits for businesses in Vietnam.

Cases where businesses must apply for a construction permit

Pursuant to Clause 1, Article 89 of the Law on Construction 2014. as amended by Clause 30, Article 1 of the 2020 Amended Construction Law, the following cases must apply for a construction permit:

Construction projects must have a construction permit issued by a competent state agency to the investor according to regulations, except for cases where construction permits are exempted in Clause 2, Article 89 of the Law on Construction 2014.

Competent authority issues construction permits

According to the provisions of Article 103 of the Law on Construction 2014 as amended by Clause 37, Article 1 of the Amended Construction Law 2020, agencies competent to issue construction permits to businesses include:

  • The Provincial People’s Committee issues construction permits for works that require a construction permit in the province, except for works under the jurisdiction of the District People’s Committee.
  • The Provincial People’s Committee decentralizes and authorizes the Department of Construction, the Management Board of industrial parks, export processing zones, high-tech zones, economic zones, and district-level People’s Committees to issue construction permits within their functions. and the scope of management of this agency.
  • District-level People’s Committees issue construction permits for level III, level IV works and individual houses in the area under their management.

Enterprises need to identify the correct competent authority to submit construction permit applications. This helps speed up application processing, avoiding submission to the wrong agency, leading to unnecessary time and expense.

Procedures for applying for construction permits for businesses

Profile components

Applications for construction permits for businesses have certain differences, depending on each project and project of the business. Specifically as follows:

  • Application dossier for a new construction permit as prescribed in Article 95 of the Law on Construction 2014. and Article 43 of Decree 15/2021/ND-CP;
  • Application dossier for construction permit in case of repair and renovation of works according to the provisions of Article 96 of the Law on Construction 2014 and Article 47 of Decree 15/2021/ND-CP;
  • Application dossier for issuance of construction permit in case of relocation of works according to the provisions of Article 96 of the Law on Construction 2014 and Article 48 of Decree 15/2021/ND-CP;
  • Dossier to request a construction permit by stage as prescribed in Article 44 of Decree 15/2021/ND-CP;
  • Dossier to request a construction permit for the project and group of works under the project as prescribed in Article 44 of Decree 15/2021/ND-CP;
  • Dossier to request a construction permit for individual houses as prescribed in Article 46 of Decree 15/2021/ND-CP.

Enterprises need to fully and accurately prepare documents according to regulations. This helps avoid the situation of returned documents or additional requests, causing delays in the construction permit application process.

Execution order

The process of applying for construction permits for businesses according to Decree 15/2021/ND-CP includes the following steps:

Step 1: Enterprise submits application for construction permit to the competent authority.

Step 2: The competent authority receives the application and checks the completeness and validity of the application.

Step 3: Within 7 working days from the date of receiving complete and valid documents, the competent agency must organize an appraisal of the documents and a field inspection.

Step 4: Within 5 working days from the date of receiving the appraisal results, the competent authority will consider and decide on the issuance of the construction permit.

Step 5: Enterprises receive results at the place of application or by post.

Enterprises need to closely monitor the progress of processing documents to promptly supplement and complete them if requested by competent authorities.

Cost of granting construction permits for businesses

According to Article 3, Circular 85/2019/TT-BTC, the fee for applying for construction permits for businesses is regulated by the Provincial People’s Council based on the socio-economic conditions in the locality where service provision activities arise. services, fees and charges.
