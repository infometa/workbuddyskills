CIRCULAR 99/2025/TT-BTC: KEY CHANGES IN VIETNAM'S ACCOUNTING RULES (Acclime)

Effective: 1 January 2026, replacing Circular 200/2014/TT-BTC after 11+ years.

KEY SHIFTS:
- From "compliance by instruction" to "compliance by principle" (closer to IFRS)
- Enterprise autonomy in accounting governance
- Digitalisation and accountability emphasis
- Modernisation, IFRS alignment, digitalisation promotion

INTERNAL GOVERNANCE:
- Businesses must establish internal accounting governance rules and control procedures
- Define who approves, records, reviews transactions; responsibility segregation
- Define powers, obligations, responsibilities of each department/individual
- Document thoroughly - regulators may review internal policies

ACCOUNTING CURRENCY:
- Default: VND
- Enterprises primarily in foreign currencies may choose foreign functional currency
- Criteria: currency influencing selling prices/settlements; affecting labor/materials costs
- If foreign currency chosen: must convert FS to VND using average transfer exchange rate
- Disclose: reason for selection, conversion method, impact on results
- Currency change only at start of new accounting year with significant operational shift

CONSOLIDATION FOR GROUPS:
- Required for enterprises with subsidiaries, dependent units, affiliated units
- Eliminate internal transactions
- Extended from parent-subsidiary to dependent and affiliated units
- Must support consolidation in accounting systems, map intercompany transactions

CHART OF ACCOUNTS & DOCUMENTATION:
- Enterprises may amend/supplement account titles, numbers, structures (Appendix II)
- Must not alter substance of financial statements
- Amendments supported by internal accounting regulations
- Self-design vouchers, e-vouchers, accounting books
- Electronic vouchers and digital signatures formally recognized
- No printing required if authenticity/traceability ensured

FINANCIAL STATEMENTS:
- "Balance Sheet" → "Statement of Financial Position"
- Retroactive principle for functional currency changes
- Standard set: Statement of Financial Position, P&L, Cash Flow, Notes
- Enhanced Notes disclosure areas:
  - Basis for accounting policy (preparation, currency, revenue recognition)
  - Currency change impacts
  - Internal transactions and risk exposures
  - Consolidation methods
  - Special characteristics (mergers/splits/restructuring)

KEY ACTIONS:
1. Gap analysis: compare existing regime vs Circular 99
2. Update accounting policy manual
3. Redesign accounting system (Chart of Accounts, voucher templates, ERP mapping)
4. Strengthen governance and capacity (define roles, training)
5. Upgrade reporting (templates, disclosures)
6. Mock closing with updated templates before first fiscal year
7. Consolidation implementation for multi-entity businesses
8. Currency transition management if adopting foreign functional currency
9. Engage auditors/advisors early
