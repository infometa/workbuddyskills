2012 Law on Water resources

Law No. 17/2012/QH13 dated June 21, 2012 of the National Assembly on Water Resources
Posted: 9/7/2012 9:18:22 AM | Latest updated: 22/12/2023 8:16:31 AM (GMT+7) | LuatVietnam: 3029 | Vietlaw: 69

Accordingly, organizations and individuals who exploiting water resources must pay the money for authorization if the purpose of exploitation are: electricity supply for commercial purposes; business, service, non-agricultural production ; cash crops, livestock, aquaculture on a large scale.

read more


In addition to amending and supplementing a number of provisions on adjustment of policies to consider the water resources are the property of the state, the economic undertakings and use of saving water, efficiency and unified in management of the river basin management of combination with the local administration, the Law specifies the measures to prevent and combat pollution, degradation, depletion of water resources; the adaptation and troubleshoot the source of pollution water, protection and development of aquatic resources; corridors protect water resources and ensuring traffic flow.
This Law takes effect from the date of January 01, 2013.

 
Effective notes
  • See more the amendments, supplements to Clause 1 Article 73 of this Law at Law No. 08/2017/QH14 dated June 19th, 2017; the amendments, supplements at Law No. 35/2018/QH14 dated November 20th, 2018; the amendments, supplements to Clause 1 Article 73 of this Law at Law No. 72/2020/QH14⚠️ dated November 17th, 2020.
  • Article 37 and point dd Clause 1 Article 38 of this Law shall be annulled from January 1st, 2022, according to point a Clause 1 Article 169 of Law No. 72/2020/QH14⚠️ dated November 17th, 2020.
  • This Law shall be expired from July 1st, 2024, replaced by Law No. 28/2023/QH15⚠️ dated November 27th, 2023.
