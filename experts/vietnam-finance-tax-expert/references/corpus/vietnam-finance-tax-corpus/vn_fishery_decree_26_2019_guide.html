Regarding the management of foreigners temporarily residing in industrial parks in Hanoi

Công văn số click to view ngày 30/3/2023 của UBND TP. Hà Nội về việc quản lý người nước ngoài tạm trú trong các khu công nghiệp (1 trang)
Posted: 5/4/2023 8:44:34 AM | Latest updated: 6/4/2023 3:39:42 PM (GMT+7) | LuatVietnam: 5673

Regarding the management of foreigners temporarily residing in industrial parks, the Hanoi People's Committee has approved the assignment of the Hanoi Industrial and Export Processing Parks Management Board to preside over and coordinate with the Department of Labor, Invalids and Social Affairs, the Department of Foreign Affairs, the City Police, the People's Committees of districts, districts appraise the needs of foreigners for temporary residence in industrial parks to serve the production and business activities of enterprises.

Particularly for foreigners who have been approved for temporary residence at accommodation establishments before Decree No. 35/2022/ND-CP⚠️ takes effect, follow the guidance in Document No. 1733/BKHDT-QLKKT dated March 13, 2023.
