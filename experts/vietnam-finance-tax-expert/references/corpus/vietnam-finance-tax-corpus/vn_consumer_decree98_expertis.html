GOVERMENT
—–		 SOCIAL REPUBLIC OF VIETNAM
Independence - Freedom - Happiness
Number: 98 / 2020 / ND-CP Hanoi, date 26 month 8 year 2020

DECREE

REGULATIONS ON PENALTIES FOR ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS IN COMMERCIAL ACTIVITIES, PRODUCTION, AND TRADING OF COUNTERFEIT AND PROHIBITED GOODS, AND FOR THE PROTECTION OF CONSUMER RIGHTS

Based on the Law on Organization of the Government dated June 19, 2015;

Based on the Law on Handling Administrative Violations dated June 20, 2012;

Based on the Investment Law dated November 26, 2014 and the Law amending and supplementing Article 6 and Appendix 4 on the List of conditional investment and business sectors of the Investment Law dated November 22, 2016;

Based on the Enterprise Law dated November 26, 2014;

Based on the Commercial Law dated June 14, 2005;

Based on the Law on Foreign Trade Management dated June 12, 2017;

Based on the Law on Electronic Transactions dated June 29, 2005;

Based on the Law on Product and Goods Quality dated November 21, 2007;

Based on the Law on Prevention and Control of Tobacco Harm dated June 18, 2012;

Based on the Law on Prevention and Control of Harmful Effects of Alcohol and Beer dated June 14, 2019;

Based on the Law on Pharmaceuticals dated April 6, 2016;

Based on the Law on Protection of Consumer Rights dated November 17, 2010;

As requested by the Minister of Industry and Trade;

The government has issued a Decree stipulating penalties for administrative violations in commercial activities, production, trading of counterfeit and prohibited goods, and protection of consumer rights.

Chapter I

GENERAL RULES

Article 1. Scope

1. This Decree regulates administrative violations, forms of penalties, penalty levels, remedial measures, authority to draw up administrative violation reports, and authority to impose penalties for administrative violations in commercial activities, production, trading of counterfeit and prohibited goods, and consumer protection.

2. Administrative violations in commercial activities, production, trading of counterfeit and prohibited goods, and protection of consumer rights stipulated in this Decree include:

a) Violations of business regulations as stipulated in the business license, except for cases already prescribed in decrees on administrative sanctions in other areas of state management;

b) Engaging in business activities in sectors prohibited from investment and business; producing and trading counterfeit or prohibited goods;

c) Business activities involving smuggled goods; goods in circulation within the country subject to emergency measures; goods past their expiration date, of unknown origin or provenance, and other violations;

d) Violations related to the tobacco business;

d) Violations related to the sale of alcoholic beverages;

e) Commodity speculation and hoarding;

g) Violations related to trade promotion activities;

h) Violations related to the export and import of goods and services related to the export and import of goods;

i) Violations of consumer rights protection;

k) Violations related to e-commerce;

l) Violations concerning the establishment and operation of commercial activities by foreign traders and foreigners in Vietnam;

m) Other violations in commercial activities.

3. Other administrative violations in commercial activities related to the business of petroleum products and liquefied petroleum gas; prices and price listing of goods and services; documents and invoices for the purchase and sale of goods and services; measurement, standards, and quality of goods circulating and traded in the market; product labels; intellectual property; business registration procedures; signs; commercial advertising; auctioning of goods; bidding for goods and services; buying and selling, and exchanging goods by border residents; and other violations shall be subject to the regulations on administrative penalties in the relevant areas of state management.

4. For acts of commodity speculation, hoarding, bidding for goods and services, and franchising that show signs of anti-competitive behavior as stipulated in the law on competition, the regulations on investigation and handling of violations of competition law shall apply.

5. For violations related to exported, imported, and transit goods, as well as outgoing, incoming, and transit transport vehicles, detected by customs authorities within their operational area, the provisions of the Government Decree on administrative sanctions in the field of customs shall apply. If the Government Decree on administrative sanctions in the field of customs does not specify such violations, the provisions of this Decree shall apply.

Article 2. Subjects liable to administrative penalties

1. Vietnamese individuals or organizations, or foreign individuals or organizations, who commit administrative violations as stipulated in this Decree within the territory of Vietnam.

2. Household businesses established in accordance with the law; households engaged in agricultural, forestry, and fishery production, salt production; and street vendors, small traders, itinerant traders, and low-income service providers who are not required to register their businesses according to the law, and who violate the provisions of this Decree, shall be subject to penalties as if they were individuals.

3. The organizations stipulated in Clause 1 of this Article include:

a) Enterprises established and operating in accordance with the Law on Enterprises; cooperatives and cooperative unions established in accordance with the Law on Cooperatives; other economic organizations established and operating in accordance with the law and affiliated units of the aforementioned economic organizations;

b) Representative offices and branches of foreign traders in Vietnam; representative offices of foreign trade promotion organizations in Vietnam;

c) Other organizations established in accordance with the law.

Article 3. Explain words

According to this Decree, the following terms are understood as follows:

1. "Production" is the performance of one, several, or all of the manufacturing, typesetting, printing, processing, ordering, preliminary processing, processing, extraction, recycling, assembly, blending, dividing, transferring, filling, packaging, and other activities that produce goods.

2. "Trading" refers to the performance of one, several, or all of the activities of offering for sale, displaying for sale, storing, preserving, transporting, wholesaling, retailing, exporting, importing, and other activities that put goods into circulation.

3. "Goods in circulation on the market" includes goods that are displayed, promoted, stored, transported, and kept during the buying and selling process.

4. “Business license” includes licenses, certificates of eligibility, professional liability insurance certificates, confirmation documents, and other forms of documents specifying the conditions that individuals and organizations must meet to carry out investment and business activities, issued by state agencies or competent authorities to those individuals and organizations in accordance with the law.

5. "Prohibited goods" include goods prohibited from being traded, goods prohibited from circulation, and goods prohibited from being used in Vietnam.

6. "Smuggled goods" include:

a) Imported goods that are on the list of goods prohibited from import or temporarily suspended from import according to the law, except in cases where the Prime Minister decides to permit import;

b) Goods imported under license but without an import license, or goods imported under conditional terms but not meeting the conditions stipulated by law;

c) Imported goods that do not pass through designated border gates, do not undergo customs procedures as prescribed by law, or involve fraudulent declarations of quantity or type of goods during customs procedures;

d) Imported goods circulating in the market without invoices or supporting documents as required by law, or with invoices or supporting documents that are illegal according to the law on invoice management;

d) Imported goods that, according to the law, are required to have import stamps affixed but do not have stamps affixed as required by law, or have stamps affixed but they are counterfeit or used stamps.

7. "Counterfeit goods" include:

a) Goods whose use value or function does not correspond to their natural origin or nature, or whose name does not correspond to the declared or registered use value or function; goods that have no use value or function, or whose use value or function does not correspond to the declared or registered use value or function;

b) Goods where at least one of the basic quality indicators or technical characteristics or quantitative values ​​of the main substance that constitute the use value or function of the goods is only 70% or less of the minimum level stipulated in the registered, published, or stated technical regulations or quality standards on the label or packaging of the goods;

c) Counterfeit drugs as defined in Clause 33, Article 2 of the 2016 Law on Pharmaceuticals and counterfeit medicinal materials as defined in Clause 34, Article 2 of the 2016 Law on Pharmaceuticals;

d) Veterinary drugs and plant protection products that lack active ingredients; do not contain all the registered active ingredients; contain active ingredients different from those stated on the label or packaging; or have at least one active ingredient content that is 70% or less of the minimum level specified in the registered and published technical regulations or quality standards;

d) Goods bearing labels or packaging that falsely indicate the name or address of the organization or individual producing, importing, or distributing the goods; falsely indicate the registration number, declaration number, or barcode of the goods; or falsely indicate the packaging of goods belonging to another organization or individual; or falsely indicate the origin or place of production, packaging, or assembly of the goods;

e) Counterfeit labels, tags, and packaging for goods.

8. "Counterfeit labels, packaging, and product packaging" includes decals, product labels, product packaging, quality stamps, quality marks, traceability stamps, warranty cards, shrink wrap, or other items of business organizations or individuals that falsely indicate the name and address of other organizations or individuals; or falsely indicate the trade name, product name, barcode, registration number, or declaration number of goods or packaging of other organizations or individuals.

9. “Evidence” includes objects, money, documents, finished or unfinished goods directly related to the administrative violation.

10. “Means of violation” include vehicles, tools, and machinery used to commit administrative violations.

11. “Consumer’s personal information” refers to information relating to an individual consumer that has been protected by the consumer or other related organizations or individuals through security measures, and whose disclosure or use without their consent would adversely affect the consumer’s health, life, property, or cause other material or psychological harm.

12. “Third parties in the provision of information about goods and services to consumers” are organizations and individuals requested by the business of goods and services to provide information about those goods and services, including:

a) Organizations and individuals engaged in business activities that provide information services about goods and services to consumers;

b) Organizations and individuals engaged in business activities participate in the creation of information about goods and services;

c) Media owners, media service providers;

d) Other organizations or individuals requested to provide information.

13. "Goods of unknown origin" refers to goods circulating in the market for which there is no basis to determine the place of production or origin. The basis for determining the place of production or origin of goods includes information displayed on the product label, packaging, accompanying documents; certificates of origin, contracts, sales invoices, customs declarations, and other documents proving legal ownership of the goods and civil transactions between the organization or individual producing the goods and related parties as stipulated by law.

Article 4. Regulations on forms of administrative sanctions and measures to remedy consequences.

1. Main forms of penalties:

a) Warning;

b) Fine.

2. Additional forms of punishment:

a) Revocation of the license or professional certificate for a period of 01 to 24 months, or suspension of operations for a period of 01 to 24 months;

b) Confiscation of evidence of administrative violations and means used to commit administrative violations (hereinafter collectively referred to as confiscation of evidence and means of violation).

3. Remedial measures:

a) To compel the removal from the territory of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam or the re-export of goods, items, and means of transport;

b) Mandating the destruction of goods and items harmful to human health, livestock, plants, and the environment, as well as cultural products with harmful content;

c) To compel the correction of false or misleading information;

d) To require the removal of infringing elements from labels, packaging of goods, business equipment, and items;

d) Mandating the recall of products and goods that do not meet quality standards;

e) To compel the return of any illegal profits obtained from committing administrative violations, or to compel the return of an amount equal to the value of confiscated goods and means of violation that have been consumed, disposed of, or destroyed in violation of the law;

g) Mandating the recall of defective goods;

h) To cancel the results of a draw that has already been held and organize a new draw for promotional programs involving chance;

i) To compel the amendment of a concluded contract or to compel the amendment of a standard contract or general terms and conditions of transaction in accordance with regulations;

k) To compel the revocation of the “.vn” domain name of an e-commerce website or to compel the removal of the mobile application from app stores or from the addresses provided.

4. Fine amount:

a) The maximum fine in the field of commerce and consumer protection is VND 100.000.000 for individuals and VND 200.000.000 for organizations; the maximum fine in the field of manufacturing and trading counterfeit and prohibited goods is VND 200.000.000 for individuals and VND 400.000.000 for organizations;

b) The fines stipulated in Chapter II of this Decree are the fines applicable to administrative violations committed by individuals, except for the administrative violations specified in point p, clause 2, Article 33; clause 2, Article 34; point b, clause 4, Article 35; Article 68; Article 70; clauses 6, 7, 8, and 9, Article 73; and clauses 6, 7, and 8, Article 77 of this Decree. In cases where the administrative violation is committed by an organization, the fine shall be twice the amount stipulated for individuals.

Article 5. Determining the value of seized items and means used in administrative violations as a basis for determining the fine range and the authority to impose penalties.

1. The determination of the value of seized items and means used in administrative violations as stipulated in this Decree shall be based on one of the criteria in the order of priority specified in points a, b, and c of Clause 2, Article 60 of the Law on Handling Administrative Violations.

2. For confiscated goods that are counterfeit as stipulated in points a, b, c, d, e, and f of Clause 7, Article 3 of this Decree, the value of the confiscated goods shall be the market price of genuine goods or goods with the same features, technical specifications, and uses at the time and place where the administrative violation is detected, as prescribed in point d, Clause 2, Article 60 of the Law on Handling Administrative Violations. If the price cannot be determined as above, the value shall be determined according to the provisions of Clause 1 of this Article.

3. If the grounds stipulated in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article cannot be applied, the competent authority handling the case may issue a decision to temporarily seize the infringing items and establish a Valuation Council in accordance with Clause 3 of Article 60 of the Law on Handling Administrative Violations.

Chapter II

ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLATIONS, FORMS OF PENALTY, PENALTY LEVELS, AND REMEDIAL MEASURES

Section 1. VIOLATIONS REGARDING BUSINESS ACTIVITIES UNDER THE BUSINESS LICENSE

Article 6. Violations of business regulations as stipulated in the business license.

1. A fine of between VND 3.000.000 and VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Adding, erasing, or altering the content recorded in the business license;

b) Leasing, lending, pledging, mortgaging, selling, or transferring business licenses;

c) Renting, borrowing, accepting as collateral, accepting as security, purchasing, or receiving the transfer of business licenses.

2. A fine of VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for conducting business outside the scope, target, scale, duration, geographical area, location, or goods specified in the issued business license.

3. A fine of between VND 10.000.000 and VND 15.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Engaging in the business of goods and services in sectors subject to conditional investment and business regulations without a business license as required by law;

b) Engaging in the business of goods and services in sectors subject to conditional investment and business activities when the issued business license has expired;

c) Engaging in the business of goods and services within conditional investment and business sectors but failing to meet the prescribed investment and business conditions during the course of business operations;

d) Using another trader's business license to conduct business.

4. A fine of VND 15.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for continuing business operations during a period when the business license has been suspended, revoked, or withdrawn by a competent state management agency.

5. Double the fines stipulated in Clauses 1 to 4 of this Article shall be imposed on entities engaged in industrial alcohol production; processing and trading of tobacco raw materials; manufacturing tobacco products; and trading, distributing, and wholesaling alcohol or tobacco products that commit administrative violations.

6. Additional sanctioning forms:

a) Confiscate the items in violation of the provisions of point a, clause 1 of this Article;

b) Revocation of business license for a period of 1 to 3 months for violations of the regulations specified in point b, clause 01, clause 2, and point c, clause 03 of this Article in cases of multiple violations or repeat offenses.

7. Remedies:

The offender shall be compelled to return any illegal profits obtained from committing acts violating the provisions of point b, clause 1, clauses 2, 3, and 4 of this Article.

Section 2. PROHIBITED BUSINESS PRACTICES, PRODUCTION AND TRADING OF COUNTERFEIT AND PROHIBITED GOODS

Article 7. Business activities involving services included in the list of prohibited investment and business sectors.

1. A fine of VND 60.000.000 to VND 80.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of conducting business in services included in the list of prohibited investment and business sectors.

2. Additional sanctioning forms:

Confiscation of evidence and means used in the violation of the provisions of Clause 1 of this Article.

3. Remedies:

The offender shall be compelled to return any illegal profits obtained from committing the violation stipulated in Clause 1 of this Article.

Article 8. Acts of producing, trading, transporting, storing, and receiving prohibited goods.

1. A fine of between VND 1.000.000 and VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

a) Trading in plant protection products that the State prohibits from being traded, circulated, or used, in quantities less than 5 kilograms or less than 5 liters;

b) Trading in smuggled cigarettes in quantities under 50 packs (1 pack = 20 cigarettes; for smuggled cigars and other finished tobacco products, 20g = 1 pack);

c) Trading in firecrackers weighing less than 0,5 kilograms;

d) Trading in other goods that the State prohibits from being traded, circulated, or used, with a value of less than 3.000.000 VND or generating illicit profits of less than 1.500.000 VND.

2. A fine of between VND 3.000.000 and VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

a) Trading in plant protection products that the State prohibits from being traded, circulated, or used, in quantities from 5 kilograms to less than 10 kilograms or from 5 liters to less than 10 liters;

b) Trading in smuggled cigarettes in quantities from 50 packs to less than 100 packs;

c) Trading in firecrackers weighing from 0,5 kilograms to less than 1 kilograms;

d) Trading in other goods that the State prohibits from being traded, circulated, or used, with a value ranging from VND 3.000.000 to less than VND 5.000.000, or generating illicit profits ranging from VND 1.500.000 to less than VND 2.500.000.

3. A fine of between VND 5.000.000 and VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

a) Trading in plant protection products that the State prohibits from being traded, circulated, or used, in quantities from 10 kilograms to less than 15 kilograms or from 10 liters to less than 15 liters;

b) Trading in smuggled cigarettes in quantities from 100 packs to less than 300 packs;

c) Trading in firecrackers weighing from 1 kilograms to less than 2 kilograms;

d) Trading in other goods that the State prohibits from being traded, circulated, or used, with a value ranging from VND 5.000.000 to less than VND 10.000.000, or generating illicit profits ranging from VND 2.500.000 to less than VND 5.000.000.

4. A fine of between VND 10.000.000 and VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

a) Trading in plant protection products that the State prohibits from being traded, circulated, or used, in quantities from 15 kilograms to less than 20 kilograms or from 15 liters to less than 20 liters;

b) Trading in smuggled cigarettes in quantities from 300 packs to less than 500 packs;

c) Trading in firecrackers weighing from 2 kilograms to less than 3 kilograms;

d) Trading in other goods that the State prohibits from being traded, circulated, or used, with a value ranging from VND 10.000.000 to less than VND 30.000.000, or generating illicit profits ranging from VND 5.000.000 to less than VND 15.000.000.

5. A fine of between VND 30.000.000 and VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

a) Trading in plant protection products that the State prohibits from being traded, circulated, or used, in quantities from 20 kilograms to less than 30 kilograms or from 20 liters to less than 30 liters;

b) Trading in smuggled cigarettes in quantities from 500 packs to less than 1.000 packs;

c) Trading in firecrackers weighing from 3 kilograms to less than 4 kilograms;

d) Trading in other goods that the State prohibits from being traded, circulated, or used, with a value ranging from VND 30.000.000 to less than VND 50.000.000, or generating illicit profits ranging from VND 15.000.000 to less than VND 25.000.000.

6. A fine of between VND 50.000.000 and VND 70.000.000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

a) Trading in plant protection products that the State prohibits from being traded, circulated, or used, in quantities from 30 kilograms to less than 40 kilograms or from 30 liters to less than 40 liters;

b) Trading in smuggled cigarettes in quantities from 1.000 packs to less than 1.200 packs;

c) Trading in firecrackers weighing from 4 kilograms to less than 5 kilograms;

d) Trading in other goods that the State prohibits from being traded, circulated, or used, with a value ranging from VND 50.000.000 to less than VND 70.000.000, or generating illicit profits ranging from VND 25.000.000 to less than VND 35.000.000.

7. A fine of between VND 70.000.000 and VND 90.000.000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations:

a) Trading in plant protection products that the State prohibits from being traded, circulated, or used, in quantities from 40 kilograms to less than 50 kilograms or from 40 liters to less than 50 liters;

b) Trading in smuggled cigarettes in quantities from 1.200 packs to less than 1.500 packs;

c) Trading in firecrackers weighing from 5 kilograms to less than 6 kilograms;

d) Trading in other goods that the State prohibits from being traded, circulated, or used, with a value ranging from VND 70.000.000 to less than VND 100.000.000, or generating illicit profits ranging from VND 35.000.000 to less than VND 50.000.000.

8. A fine of between VND 90.000.000 and VND 100.000.000 shall be imposed for any of the following violations in cases where criminal liability is not pursued:

a) Trading in plant protection products that the State prohibits from being traded, circulated, or used, in quantities of 50 kilograms or more, or 50 liters or more;

b) Trading in smuggled cigarettes in quantities of 1.500 packs or more;

c) Trading in firecrackers weighing 6 kilograms or more;

d) Trading in other goods that the State prohibits from being traded, circulated, or used, with a value of VND 100.000.000 or more, or obtaining illicit profits of VND 50.000.000 or more.

9. Double the fines stipulated in Clauses 1 to 8 of this Article for the production of prohibited goods as prescribed in Clauses 1 to 8 of this Article.

10. The fines stipulated in Clauses 1 to 8 of this Article shall also apply to administrative penalties for violations of:

a) The act of transporting prohibited goods;

b) The act of possessing prohibited goods;

c) The act of receiving or delivering prohibited goods.

11. Additional sanctioning forms:

a) Confiscation of evidence related to violations of the provisions of this Article, except in cases where remedial measures as prescribed in point a, clause 12 of this Article are applied;

b) Confiscation of equipment, tools, and machinery used to produce prohibited goods for violations of the provisions of Clause 9 of this Article;

c) Confiscation of the means of transport used to transport prohibited goods for violations of the provisions of this Article in cases where the quantity, weight, value, or illicit profit of the prohibited goods is specified in Clauses 6, 7, and 8 of this Article, or in cases of multiple violations or recidivism;

d) Revocation of the license or professional certificate for a period of 01 to 03 months for violations of the regulations in this Article in cases of multiple violations or repeat offenses, except as stipulated in point d of this clause;

d) Revocation of the license or professional certificate for a period of 03 to 06 months for violations of the regulations specified in Clause 9 of this Article.

12. Remedies:

a) Mandatory destruction of confiscated goods and items that are harmful to human health, livestock, plants, and the environment, as well as cultural products with harmful content, in violation of the provisions of this Article;

b) To compel the return of any illegal profits obtained from committing acts that violate the provisions of this Article.

13. For acts of producing or trading goods that are not permitted for circulation or use in Vietnam, the provisions of the Government's decrees on administrative sanctions in related fields shall be applied to impose administrative penalties.

Article 9. Acts of trading in counterfeit goods in terms of usability and function.

1. For acts of trading in counterfeit goods with regard to their usability and function as stipulated in points a, b, c, and d of Clause 7, Article 3 of this Decree, the fines are as follows:

a) A fine of VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the quantity of counterfeit goods is equivalent to the quantity of genuine goods with a value of less than VND 3.000.000 or the illegal profit is less than VND 5.000.000;

b) A fine of VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where counterfeit goods are equivalent in quantity to genuine goods with a value ranging from VND 3.000.000 to less than VND 5.000.000, or where illegal profits range from VND 5.000.000 to less than VND 10.000.000;

c) A fine of VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the quantity of counterfeit goods is equivalent to the quantity of genuine goods with a value ranging from VND 5.000.000 to less than VND 10.000.000, or the illegal profit ranges from VND 10.000.000 to less than VND 20.000.000;

d) A fine of VND 10.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the quantity of counterfeit goods is equivalent to the quantity of genuine goods with a value ranging from VND 10.000.000 to less than VND 20.000.000, or the illegal profit ranges from VND 20.000.000 to less than VND 30.000.000;

d) A fine of VND 30.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the quantity of counterfeit goods is equivalent to the quantity of genuine goods with a value ranging from VND 20.000.000 to less than VND 30.000.000, or the illegal profit ranges from VND 30.000.000 to less than VND 50.000.000;

e) A fine of VND 50.000.000 to VND 70.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where counterfeit goods are equivalent in quantity to genuine goods with a value of VND 30.000.000 or more, or where illegal profits of VND 50.000.000 or more are obtained without criminal prosecution.

2. Double the fines stipulated in Clause 1 of this Article shall be imposed for the act of importing counterfeit goods or goods that fall under any of the following cases:

a) Food, food additives, food processing aids, food preservatives, medicines, and pharmaceutical raw materials that are not subject to criminal prosecution;

b) This includes animal feed, aquatic feed, aquaculture environmental treatment products, livestock waste treatment products, fertilizers, veterinary drugs, pesticides, plant seeds, and animal breeds;

Article 33. Violations related to promotional activities

1. A fine of between VND 5.000.000 and VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

c) Not publicly organizing the draw for a promotional program involving chance, or organizing the draw for a promotional program involving chance without the presence of customers, or without recording the draw in a written document;

d) Failing to notify the competent state management agency about the time and place of issuing proof of winning along with goods before issuing the proof of winning when conducting a promotional program involving chance where the proof of winning is issued along with the goods;

d) Failure to comply with or improper compliance with regulations regarding the announcement of prize winning results and the public disclosure of promotional campaign results;

e) Failure to award prizes within the specified timeframe when conducting a promotional program that includes prize giving;

g) Failure to accurately and promptly confirm customer participation in the customer loyalty program;

h) Failure to fully display and store the required information as stipulated on purchase orders, service vouchers, contest entry forms, lottery tickets, customer cards, or receipts recording the purchase of goods and services used in promotional programs for frequent customers.

2. A fine of between VND 10.000.000 and VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Conducting promotions for goods and services that are not carried out by businesses authorized to do so according to regulations;

b) Conducting promotions for goods and services of other businesses without a prescribed promotional service contract, or hiring a business providing promotional services to conduct promotions for one's own goods and services without a prescribed promotional service contract;

c) Failure to notify or register with the competent state management agency as required before conducting the promotion, or providing notification or registration incorrectly;

d) Failure to notify or report the results of promotional activities to the competent state management agency as prescribed, or providing notification or reporting that is not in accordance with regulations, or providing notification or reporting that is untruthful;

d) Failure to implement, improper implementation, or delay in implementing the contents of the promotional program that has been announced, committed to customers, or announced and registered with the competent state management agency;

e) Conducting promotions where the value of goods or services used for promotion exceeds the maximum limit for the value of goods or services used for promotion as stipulated by regulations;

g) Conducting promotional activities in the form of price reductions where the price reduction of goods or services exceeds the maximum price reduction for the goods or services being promoted as stipulated by regulations;

h) Conducting promotions by reducing the selling price of goods or the price of services to below the minimum price in cases where the selling price of goods or the price of services is subject to a price range or minimum price regulation by the State; reducing the selling price of goods or services for goods and services whose prices are specifically determined by the State;

i) Implementing promotional programs in the form of price reductions that do not comply with regulations regarding the permitted time frame for conducting promotions;

k) Using proof of winning that is similar or identical to lottery results issued exclusively by the state, or using lottery results as the basis for determining winnings, or as a basis for giving or rewarding prizes in promotional programs in the forms prescribed in Clauses 5, 6 and 9 of Article 92 of the Commercial Law;

l) Failure to comply with or improper compliance with regulations regarding the remittance of 50% of the value of unclaimed prizes announced to the state budget in the case of promotional programs involving chance;

m) Terminate the promotional program before the announced or confirmed deadline, except where permitted by law or where the competent state management agency confirms an adjustment to the promotional period;

n) Termending a promotional program before the announced or approved deadline, except in cases stipulated by law;

o) Conducting promotions in violation of regulations on the principles of conducting promotions;

p) A representative office of a business entity conducts promotional activities for the business entity it represents, or hires another business entity to conduct promotional activities for the business entity it represents in Vietnam.

3. A fine of between VND 20.000.000 and VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Promotions for goods and services prohibited from business; goods and services restricted from business; goods not yet permitted for circulation, services not yet permitted for provision; alcohol, lottery tickets, tobacco, infant formula, medicines for human use including those already permitted for circulation according to regulations of the Ministry of Health (except for promotions for businesses trading in medicines), medical examination and treatment services of public health facilities, educational services of public institutions, public vocational education institutions, goods and services prohibited from circulation in Vietnam or other goods and services prohibited from promotion according to the law;

b) Using goods and services for promotional purposes that are prohibited from being traded; goods and services with restricted trading; goods not yet permitted for circulation, services not yet permitted for provision; alcohol, lottery tickets, tobacco, medicines for human use including those already permitted for circulation according to regulations of the Ministry of Health (except in cases of promotion for businesses trading in medicines), goods and services prohibited from circulation in Vietnam and other goods and services prohibited from promotion according to the law;

c) Promoting alcoholic beverages to individuals under 18 years of age; promoting alcoholic beverages with an alcohol content of 15 degrees or higher in the business of selling alcoholic beverages, or using alcoholic beverages with an alcohol content of 15 degrees or higher for promotional purposes in any form;

d) Making dishonest or misleading promotions about goods or services to deceive customers;

d) Promotional activities aimed at selling goods that do not meet quality standards as prescribed;

e) Promotional activities at schools, hospitals, offices of state agencies, political organizations, socio-political organizations, and people's armed forces units;

g) The content of the competition program in the form of sales or service provision that includes entry forms for customers to select winners according to the announced rules and prizes is contrary to Vietnamese historical traditions, culture, ethics, and customs;

h) Conducting promotions using a multi-level marketing model, where the promotional target includes multiple levels and branches, and those at higher levels benefit from the purchases of those at lower levels, without a certificate of registration for multi-level marketing activities.

c) Displaying or showcasing goods without labels or with labels that do not comply with regulations;

d) Displaying or introducing goods that do not meet the published and applicable technical standards and regulations; goods that do not meet quality and food safety standards; or goods that have expired.

2. A fine of between VND 20.000.000 and VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) A representative office of a trader directly displays and introduces the goods and services of the trader it represents at locations other than the representative office's headquarters;

b) A representative office of a business entity displays or introduces the goods and services of the business entity it represents without authorization from that business entity.

3. A fine of between VND 30.000.000 and VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Displaying and introducing goods and services that are on the list of prohibited goods and services, goods that are not or have not yet been permitted to circulate, and services that are not yet permitted to be provided in Vietnam;

b) Consumption in Vietnam of goods temporarily imported for display or presentation purposes that do not comply with Vietnamese law;

c) Displaying or introducing goods or services, or using forms or means of displaying or introducing goods or services that harm national security, public order and safety, landscape, environment, or human health;

d) Displaying or introducing goods or services, or using forms or means of display or introduction that are contrary to the historical traditions, culture, ethics, and customs of Vietnam;

(d) Displaying or introducing goods or services that reveal state secrets.

4. The act of displaying or introducing goods that are not permitted to be imported into Vietnam, or goods on the list of prohibited imports, shall be punished according to the provisions of Article 15 of this Decree.

5. Additional sanctioning forms:

Confiscation of evidence related to violations of the provisions of Clause 3 of this Article.

6. Remedies:

The offender shall be compelled to return any illegal profits obtained from committing the violation specified in point b, clause 3 of this Article.

Article 35. Violations concerning trade fairs and exhibitions

1. A fine of VND 1.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of making false or inaccurate declarations in the application dossier when registering to organize trade fairs and exhibitions;

2. A fine of between VND 5.000.000 and VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Failure to post the theme and schedule of the trade fair or exhibition at the venue before the opening day of the trade fair or exhibition;

b) Changing or supplementing the registration content for organizing trade fairs and exhibitions without registering with the competent state management agency as prescribed or without confirmation from the competent state management agency regarding the changes or supplements to the registration content;

c) Failure to publicly announce the termination of a trade fair or exhibition as required by regulations.

3. A fine of between VND 10.000.000 and VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Displaying counterfeit goods or goods infringing intellectual property rights at trade fairs and exhibitions without clearly stating that the goods are counterfeit or infringing intellectual property rights, or without clearly stating this in the registration for organizing the trade fair or exhibition;

b) Displaying at trade fairs and exhibitions goods and services that are on the list of prohibited goods, prohibited imports, restricted goods, goods not or not yet permitted for circulation, services not yet permitted to be provided in Vietnam, or goods that do not meet quality and food safety standards as prescribed, or goods that have expired;

c) Displaying goods at trade fairs and exhibitions (including goods temporarily imported for display and introduction at trade fairs and exhibitions) without labels or with labels that do not comply with the law on product labeling;

d) Failure to submit a written report or submitting an inaccurate report on the results of a trade fair or exhibition after its conclusion.

4. A fine of between VND 20.000.000 and VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Foreign traders directly organize trade fairs and exhibitions in Vietnam;

b) A representative office of a trader directly organizing or participating in trade fairs or exhibitions, or participating in trade fairs or exhibitions on behalf of the represented trader without authorization from that trader;

c) Organizing for other businesses to participate in trade fairs and exhibitions abroad without registering for trade fair and exhibition services as required by regulations;

d) Organizing trade fairs and exhibitions without registering as required or without written confirmation from the competent state management agency regarding the registration of the trade fair or exhibition;

d) Registering to organize a trade fair or exhibition but failing to organize it, or organizing it incorrectly according to the approved content, without registering amendments or supplements as required by regulations with the competent state management agency and without obtaining the approval of the competent state management agency;

e) Selling, giving away, or supplying at trade fairs and exhibitions goods subject to specialized management without complying with the regulations on specialized management for those goods and services;

g) Awarding, presenting, granting prizes or quality certificates, or titles to goods, services, or to traders, organizations, or individuals within the framework of trade fairs and exhibitions in violation of regulations;

h) Failing to handle complaints and feedback from consumers or organizations/individuals regarding trade fairs and exhibitions; and regarding goods displayed at trade fairs and exhibitions as prescribed;

i) Organizing trade fairs and exhibitions abroad under the name of the State of Vietnam, or organizing trade fairs and exhibitions in Vietnam under the name of a province or city, without meeting the prescribed standards.

5. A fine of between VND 30.000.000 and VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Organizing or directly bringing goods and services that are prohibited from export to participate in trade fairs and exhibitions abroad without the approval of the Prime Minister regarding bringing prohibited goods and services to trade fairs and exhibitions abroad;

b) Selling or giving away goods that are prohibited from export but have been temporarily exported to participate in trade fairs or exhibitions abroad without the Prime Minister's approval for the sale or gifting of those goods;

c) Selling or giving away goods that are subject to export permits from competent state agencies without written approval from those agencies for the sale or gifting of such goods;

d) Organizing trade fairs and exhibitions in Vietnam that display or introduce prohibited goods, prohibited services, goods and services restricted from business according to the law, or goods and services supplied by foreign traders that are on the list of prohibited imports according to regulations, or counterfeit goods, goods infringing intellectual property rights, except in cases where the display or introduction is for comparison with genuine goods;

d) Organizing trade fairs and exhibitions where the quality and reputation of the goods and services participating in the fair, or the reputation and prestige of the businesses, organizations, or individuals participating in the trade fair or exhibition, are not consistent with the name and theme of the trade fair or exhibition;

e) Selling, giving away, or supplying imported goods at trade fairs and exhibitions without customs registration, or selling or giving away goods requiring import permits at trade fairs and exhibitions without written approval from the competent state authority.

6. Additional sanctioning forms:

Confiscation of evidence related to violations of the provisions in points a and b of Clause 3 of this Article.

7. Remedies:

a) Mandatory re-export of goods for violations of the regulations specified in point a, clause 4 and point e, clause 5 of this Article;

b) To compel the return of any illegal profits obtained from committing acts that violate the provisions of this Article.

Section 8. VIOLATIONS REGARDING EXPORT AND IMPORT OF GOODS AND SERVICES RELATED TO EXPORT AND IMPORT OF GOODS

Article 36. Violations concerning goods prohibited from export and import.

1. For the act of exporting or importing goods on the list of prohibited exports or imports, the fines are as follows:

a) A fine of VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the value of the infringing goods is less than VND 20.000.000;

b) A fine of VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the value of the infringing goods is from VND 20.000.000 to less than VND 50.000.000;

c) A fine of VND 30.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the value of the infringing goods is from VND 50.000.000 to less than VND 70.000.000;

d) A fine of VND 50.000.000 to VND 70.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the value of the infringing goods is from VND 70.000.000 to less than VND 100.000.000;

d) A fine of VND 70.000.000 to VND 100.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the value of the infringing goods is VND 100.000.000 or more, but no criminal liability is pursued.

2. Additional sanctioning forms:

Confiscation of evidence related to violations of the provisions of this Article, except in cases where remedial measures as prescribed in point a or point b of paragraph 3 of this Article are applied.

3. Remedies:

a) Mandatory destruction of confiscated items that are harmful to human health, livestock, plants, and the environment for violations of the regulations in this Article, except in cases where remedial measures as prescribed in point b of this clause are applied;

b) To compel the removal from the territory of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam or re-export of infringing goods for violations of the provisions of this Article;

c) To compel the return of any illegal profits obtained from committing acts that violate the provisions of this Article.

Article 37. Violations concerning goods temporarily suspended from export and import.

1. For the act of exporting or importing goods on the list of goods temporarily suspended from export or import without written permission from the competent state management agency, the fines are as follows:

a) A fine of VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the value of the infringing goods is less than VND 20.000.000;

b) A fine of VND 10.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the value of the infringing goods is from VND 20.000.000 to less than VND 50.000.000;

c) A fine of VND 30.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the value of the infringing goods is from VND 50.000.000 to less than VND 70.000.000;

d) A fine of VND 50.000.000 to VND 70.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the value of the infringing goods is from VND 70.000.000 to less than VND 100.000.000;

d) A fine of VND 70.000.000 to VND 80.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the value of the infringing goods is VND 100.000.000 or more, provided that no criminal liability is pursued.

2. Additional sanctioning forms:

The evidence seized for violations of the provisions of this Article shall be confiscated, except in cases where remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 3 of this Article are applied.

3. Remedies:

The infringing goods shall be removed from the territory of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam or re-exported in violation of the provisions of this Article.

Article 38. Violations concerning quotas, export licenses, and import licenses for goods.

1. A fine of VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of arbitrarily erasing, altering, supplementing, or changing the content of quotas, export licenses, and import licenses for goods.

2. A fine of VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for the illegal use of quotas, export licenses, and import licenses for goods.

3. For the act of exporting or importing goods that are required to have quotas, export licenses, or import licenses but are not subject to such quotas, export licenses, or import licenses, the fines are as follows:

a) A fine of VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the value of the infringing goods is less than VND 20.000.000;

b) A fine of VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the value of the infringing goods is from VND 20.000.000 to less than VND 50.000.000;

c) A fine of VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the value of the infringing goods is from VND 50.000.000 to less than VND 70.000.000;

d) A fine of VND 30.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the value of the infringing goods is from VND 70.000.000 to less than VND 100.000.000;

d) A fine of VND 50.000.000 to VND 70.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the value of the infringing goods is VND 100.000.000 or more, but no criminal liability is pursued.

4. Additional sanctioning forms:

a) Confiscation of infringing items for violations of the provisions in Clauses 1 and 3 of this Article;

b) Revoking the right to use quotas, export licenses, and import licenses for a period of 03 to 06 months for violations of the regulations specified in Clause 2 of this Article in cases of multiple violations or repeated offenses.

Article 39. Violations concerning the entrustment of export and import of goods.

1. A fine of VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of entrusting or receiving entrustment for the export or import of goods included in the list of goods subject to conditional temporary import for re-export or temporary export for re-import, but failing to meet the prescribed business conditions.

2. A fine of VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of entrusting or receiving entrustment for the export or import of goods included in the list of goods subject to export or import quotas or export/import licenses, where the entrusting party or the entrusted party does not have a quota or license from the competent state management agency as prescribed.

3. A fine of VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of entrusting or receiving entrustment for the export or import of goods included in the list of goods temporarily suspended from export or import.

4. A fine of VND 40.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of entrusting or receiving entrustment for the export or import of goods included in the list of prohibited export or import goods.

Article 40. Violations concerning temporary import for re-export and temporary export for re-import of goods.

1. A fine of VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of arbitrarily erasing, altering, modifying, supplementing, or changing the content of temporary import/re-export permits or temporary export/re-import permits for goods on the list of prohibited exports, prohibited imports, temporarily suspended exports, or temporarily suspended imports.

2. A fine of VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for the illegal use of temporary import/re-export permits or temporary export/re-import permits for goods on the list of prohibited exports, prohibited imports, temporarily suspended exports, or temporarily suspended imports.

3. A fine of VND 20.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of temporarily importing and re-exporting goods on the list of goods subject to conditional temporary import and re-export without meeting the prescribed business conditions.

4. A fine of VND 40.000.000 to VND 80.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of temporarily importing and re-exporting, or temporarily exporting and re-importing, goods that require a license from a competent state management agency without the required license.

5. A fine of VND 80.000.000 to VND 100.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of temporarily importing and re-exporting, or temporarily exporting and re-importing goods that are on the list of goods prohibited from temporary import and re-export and temporarily suspended from temporary import and re-export.

6. Additional sanctioning forms:

a) Confiscation of evidence related to violations of the provisions of this Article, except in cases where remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 7 of this Article are applied;

b) Suspend temporary import-re-export and temporary export-re-import business activities for a period of 3 to 03 months for violations of the regulations specified in clauses 06, 4, and 5 of this Article;

c) Revoking the license for temporary import/re-export or temporary export/re-import of goods for a period of 03 to 06 months for violations of the regulations specified in Clause 2 of this Article.

7. Remedies:

a) Mandatory re-export of goods at the import customs point for violations of the provisions in Clauses 3 and 4 of this Article;

b) Forcing the removal from the territory of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam or re-export of goods that violate the provisions of Clause 5 of this Article.

Article 41. Violations concerning the transshipment of goods

1. A fine of VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of arbitrarily erasing, altering, modifying, supplementing, or changing the content of a goods import license.

2. A fine of VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for the illegal use of a goods re-export license.

3. A fine of VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for any of the following acts: re-exporting goods that do not conform to the specified type or quantity as stipulated in the license issued by the competent state management agency.

4. A fine of between VND 30.000.000 and VND 50.000.000 for one of the following acts:

a) The re-export of goods, which is required by regulations to have a license from the competent state management agency, was carried out without a license;

b) Engaging in re-export business involving goods on the list of goods temporarily suspended from re-export.

5. A fine of VND 50.000.000 to VND 80.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of re-exporting goods included in the list of prohibited goods for re-export.

6. Additional sanctioning forms:

a) Confiscation of evidence related to violations of the provisions of this Article, except in cases where remedial measures as prescribed in Clause 7 of this Article are applied;

b) Revoking the license to import goods for a period of 01 to 3 months for violations of the regulations specified in Clause 03 of this Article in cases of multiple violations or repeat offenses;

c) Suspending the import/export business for a period of 03 to 06 months for violations of the regulations specified in clauses 4 and 5 of this Article.

7. Remedies:

Goods that violate the provisions of Clauses 4 and 5 of this Article shall be subject to mandatory removal from the territory of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam or re-export.

Article 42. Violations concerning goods in transit

1. A fine of VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of transiting goods through routes other than those permitted, except in cases specified in point a, clause 2 of this Article.

2. A fine of between VND 20.000.000 and VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Goods requiring a transit permit are transiting through routes and border crossings that are not permitted for transit;

b) Goods in transit remain in Vietnamese territory beyond the permitted time limit.

3. A fine of VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of transiting goods that, according to regulations, require a license from a competent state management agency without having the necessary license.

4. A fine of VND 40.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for the illegal consumption of goods and vehicles in transit within the territory of Vietnam.

5. Additional sanctioning forms:

Confiscation of evidence related to violations of the provisions of Clause 4 of this Article.

6. Remedies:

a) To compel the removal from the territory of the Socialist Republic of Vietnam or re-export of goods that violate the provisions of Clauses 1, 2 and 3 of this Article;

b) To compel the return of an amount equal to the value of the goods and means of transit that were illegally consumed, in violation of the provisions of Clause 4 of this Article.

Article 43. Violations concerning the operation of duty-free shops.

1. A fine of VND 2.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 will be imposed for selling duty-free goods in excess of the prescribed quantity.

2. A fine of VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of selling duty-free goods to ineligible recipients.

3. For the act of trading goods without the required "Vietnam duty not paid" stamp, or selling duty-free goods such as cigars, cigarettes manufactured abroad, or other goods subject to conditional export or import without the required export or import license, the fines are as follows:

a) A fine of VND 1.000.000 to VND 3.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the value of the infringing goods is less than VND 5.000.000;

b) A fine of VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the value of the infringing goods is from VND 5.000.000 to less than VND 10.000.000;

c) A fine of VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the value of the infringing goods is from VND 10.000.000 to less than VND 20.000.000;

d) A fine of VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the value of the infringing goods is between VND 20.000.000 and less than VND 30.000.000;

d) A fine of VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the value of the infringing goods is between VND 30.000.000 and less than VND 50.000.000;

e) A fine of VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed in cases where the value of the infringing goods is VND 50.000.000 or more.

4. A fine of between VND 40.000.000 and VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Trading in goods that are not legally imported at duty-free shops;

b) Illegally consuming goods permitted for import and sale in duty-free shops on the domestic market.

5. A fine of VND 50.000.000 to VND 70.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of conducting business at duty-free shops involving goods prohibited from export, import, or temporarily suspended from export or import as prescribed by regulations.

6. Additional sanctioning forms:

a) Confiscation of evidence related to violations of the provisions in clauses 3, 4, and 5 of this Article;

b) Revocation of the duty-free shop business license for a period of 01 to 3 months for violations of the regulations specified in clauses 2, 03, 4, and 5 of this Article in cases of multiple violations or repeat offenses.

7. Remedies:

The offender shall be required to return any illegal profits obtained from committing acts that violate the provisions of this Article.

Article 44. Violations concerning the origin of exported and imported goods.

1. A fine of VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of erasing, altering, or falsifying the content of a Certificate of Origin, a self-certification document of origin, or a self-certification approval document issued by a competent authority.

2. A fine of VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of providing false documents or certificates to competent agencies or organizations when requesting the issuance or verification of a Certificate of Origin for goods.

3. A fine of between VND 30.000.000 and VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Falsely certifying the origin of goods after being granted permission to self-certify the origin of goods by a competent state agency;

b) Forging Certificates of Origin or self-certification documents of origin in cases where criminal liability is not incurred;

c) Providing false documents or certificates to competent authorities or organizations when requesting self-certification of origin of goods or verification of self-certified origin documents.

4. A fine of VND 50.000.000 to VND 70.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of using forged Certificates of Origin or self-certification documents of origin in cases where criminal liability is not pursued.

5. Additional sanctioning forms:

Confiscation of evidence related to violations of the provisions of this Article.

6. Remedies:

a) To compel the return of any illegal profits obtained from committing acts that violate the provisions of this Article;

b) To compel the correction of false information regarding the origin of goods for violations of the provisions of this Article.

Article 45. Violations concerning the processing of goods with foreign elements.

1. A fine of VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of placing orders for processing or receiving orders for processing goods from foreign traders without a contract as prescribed.

2. A fine of between VND 20.000.000 and VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Consuming in the Vietnamese market leased or borrowed machinery and equipment, or surplus raw materials, auxiliary materials, supplies, waste products, and scrap temporarily imported for processing and processed goods for foreign traders in violation of regulations;

b) Falsifying processing contracts with foreign traders.

3. A fine of VND 40.000.000 to VND 70.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of placing orders for processing goods or receiving goods for processing from foreign traders for export or import goods that require a license, without obtaining the approval of the competent state management agency as prescribed.

4. A fine of between VND 70.000.000 and VND 100.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Accepting goods for processing for foreign traders that are on the list of goods prohibited from export, prohibited from import, or temporarily suspended from export or import without written approval from the competent state management agency as prescribed;

b) Outsourcing the processing of goods for domestic consumption that are prohibited from export, prohibited from import, temporarily suspended from export, temporarily suspended from import, or subject to emergency measures prohibiting circulation, recalled, temporarily suspended from circulation, or goods that do not meet food safety standards.

5. Additional sanctioning forms:

Confiscation of evidence related to violations of the provisions in Clauses 2, 3, and 4 of this Article.

6. Remedies:

The offender shall be compelled to return any illegal profits obtained from committing the violations specified in point a, clause 2 and point a, clause 4 of this Article.

Section 9. VIOLATIONS OF CONSUMER RIGHTS PROTECTION

Article 46. Violations concerning the protection of consumer information.

1. A fine of between VND 10.000.000 and VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Failing to clearly and publicly inform consumers about the purpose before collecting and using consumer information as required by regulations;

b) Using consumer information for purposes other than those disclosed to the consumer, without the consumer's consent as required by regulations;

c) Failure to ensure the safety, accuracy, and completeness of consumer information when collecting, using, and transferring it as required by regulations;

d) Failing to self-correct or provide measures for consumers to update or correct information when they discover inaccurate information, as required by regulations;

d) Transferring consumer information to a third party without the consumer's consent as required by law, except where otherwise provided by law.

2. Double the fines stipulated in Clause 1 of this Article shall be imposed in cases where the relevant information is classified as personal information of the consumer.

Article 47. Violations concerning the provision of information about goods and services to consumers.

1. A fine of VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed on businesses that commit any of the following violations regarding the provision of information about goods and services to consumers:

a) Failure to warn about the potential adverse effects of goods and services on the health, life, and property of consumers, and the preventive measures required by regulations;

b) Failure to provide information on the availability of spare parts and accessories for goods as required;

c) Failure to provide instructions for use or information regarding the conditions, duration, location, and procedures for warranty service in cases where the goods or services are covered by a warranty as stipulated;

d) Failing to accurately and fully inform consumers about standard contracts and general terms and conditions of transactions before the transaction as required;

d) Concealing information or providing incomplete, misleading, or inaccurate information to consumers as required by regulations.

2. A fine of VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed on third parties who commit any of the following violations regarding the provision of information on goods and services to consumers:

a) Providing incomplete or inaccurate information about goods and services provided as required by regulations;

b) There is no evidence to prove or all legally required measures have not been taken to verify the accuracy and completeness of information about goods and services.

3. A fine of VND 30.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed on media owners and third-party media service providers who commit any of the following violations regarding the provision of information about goods and services to consumers:

a) There are no technical solutions to prevent the means and services under their management from being used to harass consumers;

b) Allowing organizations and individuals engaged in the business of goods and services to use means and services under their management to harass consumers.

4. Additional sanctioning forms:

Revocation of business license or professional certificate for a period of 01 to 06 months, or suspension of operations for a period of 01 to 06 months, for violations of the regulations specified in Clause 3 of this Article in cases of multiple violations or repeat offenses.

5. Remedies:

Mandatory correction of false or misleading information regarding violations of the regulations specified in point d, clause 1 of this Article.

Article 48. Breach of contracts concluded with consumers.

1. A fine of between VND 5.000.000 and VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Signing contracts with consumers using forms and language that do not comply with regulations;

b) Not allowing consumers to review the entire contract before entering into it in cases where the contract is concluded electronically as stipulated.

2. Remedies:

The contract concluded must be amended in accordance with regulations for violations of the provisions specified in point a, clause 1 of this Article.

Article 49. Violations concerning the registration of standard contracts and general terms and conditions of transactions.

1. A fine of VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for failing to comply with the request of a competent state agency to cancel or amend the content of a standard contract or general terms and conditions of transaction that violates the law on consumer protection or is contrary to the general principles of contract formation.

2. A fine of between VND 30.000.000 and VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Failure to register or re-register standard contracts and general terms and conditions of transactions with the competent state management agency for consumer protection as prescribed;

b) Failing to notify consumers of changes to standard contracts or general terms and conditions of transactions as required by regulations;

c) Failure to properly apply the standard contract form and general terms and conditions of transaction registered with the competent state management agency for consumer protection as prescribed.

3. The fine shall be doubled for violations of the provisions in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article if the violation is committed in two or more provinces or centrally-governed cities.

Article 50. Violations concerning the form of standard contracts and general terms and conditions of transactions.

1. A fine of VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of using standard contracts or general terms and conditions in transactions with consumers that contain one of the following violations:

a) The font size is smaller than prescribed;

b) The language of the contract is not Vietnamese, unless the parties agree otherwise or the law provides otherwise;

c) The paper background and ink color used to display the content of the standard contract and general terms and conditions of the transaction do not contrast with each other.

2. Remedies:

The standard contract and general terms and conditions of transaction shall be amended to comply with regulations for violations of the provisions of Clause 1 of this Article.

Article 51. Violations concerning the performance of standard contracts and general terms and conditions of transactions.

1. A fine of between VND 10.000.000 and VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Not retaining the standard contract form that has been concluded until the contract expires as stipulated;

b) A copy of the contract will not be provided to the consumer if the contract held by the consumer is lost or damaged as stipulated.

2. A fine of between VND 20.000.000 and VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Failure to publicly announce the general terms and conditions of transactions before engaging in transactions with consumers as required by regulations;

b) The general terms and conditions of transaction do not specify the time of application or are not posted in a convenient location at the transaction site where consumers can see them as required by regulations.

Article 52. Violations concerning the conclusion of contracts and general terms and conditions of transactions with consumers.

1. A fine of VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed on traders engaged in the business of goods and services who enter into contracts with consumers containing clauses that are invalid according to regulations.

2. A fine of VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for violations of the provisions of Clause 1 of this Article in the case of standard contracts or general terms and conditions of transactions.

3. The fine shall be doubled the amount stipulated in Clauses 1 and 2 of this Article if the violation is committed in the territory of two or more provinces or centrally-governed cities.

4. Remedies:

Article 53. Violations concerning contracts concluded remotely

1. A fine of VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of entering into a remote contract with consumers in one of the following cases:

a) Failure to provide complete and clear information as required;

b) No refund will be given within 30 days from the date the consumer unilaterally terminates the concluded contract, or no interest will be paid on overdue payments to the consumer as stipulated;

c) Restricting or hindering consumers from unilaterally terminating a concluded contract within ten days from the date of contract conclusion in cases where the business organization or individual provides incorrect or incomplete information as required;

d) Forcing or requiring the consumer to pay costs in order to terminate a concluded contract, except for costs related to the goods or services already consumed by the consumer.

2. Remedies:

The offender shall be compelled to return any illegal profits obtained from committing the violations specified in points b and d of Clause 1 of this Article.

Article 54. Violations concerning the provision of continuous service.

c) Requiring consumers to pay in advance before the service is provided, unless otherwise agreed upon by the parties;

d) Failing to notify consumers at least 03 working days in advance of the service suspension date in case of repair, maintenance or other reasons as stipulated, except in cases of force majeure or as otherwise provided by law;

d) Failure to promptly inspect and resolve issues reported by consumers regarding service quality as required by regulations;

e) Unilaterally terminating the contract or ceasing to provide services without justifiable reason;

g) Refusing or obstructing consumers from terminating a service contract as stipulated;

h) Forcing consumers to pay for unused portions of the service.

2. Remedies:

The offender shall be compelled to return any illegal profits obtained from committing the violation specified in point h, clause 1 of this Article.

Article 55. Violations of door-to-door sales contracts

1. A fine of VND 10.000.000 to VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed on door-to-door vendors who commit any of the following violations:

d) To compel consumers to pay or fulfill other contractual obligations before the expiration of 03 working days from the date of signing the contract, except where otherwise provided by law;

(d) Disclaimer of liability for the actions of door-to-door salespeople in cases where they cause harm to consumers.

e) Failing to adequately and accurately explain to consumers the terms of the contract and information related to the goods and services to be transacted with them;

g) Door-to-door sales contracts are not required to be in writing and a copy given to the consumer, unless the parties agree otherwise.

The offender shall be compelled to return any illegal profits obtained from committing the violation specified in point d, clause 1 of this Article.

d) Failure to provide information on business operations or to explain the operation of the e-commerce website or e-commerce application when requested by competent state agencies for the purpose of e-commerce statistics, inspection, examination, and handling of violations in e-commerce as prescribed.

b) Acquiring a website providing e-commerce services or an e-commerce service application without completing the transfer procedures or without re-registering with the competent state management agency as required;

d) Deceiving or providing false information when registering a website providing e-commerce services or an e-commerce service application;

d) Falsifying registration information on e-commerce service websites or e-commerce service applications;

e) Using a registered logo on a website providing e-commerce services or an e-commerce service application without prior registration confirmation from the competent state authority as prescribed;

The e-commerce business shall be suspended for a period of 06 to 12 months for violations of the regulations specified in Clauses 2, 3, and 4 of this Article in cases of multiple violations or repeat offenses.

Mandatory revocation of the “.vn” domain name of the e-commerce website or mandatory removal of the mobile application from app stores or from the provided addresses for violations of the regulations specified in points b, c, d, e, f, and g of Clause 4 of this Article.

c) Establishing a website providing e-commerce services or an e-commerce service application or a website providing other online services without transparently and fully disclosing information about the contract termination process and procedures as required by law;

d) Establishing an online ordering function on an e-commerce website or mobile application, but the contract conclusion process does not comply with legal regulations.

2. A fine of between VND 5.000.000 and VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Using the link to provide information that contradicts or misleads the information published on the e-commerce website or mobile application containing the link;

b) Interfering with the operating system and internet browser on electronic devices accessing e-commerce websites or mobile applications in order to force customers to stay on the website or install the mobile application against their will.

a) Providing false information about merchants, organizations, or individuals owning e-commerce websites or mobile applications, information about goods, services, prices, shipping, delivery, payment methods, contract terms, and general terms and conditions of transactions on e-commerce websites or mobile applications;

b) Providing information, conducting business, or trading in counterfeit goods, goods or services that infringe intellectual property rights, or goods or services prohibited from being traded on the internet;

e) Failing to comply with the terms of the concluded contract or unilaterally terminating the concluded contract using the online ordering function on the e-commerce website or mobile application as prescribed;

g) Failure to provide information and statistical reports on the provision of payment intermediary services for e-commerce websites to the competent state management agency as required by regulations.

a) Using links, logos, or other technologies to create confusion about any connection with other businesses, organizations, or individuals;

b) Using the logos of e-commerce website or mobile application credibility rating programs without official recognition from these programs;

c) Falsifying the information of other merchants, organizations, or individuals to participate in e-commerce activities;

d) Failure to implement measures to ensure the safety and security of customer payment transactions.

5. A fine of between VND 30.000.000 and VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Falsifying or copying the interface of another business, organization, or individual's e-commerce website or mobile application for profit or to mislead or undermine customer trust in that business, organization, or individual;

b) Stealing, disclosing, transferring, or selling information related to the trade secrets of other businesses or organizations, or the personal information of consumers in e-commerce without the consent of the parties involved.

6. A fine of between VND 40.000.000 and VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Deceiving customers on e-commerce websites or mobile applications;

b) Exploiting the guise of e-commerce business activities to illegally raise capital from other businesses, organizations, or individuals.

7. Additional sanctioning forms:

a) Confiscate the evidence and means used to commit the violations specified in clauses 5 and 6 of this Article;

b) Suspension of e-commerce activities for a period of 6 to 12 months for violations of the regulations specified in clauses 5 and 06 of this Article.

c) Multi-level marketing activities in localities where the business has not been granted registration confirmation for multi-level marketing activities in that locality.

c) Organizing seminars, conferences, or training sessions on multi-level marketing without written authorization from the multi-level marketing company;

d) Enticing, persuading, or bribing participants in multi-level marketing schemes of other businesses to join the network of the business they are currently participating in;

d) Abusing one's position, authority, social status, or profession to encourage, request, entice, or persuade others to participate in multi-level marketing networks or purchase goods sold through multi-level marketing methods.

4. A fine of between VND 20.000.000 and VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Participating in the activities of organizations or individuals conducting multi-level marketing without having been granted a certificate of registration for multi-level marketing activities;

b) Organizing conferences, seminars, training courses, or presentations about multi-level marketing business activities of organizations or individuals conducting multi-level marketing but who have not been granted a certificate of registration for multi-level marketing activities.

5. A fine of VND 30.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed for conducting multi-level marketing business without a certificate of registration for multi-level marketing activities, resulting in illicit profits of less than VND 200.000.000 or causing damage to others of less than VND 500.000.000, except for cases specified in point h, clause 9 of this Article.

6. A fine of VND 30.000.000 to VND 40.000.000 shall be imposed on multi-level marketing businesses that commit one of the following violations:

b) Failure to comply with, or improper compliance with, the notification procedures in case of changes to the list of goods traded through multi-level marketing as prescribed by law;

c) Failure to follow the procedure for requesting the re-issuance of a multi-level marketing business registration certificate in cases where the law stipulates it;

d) Signing a multi-level marketing participation contract with an individual who does not meet the eligibility requirements for participating in multi-level marketing as stipulated by law;

d) Signing a multi-level marketing participation contract that does not include all the basic contents as prescribed by law;

e) Not terminating contracts with multi-level marketing participants when those participants are penalized for prohibited conduct in multi-level marketing activities;

g) Failure to comply with or improper compliance with regulations regarding the compilation of trainer lists, retention of accompanying records, publication of trainer lists on the website, and notification to the Ministry of Industry and Trade;

h) Failing to comply with or improperly complying with regulations regarding updating the list of trainers on the enterprise's website and notifying the Ministry of Industry and Trade of any changes to the list of trainers;

k) Failing to establish or publish the selling prices of goods sold through multi-level marketing or failing to comply with the published selling prices;

l) Failing to supervise the activities of multi-level marketing participants to ensure that they comply with the multi-level marketing participation contract, operating rules, and compensation plan of the enterprise;

m) Failing to comply with, or improperly complying with, regulations regarding the registration of amendments or additions to the content of multi-level marketing activities at the local level with the Department of Industry and Trade in cases prescribed by law;

n) Having notified the Department of Industry and Trade about organizing conferences, seminars, or training courses but failing to carry them out without notifying the Department of Industry and Trade in writing as required;

o) Failure to follow the procedure for amending the deposit confirmation document with the bank when there are changes to the information on the deposit confirmation document;

p) Failure to comply with regulations regarding the deadline for renewing the certificate of registration for multi-level marketing activities;

q) Failure to promptly notify the agency that issued the multi-level marketing registration certificate in case of malfunctions in the information technology system managing the network of multi-level marketing participants;

r) Failing to comply with, or improperly complying with, regulations regarding the storage and presentation of records and documents related to multi-level marketing activities in the locality as required by competent management agencies;

s) Failure to fulfill or improper fulfillment of reporting obligations in multi-level marketing activities as required by law.

a) Organizing multi-level marketing activities in a province or centrally-administered city without having received written confirmation of registration for multi-level marketing activities from the Department of Industry and Trade of that province or centrally-administered city;

b) Failure to maintain a local representative as required in cases where there is no head office, branch, or representative office in the locality;

c) Failing to comply with or improperly complying with regulations regarding notification to the Department of Industry and Trade when organizing conferences, seminars, or training sessions on multi-level marketing with the participation of 30 or more people, or with the participation of 10 or more multi-level marketing participants in the locality where the business has been granted registration for multi-level marketing activities;

d) Failing to cooperate with relevant authorities during the process of monitoring, inspecting, and supervising conferences, seminars, and training sessions on multi-level marketing conducted by businesses;

d) Paying participants in multi-level marketing schemes a total value of commissions, bonuses, and other economic benefits, including benefits received under promotional programs, exceeding 40% of the multi-level marketing sales revenue of the business in that year;

e) Not paying commissions, bonuses, promotions, and other economic benefits to participants in multi-level marketing schemes in cash via bank transfer;

g) Failure to sign a written multi-level marketing participation contract with a multi-level marketing participant, or a multi-level marketing participation contract that does not meet other formal requirements as prescribed by law;

h) Failing to fulfill or improperly fulfilling the obligation to pay participants in multi-level marketing schemes commissions, bonuses, and other economic benefits that the participants are entitled to receive during their participation in the multi-level marketing network of the enterprise after the termination of the contract;

i) Failing to provide or improperly providing basic training to participants in multi-level marketing as required by law, or charging basic training fees to participants in multi-level marketing;

k) Failing to issue or improperly issuing membership cards to participants in multi-level marketing schemes as required by law, or collecting membership card fees;

l) Appointing trainers who do not meet the qualifications to conduct basic training for participants in multi-level marketing;

b) Conducting promotional activities using a multi-level, multi-branch network where participants in the promotional program have more than one position, code, or other equivalent forms;

c) Allowing participants in multi-level marketing to receive money or other economic benefits from referring others to participate in multi-level marketing activities, rather than from the purchase or sale of goods by those referred;

4. A fine of VND 10.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed on members of commodity exchanges who commit any of the following violations:

a) Enticing customers to sign contracts by promising full or partial compensation for any damages incurred or guaranteeing a portion of the profits for the customer;

b) Using artificial pricing and other fraudulent methods when brokering for clients;

c) Engaging in brokerage activities for buying and selling commodities through a commodity exchange without being a trading member or brokerage member of the commodity exchange.

5. A fine of VND 30.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed on members of commodity exchanges who commit any of the following violations:

a) Failure to make or improper making of transaction deposits as required;

b) Failure to comply with regulations regarding total transaction limits or transaction limits;

c) Enticing customers to sign contracts by promising full or partial compensation for any damages incurred or guaranteeing a portion of the profits for the customer.

6. A fine of VND 5.000.000 to VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed on commodity exchanges that commit any of the following violations:

a) Inaccurate or untimely declaration of information in the application for the issuance, re-issuance, amendment, or supplementation of a license to establish a commodity exchange;

b) Refusing to approve membership in a commodity exchange without providing a written response or without stating the reasons for the refusal.

7. A fine of VND 10.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed on commodity exchanges that commit any of the following violations:

a) The specific transaction times were not disclosed as required by regulations;

b) Failure to publish the operating regulations and establishment license of the commodity exchange that have been approved, issued, amended, or supplemented by the competent state management agency;

c) The procedure for requesting a reissue of the commodity exchange establishment license will not be carried out in cases where the commodity exchange establishment license is lost, torn, burned, or destroyed in any other way;

d) Failure to follow the procedure for requesting amendments or additions to the license for establishing a commodity exchange in cases where there are changes to the contents of the license for establishing a commodity exchange;

d) Failure to disclose, or untimely disclosure, of cases of trading suspension as prescribed;

e) Failure to publish, incomplete publication, or inaccurate publication of the list and information of members of the commodity exchange; information on transactions and trading orders for buying and selling commodities through the commodity exchange; and other information as required by the commodity exchange's operating regulations;

g) Failure to comply with, or incorrect or incomplete compliance with, regulations on periodic or ad hoc reporting as required by competent state management agencies regarding information related to the trading of goods through commodity exchanges and their members at the time of reporting;

h) Spreading false information about transactions, markets, or prices of commodities traded through commodity exchanges.

8. A fine of VND 30.000.000 to VND 50.000.000 shall be imposed on commodity exchanges that commit any of the following violations:

a) Using forged documents in applications for the establishment, amendment, supplementation, or re-issuance of licenses for the establishment of commodity exchanges;

b) Granting membership to businesses that do not meet the eligibility requirements;

c) Failure to make or improper making of transaction deposits as required;

d) Failure to establish internal control systems, risk management and monitoring, and prevent conflicts of interest within the organization and in transactions as required by regulations;

d) Allowing members whose membership has been terminated to continue trading goods through the commodity exchange;

e) Organizing trading activities involving goods not on the list of prohibited, restricted, or conditionally traded goods without notifying the state management agency as required;

g) Failure to comply with regulations regarding total transaction limits or transaction limits;

h) Failure to properly implement trading methods or order matching principles, or to disclose trading information as required;

i) Organizing trading activities involving goods on the list of restricted or conditionally traded goods without permission from the competent state management agency.

9. Remedies:

The offender shall be required to return any illegal profits obtained from committing acts that violate the provisions of this Article.

Article 78. Violations of regulations on market management, supermarket and shopping mall business.

1. A fine of between VND 5.000.000 and VND 10.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) Naming a business establishment as a supermarket, shopping mall, or equivalent terms in a foreign language without ensuring compliance with prescribed standards;

b) The operating regulations of supermarkets, shopping malls, and markets do not fully reflect the required content or have not been approved by the competent state management agency;

c) Failure to display market regulations, supermarket operating regulations, or shopping mall operating regulations as required;

d) Failing to implement or incorrectly implementing the instructions of competent authorities regarding the organization of economic information activities and the dissemination of policies and legal regulations;

d) Failure to submit periodic or ad hoc reports on the operational status of markets, supermarkets, and shopping centers as required by competent state management agencies.

2. A fine of between VND 10.000.000 and VND 20.000.000 shall be imposed on one of the following acts of violation:

a) There is no supermarket or shopping mall sign as required by regulations;

b) Displaying supermarket or shopping mall signs that do not comply with regulations regarding content and format;

c) Operating supermarkets or shopping malls that are not registered businesses engaged in commercial activities as prescribed by law;

d) Goods sold in supermarkets and shopping malls do not have the names of the goods or services and the name of the supermarket or shopping mall as required by regulations;

d) Goods sold in supermarkets and shopping malls with warranties that do not clearly state the warranty period and location as required by regulations;

A fine of VND 20.000.000 to VND 30.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of trading in markets, supermarkets, and shopping centers goods containing radioactive substances or devices emitting ionizing radiation exceeding the permitted levels; explosives, flammable liquids and gases; veterinary drugs, pesticides, goods containing toxic chemicals on the restricted list and toxic chemicals on the conditional list that are not permitted to be traded in markets, supermarkets, and shopping centers as prescribed.

Article 79. Violations of regulations on sealing and temporarily detaining evidence and means of violation.

1. A fine of VND 3.000.000 to VND 5.000.000 shall be imposed for the act of arbitrarily removing or breaking the seals on seized evidence and vehicles involved in violations, or arbitrarily altering the scene of an administrative violation.

Chapter IV

TERMS ENFORCEMENT

Article 89. Enforcement

1. This Decree takes effect from March 15, 10.

2. This Decree replaces:

a) Government Decree No. 185/2013/ND-CP dated November 15, 2013, stipulating administrative penalties for violations in commercial activities, production, trading of counterfeit and prohibited goods, and protection of consumer rights;
